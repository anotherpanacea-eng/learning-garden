# Source audit: spec 137, Mills, Bridges and Pilgrims

Date: 2026-10-25. Writer: Claude (writing only). Scope: every real-history claim in [spec 137](../specs/137-mills-bridges-pilgrims.md) and [its companion text](../content/137-mills-bridges-pilgrims-text.md).

**Method and limits.** Claims were checked with web search on 2026-10-25. Direct page fetches failed in this session (DNS errors for wikipedia.org, britannica.com, nationalarchives.gov.uk and millsarchive.org), so the supports below rest on search-result summaries of the named pages, not on full-page reading. The shared web-search budget ran out before the last group of general claims (period label, castles, markets and guilds, peasant obligations, food) could be searched; those were left for the second pass below. A subject reviewer should open each URL before build.

**Second pass (2026-10-25, 22 further searches).** A fresh search budget covered every item left open by the first pass. Each was confirmed (marker removed), corrected in the text, or reworded to what the sources support. Page fetches were still blocked, so these supports also rest on search-result excerpts. Several are secondary (World History Encyclopedia, History Learning Site, popular-history blogs); they are marked *secondary* below.

6. The *Piers Plowman*, Codex and Ely wordings are paraphrased from translations; no quotation is used.

## Rights notes for images

No images are embedded. Recommended, subject to a rights check at build time:

- **Pilgrim scallop badges:** London Museum or British Museum collection images, only where the object page carries an explicit open license (for example British Museum 1856,0701.2106; the museum's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms are non-commercial, so confirm fit for this project before use). Otherwise an original modern illustration, labeled as such.
- **Puente la Reina:** a Wikimedia Commons photograph with a CC BY or CC BY-SA license and attribution, or an original illustration.
- **Domesday Book page:** The National Archives' images carry their own terms; prefer an original illustration of "a page of lists," labeled as a drawing, with a link to the real record.
- **Labours of the Months:** public-domain manuscript images from a library open-access program (for example, the British Library's or the Met's public-domain collections), with the exact object record cited.
- **Willowbank** art (the shell, the mill, the survey picture, the ribbons) is original fiction art.

Do not colorize, "restore" or invent detail in any historical image; do not present a modern drawing as a primary source.

## Review pass (independent review, 2026-10-25)

- **Multure range:** one search found no source giving English multure rates, so 1/30 to 1/10 still rests on the Mills Archive summary (open check 5 stands).
- **Codex Calixtinus, Book V:** card 2.3's claim that the guide names "a lower valley way" around the Cize pass was not supported in the audit and is cut; the card now says only that the guide names the pass of Cize.
- **Guidebook age:** about 1140 is about 885 years ago, so "nine hundred years old" became "almost nine hundred years old".
- Child text no longer carries a raw `[estimate]` tag on card 2.9; the card says "Both are estimates."
- Per-choice feedback examples pointed at the wrong items (A4, B6, D11, C6); corrected to A3, B5, D9 and C5.
- Scene 1: the miller's "Older than the mill" (illogical for a shell hidden in the mill wall) became "Old."
- US spelling throughout the spec and companion (plow, labor, travelers, centers, criticized, catalog, totaled), except the proper names "Labours of the Months" and the pinned topic "Knights & Armour".
- Reviewer script re-checked every numeric answer and wrong choice, and the hard-prerequisite crosswalk against the pinned files; all correct.
