The broad `operations-support` asks a 17−8 take-away question. The current generated broad subtraction parameters keep starts 11–18 and removals 2–9, so 23−11 is outside that set. No local activity found in those inspected surfaces asks the learner to *pair two conserved collections* and then reinterpret the same arithmetic as an actual return. The proposed 23/11/12 pair, art, wording and model are original. It narrows the missing M1 comparison-versus-take-away contrast; it does not teach subtraction in every unknown position, regrouping, fluency or an oral explanation.
