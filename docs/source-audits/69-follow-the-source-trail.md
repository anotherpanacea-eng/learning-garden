## Missing lesson and bounded content constraints

Existing Archive already asks whether production date proves capture time, which source supports a question, what a photograph shows and what it cannot establish. Two Windows compares differently situated **original fictional** accounts and revises a supplied reader message; it is not authenticated historical corroboration.
