# Tenths Table: source and overlap audit

Prepared 2026-10-22 for [spec 81](../specs/81-tenths-table.md). This records a bounded design reference, not source incorporation, an efficacy finding, independent clearance or child placement. No runtime code or assessment item is borrowed.

## Primary learning references checked

- [Common Core, 3.NF.A.1–2](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/NF/) defines a fraction through equal parts of a specified whole and develops the corresponding magnitude on a number line. This supports keeping the whole and unit part visible. The table uses an area picture; number-line construction is deferred, and the Grade 3 denominator list does not itself specify tenths.
- [Common Core, 4.NF.C.6–7](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/4/NF/) connects denominator-ten/hundred fractions with decimal notation and requires a shared whole when comparing quantities. The table practices only the tenths part of the notation bridge, zero through one; hundredths, decimal comparison and full standard coverage are not claimed. The standards do not validate the proposed game or establish an individual learner's readiness.
- [England Department for Education, mathematics programme](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study), Year 3 fractions and Year 4 decimal notes: equal tenths connect to place value; counting in both directions and multiple representations are encouraged. This informs reversible selection and a stable whole. The programme's ages/year groups are curriculum conventions, not app profile fields or gates.

The resulting teaching contrast is our design judgment: ten whole counters at Bundle Exchange total ten, while ten parts each worth one tenth of the fixed strip total one. A decimal point is not a movable object, and decimal notation does not mean counting unrelated objects. The proposed activity does not teach division by ten or percentage reasoning through this contrast alone.

## Existing app patterns and limits

Sharing Counter provides the stable-whole/equal-parts contrast and reversible interaction/Keep patterns. Fraction Trail uses a 12-tick whole and cannot simply accept denominator 10 without changing its model. Bundle Exchange and Joining Supply Orders provide scoped support, frozen source snapshots, kept work and bounded recovery/report preflight patterns. These inform a small separate ten-part model; copying their counters or entire strict validators would misstate the unit or add unnecessary machinery.

All request, helper and feedback wording in spec 81 is original. Any future assessed decimal task or cross-game link must explicitly qualify the equivalent names already supplied here. No current response is reclassified as independent decimal knowledge. Independent specification/content review and implementation verification remain required.
