## How the checking was done

Direct page fetching was not available in this session: the egress proxy refused connections to the source hosts, including britannica.com, whc.unesco.org and nasa.gov. So each claim below rests on the search-returned text of the cited page, not a full read. The session's search budget ran out before two claims could be checked (listed under Open checks). A subject reviewer should open the primary pages before build. Britannica could not be opened at all; where a Britannica page surfaced in results, it is listed as corroboration only.

Two facts come from already-audited packs in this repo and were not re-searched: the Egyptian writing board date ([spec 95](../specs/95-egypt-writing-book.md), [its audit](95-egypt-writing-book.md)) and the Paris 1910 peak date ([spec 46](../specs/46-travelers-archive-first-visit.md)).

## Images and rights

This spec embeds no images. If cards get pictures, prefer: The Met Open Access (CC0) objects such as the writing board already used in spec 95; NASA Apollo 11 photographs (generally not copyrighted when created by NASA; check each item's page); Library of Congress Gutenberg Bible images where marked as having no known restrictions; the CC0 Paris Musées photograph already cleared in spec 46. Otherwise use original modern illustrations, labelled as such. Do not use reconstructions as if they were ancient objects. Dinosaur pictures are reconstructions and must be labelled "an artist's picture".
