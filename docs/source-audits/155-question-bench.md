## What is fiction, what is a model, what is real

- **A story from Willowbank (fiction):** the Wonder Board, the slips, the café week, Puff's notebook, Hob's lettuces, the night garden, the spinners, the scales and every name.
- **A made-up model:** spinner fall times (2.4, 2.6, 2.5; 1.8, 2.0, 2.2; Bramble's 2.5, 2.1, 2.7 and 2.4, 2.6, 2.2; odd reading 4.1); scale readings (512, 511, 513; 494, 506, 500 g against a 500 g weight); café temperatures, ice creams and hats; ice melting times of about 9 and 20 minutes. None of these are measurements. Only the direction of the ice result is claimed as real.
- **A true thing from science:** the continental drift history, the ice-on-metal direction and explanation, and the precision/accuracy definitions.
