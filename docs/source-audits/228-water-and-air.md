## Open checks

1. **Antarctica as "largest desert" `[check]`:** still only secondary pages; kept out of child text. "Antarctica is a cold desert" is now confirmed (NASA SVS, review).
2. **Winds and the Gulf Stream `[check]`:** the Met Office article credits the current only; the Marble record asks that winds and latitude also be named. Child text says the current "with the winds that blow over it" helps; a subject reviewer should confirm the wording.
3. **Fossil-fuel origins `[check]`:** coal from buried plants, oil and gas from tiny sea organisms, over millions of years. The review found agreeing educational pages (Smithsonian Ocean oil interactive, North Dakota Studies) but still no EIA or USGS page. Confirm against one before build.
4. **Paper thickness:** "two sheets of paper" assumes about 0.1 mm per sheet (a 500-sheet ream about 5 cm); computed, not sourced.
5. **Qanat age `[check]`:** estimates differ (about 800 BCE to about 3,000 years; the Ghasabeh component is often called 2,500 years old); child text says "very old" only. The writer's Mozd Abad date was unsourced and is removed.
6. **Humboldt's height on Chimborazo:** sources differ (about 5,600 to 5,917 m); child text says "climbed high" only.
7. **Nuria's Spanish line** needs the fluent-reader review spec 120 requires.
8. **Subject check** on the zone bench rule, the carbon bench's regrowth rule and the peat scene (spec review asks).

## Fact-check notes

- **Water shares:** the 100-cup model rounds 97.48% saline to 97 and fresh to 3; the real fresh share is about 2.5%, stated in the spec and kept out of Sprout and Seedling text. The fresh split (2 ice, 1 underground) follows the USGS fresh-water percentages (computed 2.06, 0.90 cups). **Fixed at review:** the writer's "a few spoonfuls" for rivers and lakes used the whole 1.2% "surface and other" share (0.036 cups), which is mostly ground ice and permafrost. Fresh lakes and rivers alone are 0.0072% of all water: 0.0072 cups of 100, about 0.35 of a US teaspoon. Child text now says "less than a spoonful", and the fresh-only graph's third bar is labelled "everything else 1 (rivers, lakes, wet soil, air)" instead of "rivers and lakes 1".
- **Wells, not "under most land" (review):** USGS lists more saline groundwater (0.93%) than fresh (0.76%), so T5's "the water under most land is fresh" became "the water in most wells is fresh".
- **Carbon regrowth rule (review):** with only the dead-wood arrow paused, the soil still gives back 1 a turn and air stays at 9 while soil drains (computed 4, 4, 3, 2, 1). The stated 9, 8, 7, 6 needs both rotting arrows to rest. The spec now says so, and the companion has a regrowth line.
- **Distractors and wording (review):** four distractors whose tag named no real misconception ("guessing a cause" in 3.1 and 5.2, a vague 3.3B, and 1.1C tagged water-only-where-seen) were rewritten or retagged: 3.1B "longest days" (day-length-causes-heat), 5.2B "the sea warms up faster than land" (water-heats-fast), 3.3B "only slanted sunlight" (ray-angle), 1.1C clouds-hold-most-water. Answer positions unchanged (A 9, B 8, C 8). Glossary gained mercury, dam, wind turbine and solar panel, which child text used untaught. Six runs of six or more words shared with the Marble research text were reworded (spec Natural-resources row, water and carbon lines; companion 2.4, 4.2, 5.5, sink card, glossary, grown-up panel). The saved `coast` field (a seen flag nothing read) was cut.
- **Season (Codex review, PR #178):** spec 120 §6 ties seasons to the device calendar, and a chapter can be replayed in any season, so the high-ford scene no longer says "summer" or "apples are ripening". It contrasts the cold mountain with the warmer valley instead. Nuria's "every spring" snowmelt is a yearly pattern in a letter, not the current season, so it stays.
- **Bramble in Act IV (review):** the writer's Bramble line ("wood is carbon too") was true but weak beside a peat store; spec 120 says she is allowed to be right. She now also says a new marsh would take ages to build peat, which is true (under about 2 mm a year), and the librarian says she is right on both.
- **Troposphere scale:** 10 km on a 30 cm globe is 0.235 mm (computed); "about two sheets of paper".
- **Air weight:** 1.2 kg/m³ × 50 m³ = 60 kg; 101,325 Pa over 1 cm² is 1.03 kg (computed). Values appear only at the Keeper's Desk, per the Marble constraint.
- **Périer:** (26 + 3.5/12) − (23 + 2/12) = 3.125 French inches; at 27.07 mm per inch, 84.6 mm (computed), "about 8 cm".
- **CO₂:** 420 ÷ 280 = 1.5, "half as much again". The child card says "more than 420", which holds for every reading since 2022.
- **Balloon on a balance:** an inflated balloon holds air above surrounding pressure, so it outweighs a flat one slightly even after buoyancy; the text says "a tiny bit".
- **Hollis rule and story facts:** no scene names the old Keeper; Vadoalto's snowmelt flood is new Willowbank fiction that fits spec 120 (upstream, mountain side) and contradicts nothing there.
