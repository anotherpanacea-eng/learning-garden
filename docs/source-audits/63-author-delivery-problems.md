## Feasibility and frozen-work boundary

Require all four native landmarks placed at distinct in-bounds intersections and three **distinct** selected landmarks. In this unobstructed orthogonal grid, the exact minimum required number of moves is:

`abs(start.x-via.x) + abs(start.y-via.y) + abs(via.x-to.x) + abs(via.y-to.y)`.

Each segment must change the corresponding coordinate difference, and an axis-aligned route attains that sum entirely within the rectangular grid. Distinct via/destination ensures the required stop can occur before the final endpoint. An earlier pass through the destination remains allowed because delivery is checked at route completion. This proof relies on current no-obstacle, no-diagonal, unit-move semantics; a later obstacle model would need a different feasibility check.

Only create the solving job when the minimum is≤12. For example, start `(0,0)`, collect `(5,5)`, deliver `(0,1)` needs19 moves and is structurally readable but cannot fit this task limit. Explain the limit and allow revised positions/conditions; do not publish an impossible request silently. Feasibility is a **creation-time rule**, not a new historical-source/result validity rule. Old jobs/checks must remain readable exactly, including preserved outcomes or maps that newer authoring controls would refuse.

Freeze the complete map, start/via/destination and canonical request in the new sheet. Begin its A/B lists empty so author routes/checks do not become another solver’s answer. Preserve the source free map, its live commands and prior checks except ordinary explicitly disclosed12-map retention. Creating a new sheet at capacity may evict the oldest readable sheet under the existing cap; preview/selection/rendering must not do that. Future/unsupported records remain exact and conservative. Existing source map edits cannot alter a frozen job or silently relabel old checks.

Reuse existing `mapJobFacts`: complete unblocked route, final destination and intermediate visit before final endpoint. Arrival and fulfillment remain distinct. Do not compare commands with a stored exemplar, require the shortest route, award a shortest-path badge or require both A/B lists to succeed before continuing. Routes with detours/retracing within12 commands can satisfy the request. Familiarity, author exposure, narration/help and a human partner’s possible involvement remain separate from route outcomes and independent performance claims.
