# Source audit: spec 265, The Magpie Takes It Literally

Date: 2026-10-25. Writer's first pass for [spec 265](../specs/265-magpie-takes-it-literally.md) and its [companion text](../content/265-magpie-takes-it-literally-text.md).

**Method and limits.** Standards pages were fetched directly (Common Core, gov.uk); everything else was checked against web-search excerpts from the named pages (14 searches). Merriam-Webster blocked a direct fetch, so its definitions are from search excerpts. A reviewer with ordinary web access should open the cited pages before build, and fluent Spanish and French readers must check every **[ES]** and **[FR]** line. Claims not confirmed are marked `[check]` below and kept out of child text, or worded so the child text does not depend on them.

## Fiction and new writing (no source needed; labeled in the text)

Willowbank, the magpie and everything it says, Ember's party, Dot's four-line birthday verse, the six party lines, the two storms, the market debate, the baker's "We knead you" sign, the umbrella stall, the fog blanket and the silver-ribbon brook, the slender, thin and scrawny cat, and Bramble's Act IV rehearsal. All newly written for this pack and labeled "A story from Willowbank". Bramble's Act IV figures restate spec 120's own positions (the Wall protects what is behind it; the orchard families have farmed the low fields for three generations; the Sponge is Hollis's map label), and the "leaves out" lines use only spec 120 §9's modest claims (a wall can send water elsewhere; a marsh can hold water and slow it).
