## Review pass (independent review and fact-check, 2026-10-25)

Nine WebSearch calls (WebFetch blocked). Math rerun with `python3 -I` (trays, B5, D3, E2, all 12 `stall-v1` designs): all correct.

- **Radiosondes:** NWS poster says almost 900 sites, Hurricane Science over 800, a WMO-register paper about 750; not all launch twice daily. Child card now says "hundreds of places".
- **Met Office 1854:** confirmed (Met Office history page: Board of Trade Meteorological Department, 1854; FitzRoy appointed 1 August 1854).
- **"Forecast":** Met Office blog says FitzRoy chose the term over "prediction"; the word itself is older. Child text now says only "He called them forecasts."
- **The Times, 1 August 1861:** confirmed (Met Office archive; written on the 31 July Daily Weather Report).
- **Le Verrier and 14 November 1854:** confirmed (Observatoire de Paris PDF; storm took days to cross from the Atlantic). "Wrecked" softened to "battered".
- **Franklin:** pointed rods proposed 1750 (letters to Collinson, grounding wire in July 1750); first precise instructions in *Poor Richard* for 1753 (Founders Online chronology). Kept.
- **TIROS-1, 1 April 1960:** confirmed (NOAA NESDIS). Not in child text.
- **NOAA 90/80/50%:** the K-12 page states no definition; cut from the spec.
- **NWS thunder line:** weather.gov says hearing thunder means you are probably within striking distance and should get to a safe place; grown-up card reworded to match. No model-made safety advice anywhere.
- **Wind arrows:** spec 140's vane points into the wind (where it comes from). This pack's map arrows show where the wind goes, consistent with its H-to-L flow arrows; the key now contrasts the two in one line.
- **Story:** per spec 120 and spec 143's device, Hollis is never named before Act III's end; the sea letter is a postcard signed only "H." and nobody identifies the sender; the Forecast Room belonged to "the old Keeper". Wren Halloway she/her confirmed in spec 120.
- **Pedagogy:** shortened right answers in A3, A5, B2, B4, C3 so they are not the longest or only hedged; D2 hint no longer gives the answer; history and flashlight cards split to stay under 30 words a card.
