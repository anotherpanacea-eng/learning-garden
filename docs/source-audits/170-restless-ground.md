## Review pass (independent review and fact check, 2026-10-25)

Three web searches (excerpts only; page fetches blocked); arithmetic re-run in a scratch `python3 -I` script.

- **Garrotxa station (open check 1):** ICGC's ZVGAR page lists the owner as Instituto Geográfico Nacional, code ES.ZVGAR, recording since 6 August 2021 ([ICGC](https://www.icgc.cat/en/Seismic-stations/Seismic-stations/Volcanic-Zone-la-Garrotxa-seismic-station-ZVGAR)). "Records very little" rests only on the 2021 3Cat report, so the spec now dates it and the child card says "hearing little so far." No current status found.
- **Pyrenees (cross-check with spec 139):** sources describe Iberia-Europe collision with Iberian lower crust partly subducted beneath Europe and upper crust thrust into a wedge (Field Trip B16, Geologische Bundesanstalt; UB repository). Card 3's "Neither dived under" was wrong and now reads "Continental crust is too light to sink far, so it crumpled." Matches spec 139 (Iberian and European plates converged and collided).
- **Alert colors (open check):** USGS Orange is "heightened or escalating unrest with increased potential of eruption, timeframe uncertain" ([USGS HVO](https://www.usgs.gov/observatories/hvo/news/volcano-watch-usgs-updates-volcano-observatory-notice-aviation-alerts)). The Watch Desk colors are now labeled as Old Ash's own, borrowed from aviation colors; C2's right answer says "likelier," not "likely."
- **Magnitude (open check 4):** one wording throughout: each whole step is ten times taller wiggles (amplitude) and about 32 times more energy; two steps 100× wiggles, about 1,000× energy (10^1.5 ≈ 31.6). Card 5 changed from "bigger" to "taller."
- **Tsunami (open check 5):** spec says "a few feet high or less (NOAA)"; child text says "low" or "only a little higher than normal"; B6's explanation dropped "a few feet." √(9.8 × 4,000) ≈ 198 m/s ≈ 713 km/h; at 40 m ≈ 19.8 m/s, a 10× ratio: confirmed.
- **Plate edges:** "big earthquakes" no longer marks only transform edges (convergent edges make the largest ones); card 3 and B2 say "earthquakes, usually no volcanoes," and the Plate Edge Board lets the earthquake chip fit every edge.
- **Spain code:** the Eurocode 8 clause was cut; NCSE-02 mandatory per IISEE 2024.
- **Math:** `shake-v1` enumeration (12 designs, 6 stand in the big shake, max 5, What if? cracks), B7 (P 6, S 3 km/s → 6 km per second of gap), C4 and C8 all confirmed.
- **Story:** spec 120 has nobody in Willowbank know the heron's name in Act I, and the flood ledger is the parcel's page 2 (end of Act II). Scene 1 is now Wren's own storm-damp town ledger with a column in an unnamed old hand; Hollis is not named anywhere in the 170 files. No death, no peril; tsunami natural signs stay only in the Grown-up panel with a pointer to local authorities.
