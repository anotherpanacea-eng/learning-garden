## Corrections to the topic records

- **Suffixes (age 9+), evidence 1:** "-cious follows a vowel while -tious follows a consonant" is contradicted by its own examples (cautious, ambitious have a vowel letter before t) and is not DfE's rule. Not taught; S1's -ce root rule is.
- **Silent Letters, evidence 1 and 2:** psalm is listed among letters "once pronounced"; island and doubt among "remnants of earlier pronunciation". S2 and S3 show island's s and doubt's b were never said in English. Psalm's English history was not checked, so psalm is left out (`[check]`, not in child text). DfE S1 lists doubt and island beside "used to be sounded"; the pack is more precise.
- **Homophones (age 9+), evidence 2:** the -ise/-ice verb/noun clue (practise, licence) is British; US spelling uses practice and license for both. Grown-up note only.
