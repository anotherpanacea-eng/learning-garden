- A first search budget ran out before every claim had a second source; a second fact-check pass (below) cleared the open items.

## Review pass (independent reviewer, 2026-10-25)

- **"About 7 cubits good at Elephantine" (source 3):** a review search found no confirmation, and surviving Elephantine readings run much higher (Greek-era floods over 24 cubits on that scale; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilometer, orientation only). The sentence is cut from the spec, and item C5 now uses two made-up gauges, A and B, labeled as made up.
- **Misconception tags:** `bigger-is-better`, `same-zero` and `slaves-built-pyramids` were listed but tagged on no item. C5 and E4 now tag them; E4's "aliens or giants" choice, which was not plausible, became the enslaved-workers story. `roda-is-pharaonic` and `pictures-only` are marked as handled by teach text and build checks.
- **Privacy:** the off-screen numeral activity no longer suggests writing a house number. The two Library talk prompts ask about Willowbank, not the child's own town or year.
- **US spelling** applied (organized, laborers, license, artifact), except in Marble topic names.
- Arithmetic re-run by the reviewer (`python3 -I`): 30 and 12 lifts, 832, 208, 240 ÷ 52 ≈ 4.6, 21, 8, 28, 13, 241, 695 (20 signs), 1,200, 365, 30, 1,460, 100, the timeline order. All pass. Hard-prerequisite crosswalk re-checked against the pinned files by script; complete and correct.
