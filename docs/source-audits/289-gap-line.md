## Primary mathematics/teaching references actually read

The source reader read [NCETM1.27 Negative numbers](https://www.ncetm.org.uk/media/q1cf4wj0/ncetm_mm_sp1_y5_se27_teach.pdf), teaching point5, printed pp21–22 (PDF pages22–23), visually because extracted glyphs were incomplete. It distinguishes signed change from unsigned jump lengths, uses unheaded arcs before directional arrows, varies the starting side and includes entirely negative intervals. [NCETM Single number lines](https://www.ncetm.org.uk/media/8d84e79498db675/ncetm_ks3_representations_single_number_lines.pdf), printed p2, was read for ordered position and meaningful fractional intervals.

Official [7.NS.A.1c](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/7/NS/A/1/c/) supports distance as absolute difference; [7.NS.A.1b](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/7/NS/A/1/b/) distinguishes signed direction and distance. [6.NS.C.7c](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/6/NS/C/7/c/) concerns distance from zero, not every interval. [3.NF.A.2a](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/NF/A/2/a/) and [2b](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/NF/A/2/b/) keep the0–1 whole and equal fractional lengths explicit. These are institutional mathematical/teaching references, not experimental evidence that this game improves learning or grade/placement gates.

## Finite model and independent candidate checks

For constructed end C, signed move is C−A; unsigned length is |C−A|. At the supplied Finish, length is |B−A|. Reversal preserves length and negates change. Splitting gives |A|+|C| only when zero lies between the endpoints; a same-side span must not detour to zero. At coincidence, both length and change are0. Count equal intervals, not their boundary marks. Half-unit pieces retain the same whole unit.

The six authored lengths are13,6,13,5,0,3½, with signed changes+13,−6,−13,+5,0,+3½. The half-unit return−1½→+2 has seven half pieces: three to zero, four beyond. Same-below can have a wrong ruler end+4 with the correct length6, demonstrating why endpoint and length results stay separate. An earlier exact temporary source study checked841 half-grid endpoint pairs for reversal, zero, decomposition and length identities; it did not execute the proposed implementation or review these final source/copy files. Separate finite math/content review has since checked the six authored cases, all nine helpers and146 endpoint positions across four presentation settings with0 findings; the six-lens panel and final independent specification adjudication are recorded in [review289](../reviews/289-gap-line-spec.md).
