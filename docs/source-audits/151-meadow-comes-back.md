Date: 2026-10-25. For [spec 151](../specs/151-meadow-comes-back.md) and [its companion text](../content/151-meadow-comes-back-text.md).

## Method and limits

- 17 web searches in the writing pass and 9 in a fact-check pass (26 in all), aimed where possible at encyclopedia, conservation-charity, learned-society and peer-reviewed sources.
- **Direct page fetches were blocked in this session.** Every claim below rests on the search engine's excerpts of the named pages, not a full reading. A reviewer with page access should open each URL and confirm the excerpt before build.
- Some excerpts came from blogs or community posts of reputable bodies (RSPB reserve blogs, a river trust news page). They are marked "indicative" and are not the only support for a child-facing claim unless noted.
- Fiction is labeled separately: Willowbank, Hob's meadow, the fallen willow, the survey square and all its numbers, the lawn square, Nuria's letter and the joint balsam pulling are a story from Willowbank. The survey and energy numbers are a made-up model. No quotation, diary or eyewitness voice is invented.

## Fact-check pass (2026-10-25)

All seven open checks from the writing pass were settled:

1. **Willow seed lifespan:** confirmed as days to a few weeks for summer-dispersed willows (39). The Keeper's note now says so; no `[check]` remains.
2. **Fallen willow still growing:** no source documents a fallen trunk re-rooting directly, apart from anecdote. The wording was **corrected** to what sources support: willows resprout strongly after being cut or broken and root easily in damp ground, so a fallen willow "can stay alive and sprout" (5, 6, 40).
3. **Barn owl and field voles:** confirmed, about 45% of British diet (37). Evidence line now "the barn owl's main food in Britain".
4. **Grasshopper links:** grasshopper → kestrel confirmed from Spanish and Canary studies (41). The evidence line now says "in parts of Spain, grasshoppers are a kestrel chick's main food". Grasshopper → frog confirmed only as a minor prey item (42); the line now reads "frogs catch many kinds of insects, grasshoppers among them". A frog's main prey is not overstated.
5. **Himalayan balsam in the Pyrenees:** confirmed. First records in the upper Segre and Llobregat basins of the Catalan Pyrenees (38). Balsam kept; the Keeper's note adds "recently recorded in the Catalan Pyrenees too".
6. **Succession in a Pyrenean meadow:** supported in general (43). In the Pyrenees, abandoned meadows go to shrubs and forest, and grazing limits this. The willow-first detail rests on floodplain sources (26) and stays a review ask.
7. **Silt as fertilizer:** confirmed from Floodplain Meadows Partnership pages (44), with the caveat about extreme floods added to the spec.

No `[check]` markers remain in the spec or companion. Every claim still rests on search excerpts, so a reviewer with page access should open the URLs.

## Review pass (independent review, 2026-10-25)

Two web searches (excerpts only; pages could not be opened):

- **Barn owl diet:** the Barn Owl Trust page (source 37) gives field voles 45%, common shrews 20%, wood mice 15% of British diet. Confirmed. Epping Forest District Council (https://www.eppingforestdc.gov.uk/countrycare/barn-owls/) gives 40-80%, so "main food in Britain" stands.
- **Himalayan balsam in the Pyrenees:** a French state factsheet (Cantal prefecture, https://www.cantal.gouv.fr/contenu/telechargement/6534/72002/file/Impatience_Himalaya_cle0ea26b.pdf) says it has been seen along French Pyrenean rivers since the early 20th century. So the 2021 Catalan paper (38) gives new records for the upper Segre and Llobregat basins only, not the first in the Pyrenees. The spec's wording was corrected. The Keeper's note now says "on many European riverbanks, the Pyrenees among them".

Other changes: the spec now says the 10 percent rule is "usually credited" to Lindeman. One balsam line was softened from "nothing else gets light" to "smaller plants get little light". A script (scratch, `python3 -I`) recomputed every number (15, 4, 14, 4.5, 10, 500, 125, 2,000, 16; energy-slider levels) and confirmed them. It also checked choice positions (A, B and C five times each) and word limits. The "40 percent" in the spec's check list was removed because no item asks it.
