Date: 2026-10-26. Writer's first pass for [spec 251](../specs/251-dots-first-postcards.md) and its [companion text](../content/251-dots-first-postcards-text.md).

**Method and limits.** England's programme of study page was fetched directly; Appendix 1 (spelling) and Appendix 2 (vocabulary, grammar and punctuation) were downloaded from GOV.UK and their text extracted with `pdftotext`. The Common Core grade 1 Language page was fetched directly. The handwriting-family lists, the EEF guidance, the handwriting meta-analysis, Wednesday's pronunciation, capitalization style and Thai word breaking were checked through web-search excerpts (7 searches, 3 page fetches). Rows marked * were seen only through excerpts; a reviewer with ordinary web access should open them before build. No research text is quoted at length.
