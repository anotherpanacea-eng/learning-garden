## Actual app overlap and minimum new action

`dist/body-clues-content.mjs` and the frozen v1 `dist/body-clues-art.mjs` already show a land snail's broad foot and an earthworm's ringed body. `dist/body-clues-ui.mjs` asks for jointed walking legs and a second feature, then stores frozen picture-key trails in `bodyClues`. It does **not** let the child change a body-motion model. The earthworm's tiny bristles are not depicted in that old drawing; adding bristles to the frozen v1 renderer would change the apparent source for older saved visits. The new original drawing is therefore separate, while using the two familiar pictured adults and the Living Garden location. The new action is stepping two mechanisms and comparing what changes shape or grips, not following another identification branch or answering a multiple-choice recall item.

Life Stages (`dist/life-stages-*`) models a butterfly's life-cycle arrows; Food Links/Fork (`dist/food-links-*`, `dist/food-fork-*`) models food-to-eater evidence, not locomotion. Habitat visits ask where conditions fit; they do not establish structure-to-movement reasoning. A sibling `#living/movement` teaching view keeps those histories unmodified. No learner response, check or later suggestion depends on which model frame was last open, so a transient index is enough. The existing host connection panel can still register shown arithmetic facts on Living Garden routes; that is not caused by the movement model. Old key records and their frozen copy remain the authority for old visits, never today's model caption.
