A coupled clock must move the hour hand as the minute hand advances. At half past9 the minute hand is at6 and the hour hand is midway from9 toward10; it does not stay on9 or jump to10. At the next full hour the minute hand returns to12 while the hour hand advances one numeral. The same printed face numeral has different hour/minute meanings. Half-hour30 and whole-hour60 are durations, whereas9:30 is a time-of-day label; `:30` is not a decimal fraction of an hour.

## New learner action, limits and review risks

The bounded candidate is an original preparation context with a coupled analogue clock, labelled native30-minute steps and analogue/digital matching at hour and half-hour positions. This can teach how a stated time has two representations and how the hour hand changes between numerals. Optional supplied30/60-minute advancement may demonstrate the unit relationship; it must not be recorded as independently calculating an unknown endpoint or elapsed duration. Final case selection, input bounds, support visibility and check behavior remain for specification/review.

Coupled controls deliberately prevent impossible hand arrangements. They show correct geometry, but the software supplies the independent coordination/placement of both hands. A successful target reached by labelled steps may reflect counting supplied increments or following a digital readout. Record the actual interaction and support; do not infer independent analogue reading when an answer label is visible, independent drawing, unsupported recall or general duration problem solving. Do not remove accessible text merely to make an assessment appear independent; define any separate reading/matching prompt and its support honestly.

A12-hour face repeats: face position alone cannot tell morning/afternoon, date or elapsed time across cycles. Same-morning supplied cases strictly before noon avoid ambiguity for a first contextual task; they do not teach noon/midnight, a.m./p.m. distinctions or12-/24-hour conversion. If an exploratory control can wrap through12, identify it as face exploration rather than silently inventing the next actual appointment or resetting a chronological timeline. Native30-minute simulation is not30minutes of physical elapsed time, a real-time deadline, stopwatch use or a measured child activity.
