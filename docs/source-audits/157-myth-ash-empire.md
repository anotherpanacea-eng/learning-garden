# Source audit 157: Myth, Ash and Empire

Date: 2026-10-25. For [spec 157](../specs/157-myth-ash-empire.md) and [its companion text](../content/157-myth-ash-empire-text.md).

**Method and its limit.** Direct page fetches were blocked in this session. Every claim below was checked through web-search results that quote or summarize the named pages, in two passes (18 searches, then 20 in a fact-check pass). Where the strongest source surfaced was a secondary or general site, the row says so. Superseded first-pass rows (lunch, election-notice counts, Julius Nepos) are no longer used in the text. Claims carried over from sibling specs rely on those specs' audits (133 for Romulus, the Palatine huts and the Parthenon's dates; 139 for the Pompeii card). **A reviewer with ordinary web access should open the cited pages before build**.
