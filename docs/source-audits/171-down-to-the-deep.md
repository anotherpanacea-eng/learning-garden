## Review pass (independent review and fact-check, 2026-10-25)

Ten WebSearch calls (fetches blocked). Findings and changes:

- **Seabed 2030:** 27.3% of the seafloor mapped to modern standards, announced 21 June 2025 from the GEBCO 2025 Grid (Nippon Foundation news https://tnfsa.nippon-foundation.or.jp/news/2756701 ; Map Room https://www.maproomblog.com/2025/06/27-3-of-the-ocean-floor-has-now-been-mapped/); prior 23.4%. Spec now gives the dated figure; child card says "just over a quarter" by 2025. `[check]` cleared.
- **Not crushed:** secondary sources (Discover Wildlife https://www.discoverwildlife.com/animal-facts/marine-animals/why-deep-sea-animals-dont-implode ; Naked Scientists) agree tissue and water barely compress and the risk lies in gas spaces; many deep fish lack swim bladders. Teach 6 softened to "mostly water, which hardly squashes, with little or no air inside." No NOAA page returned; a subject expert should confirm.
- **Clownfish diet:** Britannica and aquarium pages: zooplankton and small invertebrates plus some algae. "Tiny animals and bits of algae" kept.
- **Sea cucumbers and marine snow:** abyssal sea cucumbers are deposit feeders on marine snow that settles on the floor (Museums Victoria *Scotoplanes* profile https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/species/16875 ; University of Gothenburg CCZ expedition news). Kept.
- **Challenger 1875:** 23 March 1875 and 4,475 fathoms confirmed as the usual figure (History.com; Edinburgh "Measuring the depths"); the 1891 report table lists 4,476 fathoms at station 226. Spec notes the variant; child text says "about 8,184 m".
- **Trieste:** 23 January 1960, Piccard and Walsh confirmed (USNI *Proceedings* 2010 and 2020; Navy NHHC). Walsh gives 5 h down, 20 min, 3½ h up; other accounts give 4 h 47 min down and 3 h 15 min up. Child card now credits "Walsh's own figures"; A9 uses them with "about".
- **Alvin 1977:** Galápagos Rift, February 1977 (dive 713 on 17 February per WHOI Dive and Discover https://divediscover.whoi.edu/archives/ventcd/vent_discovery/thediscovery/trail_77_page5.html), tube worms and giant clams (WHOI). Kept at year level.
- **Tharp:** 1957 North Atlantic physiographic diagram confirmed (National Library of Australia copy https://nla.gov.au/nla.obj-3005098339/view); barred from ships, first cruise 1968 (Columbia Science FAS; WHOI biography https://www.whoi.edu/person/marie-tharp). Kept.
- **0.001%:** Bell et al., "How Little We've Seen," *Science Advances*, May 2025; under 0.001% of the deep seafloor (deeper than 200 m) visually observed, about Rhode Island or a tenth of Belgium (NPR via WUSF https://www.wusf.org/2025-05-07/humans-still-havent-seen-99-999-of-the-deep-seafloor). Kept.
- **Reef 25% and 80% from land:** not re-searched; the writer's handling (estimate of unclear origin; "most" in child text, 80% only as an old rough estimate at Keeper's Desk) is the right level of caution and is kept.
- **Science fix (not search-based):** the sunlight and vent chains had "sunlight" and "chemicals" as arrow nodes read "is eaten by", which is false. Chains now start at phytoplankton and bacteria with the energy source as a label. Tube worms host symbiotic bacteria rather than eating them, so the vent chain uses bacteria, vent snail (grazers of bacterial mats), crab; Teach 9 says tube worms keep bacteria inside them.
- **Story:** postcard D3 no longer says "Tell whoever thought it was flat", which implied H. knew Willowbank's talk and blurred spec 143's rule that H. never reveals Hollis before Act VI. All H. use now matches spec 143 option (a).
- **Math:** all Set A answers and spec figures recomputed with `python3 -I`; all correct. Answer positions A 4, B 4, C 3 confirmed. Hard-prerequisite crosswalk verified by script against pinned files.
- **Cross-pack:** no conflicting figure for Challenger Deep, Everest, Tharp or depth zones in specs 133-173; 1,000 m midnight zone matches spec 143.
- **Size:** spec and companion trimmed under the 22,000 and 32,000 byte limits.
