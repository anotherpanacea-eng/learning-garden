## Corrections to the brief and to Marble

- **Quorum:** presented as two readings (Plutarch: 6,000 in total; Philochorus: 6,000 against one man), with most scholars following Plutarch and one recent argument for no quorum.
- **Council by lot:** confirmed for the classical period; whether lot applied from 508/507 itself is left as a subject-review question rather than stated.
- **Marble's "Athens invented democracy"** and **"roughly 30% of adults"**: softened to "one of the earliest and best-recorded" and replaced by stated ranges, with the adults-versus-everyone distinction taught.
- **Ostraka count:** "thousands survive" is supported and sharpened to "more than 10,000" (Kerameikos 9,367 plus the Agora's 1,336 catalogued in 1990 and later finds).
