These references guide design; two authored screen cases do not satisfy the standards or establish an intervention effect.

## Author checks and review questions

The arithmetic is exact: `24=2×10+4`, `13=1×10+3`, `17=1×10+7`; combined loose ones are seven or eleven; the latter can become one new ten plus one loose one. The example `12+18=30` uses two original tens plus a new ten from ten ones. The counter labels and colored/patterned marks must preserve who supplied each unit, even after a mixed-origin wrap. A number typed into the proposed total is a response to a supplied and pictured question, not evidence that the user independently performed or wrote a place-value algorithm.

A temporary exhaustive author check of the proposed seven-field state (separate/joined, loose/frame/new-ten counts by origin) found 21 reachable states for `24+13` and 44 for `24+17`, including two valid mixed-origin new tens, `3 Pip + 7 Dot` and `4 Pip + 6 Dot`. It counted 83 and 182 enabled transitions respectively, including Join and explicit Reset. Every enumerated transition preserved each operand's own counter count, combined total, frame capacity and the at-most-one-new-ten rule. This is a design arithmetic check, not a runtime test or pedagogical result.

Independent review should challenge whether one-click joining plus manual framing is enough learner action, whether mixed-origin constituents stay countable at phone width, whether the example gives too much of the answer, whether the no-regroup case remains interesting, and whether a separate save root under the same Bundle Burrow is the smallest safe compatibility choice. The user-facing route and mode must remain clearly connected to the Exchange Table without rewriting prior saves. Source/content reviewers should reject any visual that counts a wrapper as an extra counter, hides an operand after joining, presents a wrong draft as a true equality, or calls a shown/correct answer independent strategy evidence.
