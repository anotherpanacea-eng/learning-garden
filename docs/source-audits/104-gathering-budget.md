# Gathering Budget: source and overlap audit

Author preparation for [spec 104](../specs/104-gathering-budget.md), 2026-10-22. This is a proposed two-case fictional planning table, not a taxonomy repin, runtime change, financial assessment, or claim that a family has or needs a particular amount of money.

## Actual existing actions and the new boundary

Read the live `dist/coin-counter-core.mjs`, `market-change-core.mjs`, `cafe-core.mjs`, `sharing-counter-core.mjs`, `garden-paving-core.mjs`, Workshop dispatch/UI, and specs 22, 43, 57, 67 and 96. Coin Counter compares two coin compositions against one fixed price. Market Change compares fixed tender and price, then builds the returned amount. The Café matches a guest's food wishes and later finds ingredient stock; money is absent and its cupboard is part of that ongoing order. Sharing Counter makes a fraction parcel, while Garden Paving toggles patches to complete a square; neither plans spending. The [strand map](../strand-map.md) and [roadmap](../roadmap.md) explicitly leave a budget/useful trade-off action missing in L1. The new action is to **choose and revise a multi-packet basket against two separate constraints**, a spending limit and a supply request. No existing payment/check/stock record is reinterpreted.

The proposed first table is transient, tied to one visit and explorer. It reads only its original case data and touches no notebook, Café order, coin/change round or saved Workshop experiment. The optional typed total is a separate observation about the chosen basket, not a prerequisite for a working basket. A purchase or monetary exchange does not actually occur.

The closest Marble topic is `mt_Qzbh-_v0Gq`, **Budgeting Pocket Money**. Its actual description is planning uses of a fixed allowance and making trade-offs. Its evidence includes a £10 plan with money left, explaining a budget, and identifying a trade-off. Treat the topic as source context, **not a new declared atlas reference** or completed topic. A future declaration needs its own semantic review and caveats.

The [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's 2022 teacher guide, *Using a budget to shop for a party*](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_building_block_activities_using-budget-shop-party_guide.pdf) explicitly uses an imaginary party, a finite spending limit, choices from a list, adding selected costs, and revising an over-limit choice. This is primary pedagogical context for the constrained-choice concept, not proof of efficacy. Our town setting, two-constraint packet model, all catalog entries, numbers, child copy, graphics and controls are original. We do not copy its worksheet, $25 scenario, food list, pictures, lesson text or reflection questions; we do not treat its stated grade/age as an access rule or a measured outcome. The source discusses actual budgeting and personal choices; our game deliberately does not evaluate a family's priorities, identify necessities, or teach an authoritative financial rule.

## Finite model verification and scope held back

The welcome catalog has 48 stock-respecting basket vectors and five that both meet the request and fit 16¢. The path catalog has 90 vectors and three that meet its request and fit 18¢. Both contain examples for budget-fit/request-miss, budget-miss/request-fit, both-fit, and both-miss. Prices are small whole pretend cents; each packet's content and stock are fully supplied. The learner can propose a cost but need not do so. Feedback must never call a basket wrong merely because the typed total is different, or call a basket working merely because it fits the budget. No optimization, loans, earning, change, savings, profit, real shopping, family disclosure, full entrepreneurship loop or measured financial judgment is in scope.

The first implementation should use the existing Workshop route/disclosure style, local counting arithmetic, original SVG/CSS packet art and native buttons. It must not mutate the saved Workshop experiment merely by opening this branch. The [static responsive mock](../specs/assets/104-gathering-budget-mock.html) is design evidence only. Independent spec review still must check numeric truth, copy, source restraint, lifecycle and rendered phone/tablet geometry before build. No atlas output, coverage count or upstream database was edited for this proposal.
