## Fiction and new texts (no source needed; labeled in the text)

Willowbank, the librarian, the storm-soaked shelves and the baker's windowsill, the coverless book, the notes table, both notices, and the authorship of every library book (Hob Pennick, Wren Halloway, Pip, Ember, Puff, the baker) are fiction, labeled "A story from Willowbank". All book text (*Swallows Come Home*, *The Busy Hive*, *Ask the Weather*), the two story covers and the baker's poem *Bread* were written new for this pack on 2026-10-25. No owner writing, Dropbox material or living author's text is used.
