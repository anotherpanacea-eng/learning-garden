### Definition and bounded knowledge

Primary [NPS, What is a Fossil?](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/fossils/what-is-a-fossil.htm), updated 1 November 2024, distinguishes body remains from activity traces and requires geological context. It also says fossils need not all be stone-replaced or above one age threshold. The first pack therefore teaches past life in rock/**other geological deposits**, without a universal millions-of-years rule, death requirement or fossil-from-any-mark claim. The two particular source records support the supplied specimen identities; the tiny picture selection is not independent field identification. The pictures' photographed colors do not establish living color or sound, dates of modern source pages are not fossil dates, and two sources from different places do not establish contemporaneous organisms or a common environment.

No source images are reconstructed, recolored, flipped or completed by AI. A viewing rectangle and its crop are the same photo; pinning is not independent corroboration. Exact normalized rectangles/visible captions must be checked against the actual photo pixels at phone/tablet sizes before build. The two photographs are not at a shared physical scale. Full credited records/originals remain reachable next to images/details.
