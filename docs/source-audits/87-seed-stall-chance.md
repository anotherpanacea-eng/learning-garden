## Model and content boundaries

Uniform choice among six visible positions is an **authored rule of the screen model**. Returning the chosen counter before the next request keeps that rule fixed. This does not imply that a real bag has same-sized seeds, is thoroughly mixed, or yields equal physical selection probabilities; the picture uses same-sized counters to avoid that hidden premise. A 4:2 bag makes the first mark more likely on each screen draw, but does not guarantee more of it among six outcomes. At 0:6 or 6:0, the single present mark is guaranteed by the pictured source. Counts of the two marks in each six-draw run are observations **of this virtual run**, not estimates sufficient to certify the software selector or a real bag. No short-run mismatch is treated as a mistake, and no compensating next draw is promised.

All mark names, source explanations, prompts, result wording, counters and chart graphics will be original Learning Garden work. No Jane or Marble wording, pack item, illustration, screenshot or code is incorporated. Source links are for reviewer provenance and limited topic comparison. The implementation must separately verify browser random selection, source freezing, progress/report limits and display; these repository and curriculum sources do not establish those runtime facts.

## Independent review questions

1. Does the configured bag and one-press-per-draw flow create a real learner experiment, rather than a formula quiz or a predetermined story?
2. Are prediction-before-outcome, per-run source and observed draws kept separate, including surprising outcomes and one-mark-only bags?
3. Does the wording avoid a fairness proof, “due next” reasoning, physical evidence, and a long-run convergence claim from at most 18 draws?
4. Are frozen source, raw sibling recovery, actual report preflight and history-cap actions implementable within the existing optional-root patterns without touching other games?
5. Is the partial Marble relation truthful and correctly kept out of the atlas until built and separately reviewed?
