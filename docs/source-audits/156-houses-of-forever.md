# Source audit 156: Houses of Forever

Date: 2026-10-25. For [spec 156](../specs/156-houses-of-forever.md) and [its companion text](../content/156-houses-of-forever-text.md).

## Method and limits

- 18 web searches in the first pass and 15 in a fact-check pass, aimed at the claims that matter most: who built the pyramids, how stone was moved and raised, monument sizes and sequence, mummification, the weighing of the heart, the gods' names, the social limits, trade and the deben.
- **Direct page fetches were blocked.** Every claim rests on search excerpts of the named pages. A reviewer with page access should open each URL before build. Where only a weak or secondary source was found, it is marked as such and the claim is either softened or listed under Open checks.
- Facts shared with spec 134 (Great Pyramid date, Khufu, the Heit el-Ghurab town, Tutankhamun, museum ethics) reuse 134's audited sources, cited by number below as "134-#".
- No primary source, quotation, diary or eyewitness voice is invented. Merer's logbook and the Turin Strike Papyrus are named and paraphrased, never quoted. All Willowbank material (the librarian, Pip, Dot, Bramble, Wren, Vadoalto's museum and its loan box of copies) is fiction and labeled.
