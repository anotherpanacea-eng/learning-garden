## Review pass (independent reviewer, 2026-10-25)

- **RIB 2244** re-searched: RIB confirms the date 119-120 CE, "from Ratae 2 miles", and the find point exceeding 2 Roman miles from the east gate by 375 m, with the editors' suggestion that distances were measured from Lincoln if the stone was found in place (https://romaninscriptionsofbritain.org/inscriptions/2244). Kept.
- **Water priority:** Vitruvius 8.6.1-2 (Perseus, https://cts.perseids.org/read/latinLit/phi1056/phi001/perseus-eng1/8.6.1-8.6.3) gives the three-part tank as design advice so public water does not run short; a BMCR review (https://bmcr.brynmawr.edu/?p=22890) reports that the Pompeii castellum's outlets sit on one level, against the traditional reading. Card 5, E3 and the spec now attribute the rule to Vitruvius and no longer say fountains "usually came first" in practice.
- **Story order:** the miller's scene moved from Act III chapter 3 to chapter 4, since it follows Léon's chapter 4 letter. Bramble's Short scene no longer says roads came first "by about two thousand years" (the ribbon has no date).
- Arithmetic and every bench row re-run by the reviewer (`python3 -I`, exact fractions): all match.
