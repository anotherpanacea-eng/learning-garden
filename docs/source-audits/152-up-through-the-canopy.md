**Respect review of the people cards.** Both cards were rewritten to stay present tense, add substance and agency, and avoid both "noble savage" and victims-only framing: each now names the people's own name (Yanomami from *yanõmami*; Baka as their own name, with "Pygmy" avoided), the Yanomami card adds the population and the Hutukara Association (Yanomami people running their own organization), and the Baka card adds the Baka language's forest vocabulary and that farming families still gather in the forest. Related fixes: shifting cultivation no longer says families "move on" (they clear a new garden nearby); the curare card says hunters "have long made" curare; the vanilla card says the Totonac "have grown vanilla for centuries, and many still do"; the needs card gives the Indigenous community agency ("keep its land and make its own choices about it"); the land-evidence card says illegal mining and logging inside territories is by outsiders; D7 no longer argues against a "just stories" straw man.
