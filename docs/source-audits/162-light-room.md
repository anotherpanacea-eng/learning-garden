# Source audit 162: The Light Room

Date: 2026-10-25. For [spec 162](../specs/162-light-room.md) and [its companion text](../content/162-light-room-text.md).

## Method and limits

- Fourteen web searches (budget 18), then four in a second pass, aimed at the dated history claims, the numbers and the safety rule. Optics that is standard textbook physics (straight-line travel, the law of reflection, a 45-degree mirror turning a beam 90 degrees, a periscope's two parallel mirrors, refraction toward and away from the normal) was not searched and is left to the subject check.
- **Direct page fetches were blocked in this session.** Every claim below rests on the search engine's excerpts of the named pages, not a full reading. A reviewer with page access should open each URL before build.
- Several results were popular or secondary sources (Wikipedia, popular science sites). They are marked as secondary, and the spec words those claims cautiously.
- No primary source, quotation, diary or eyewitness voice is invented. Newton's phrase "New Theory about Light and Colours" is a published title, not a quotation from his text. All Willowbank material (the Loft, Dot, Bramble, the miller's cat, the show, the rainbow over Willow Brook) is fiction and labeled.
