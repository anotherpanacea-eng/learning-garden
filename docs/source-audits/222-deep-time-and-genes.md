- **Independent review (2026-10-25):** a fresh reviewer re-ran 13 web searches (Renne 1997 abstract and Lanphere 2007; the 2026 *Science Advances* study; the bucardo; the melomys; Australian megafauna dating; Pompeii's unexcavated share; saurischian pneumaticity; Pliny 6.16 and 6.20; Mojica) and a fresh script, `review222.py` (scratch area `oca13/review-222/`, `python3 -I`): **332 checks, 0 failed** after fixes (4 failed before: the D4 agreement count named a "rock" line that is not a tray; "no dinosaur DNA is left" contradicted birds-as-dinosaurs; F2 asked about the melomys with no card teaching it).
