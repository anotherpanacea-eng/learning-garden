## Actual operation and overlap

198’s Fit Board proposes repeating one fraction-sized measure along another amount. Its old claim that nobody multiplies/divides by a fraction is stale: `linear-pathway-content.mjs` already explains6÷(3/2)=4, Equality Desk already supports rational arithmetic, and Field Strips290 constructs part-of-part area. Sharing Counter partitions shares; Join Portions adds fixed-denominator pieces; Fraction Trail places values; Recut/Matches rename or match fixed amounts; Common Cuts292 makes two amounts use common partitions.294 adds a different learner operation: repeatedly place a reusable group-sized measure, subdivide that group and place a fractional final group. The original unit, whole group and selected partial group stay distinct. No history/Mahavira/translation, whole-pack schema, reciprocal derivation or story adapter is reused or commissioned.
