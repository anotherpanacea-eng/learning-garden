## Fiction, labelled as fiction

Willowbank, Vadoalto, Willow Brook, Hollis's station and both of Hollis's books are fiction. Every rain number in the companion (the 30-year record, the week of rain, the placement readings, the five-year and four-year items) was **made up for this spec** and is labelled "Made-up numbers" on screen. The record was designed so that the decade means differ by much less than the year-to-year spread (population standard deviation about 130 mm), so the "one difference is not a trend" lesson is honest about the data shown. No real station data were used or imitated.
