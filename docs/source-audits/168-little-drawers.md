## Review pass (independent reviewer, 2026-10-25)

Eight web searches (excerpts only), plus scripts. Settled:

- **Germanium:** found 6 February 1886 by Winkler in argyrodite (Wikipedia, Middlebury); 1887 is his follow-up chemistry. Spec now says 1886 only. Mendeleev's eka-silicon: atomic weight about 72, density about 5.5 against germanium's 5.32 (not used in text).
- **Eka-aluminum density:** tables give 5.9 or 6.0; Lecoq's first figure was about 4.7, corrected to 5.9 after Mendeleev's challenge (Wikipedia; Slate). "Near 6" stands.
- **Moseley:** 1913 X-ray work, extended 1914 (Linda Hall Library; Wikipedia). `[check]` removed.
- **Red cabbage and baking soda:** ACS says green for bases; other sheets blue to blue-green near pH 8. Color Jar authors blue-green.
- **Copper patina:** in town air mostly basic copper sulfates (brochantite); carbonates in cleaner air, chlorides by the sea (MDPI *Coatings*; Polimi roof study). Spec corrected from "mostly carbonate."
- **Magpies (2014, Exeter, Shephard et al., *Animal Cognition* 2015):** wary of all new objects, shiny or matte. Companion line made exact.
- **Wolframio:** RAE-preferred Spanish name (secondary source); Elhuyar volfram 1783 confirmed (RAH biography). Greetings "¡Hola!" and "Bonjour !" are correctly punctuated; a fluent read is still owed.
- **Bleach:** pH 11 to 13; C5 now says about 12.
- Not searched, standard and unchanged: 18 groups, 7 periods, 118 named elements (IUPAC 2016), gallium 1875, scandium 1879, Sørensen 1909 (Science History Institute above), Elhuyar 1783.

Other fixes: mixtures ownership line corrected; Mendeleev's Cards now names its twenty cards so the gap under aluminum falls between zinc and arsenic, as the letter says; model label added to the first particle card; two hints no longer give the answer; Dalton's symbols are "for each kind of atom he knew" (some of his "elements" were compounds); hook says "more than a hundred." Crosswalk re-verified by script against pinned files: all hard edges and names correct. No topic id is claimed by any other spec 133-173. Word limits, choice positions (A 5, B 4, C 5) and all numbers rechecked.
