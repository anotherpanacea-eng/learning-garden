## Rights notes for images

No images are embedded. Recommended, subject to a rights check at build time:

- **Bayeux Tapestry:** use an original drawing "in the style of" the embroidery, labeled as a drawing (the stitch desk already specifies this). Photographs are controlled by the Bayeux Museum and the current lender; do not reproduce them without a license. Nineteenth-century public-domain engravings exist but are themselves interpretations and must be labeled as such.
- **Skuldelev 2 and the Gjermundbu helmet:** museum photographs only where the object page carries an explicit open license; otherwise an original illustration, labeled.
- **Vale of York hoard:** British Museum collection images are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial); confirm fit before use, or draw.
- **Castles (Rochester, Beaumaris, Loarre, White Tower):** Wikimedia Commons photographs under CC BY or CC BY-SA with attribution, or original illustrations. Léon's castle is original fiction art.
- **Dye plants:** original botanical drawings; avoid images that could encourage children to gather plants.
- **The Black Death folder:** no images.

Do not colorize, "restore" or invent detail in any historical image, and do not present a modern drawing as a primary source.
