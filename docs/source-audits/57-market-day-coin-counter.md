These are source relations plus explicit access dispositions, not new source edges or learner locks.

Existing overlap inspected: Bundle Exchange conserves a fixed object total under tens/ones packaging; the Coin Counter instead conserves cents across unequal-value denominations, and the **coin count may change**. The Café has ingredients/counts but no monetary exchange. Its meals and inventory remain separate. Sharing Counter measures fractions of a declared whole, not coin value; the word quarter here is a coin name, not a newly assessed fraction. No duplicated currency engine, real payment integration or full-market economy is needed.
