## Review pass (independent reviewer, 2026-10-25)

Five web searches (excerpts only). Settled or softened:

- **Paper cycles:** primary page found, US EPA archived FAQ (https://archive.epa.gov/wastes/conserve/materials/paper/web/html/faqs.html): fibers too short after five to seven cycles. Holmen (paper maker, https://int.holmen.com/en/paper/sustainability/sustainability-stories/paper-buyers-recycled-or-fresh-fibre) says about 3-4 for most quality grades. Spec now cites EPA with "some mills say fewer"; E3 says "a few times, often said to be 5 to 7".
- **"Without loss of quality" for metals and glass:** only industry sources (International Aluminium Institute, Novelis, AISI, GPI); a UC Irvine thesis (https://escholarship.org/uc/item/99b0564m) notes alloy mixing downcycles aluminum, and glass reuse depends on color sorting and contamination. Wording cut to "many times if kept clean and sorted" (spec and Scrap Box).
- **Pyrenean roofs (was `[check]`):** heritage sources show slate in Aspe and Ossau (CAUE 64, https://www.caue64.fr/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/16-habitat-traditionnel-aspe_ossau-livret.pdf) and the Couserans (PNR Pyrénées Ariégeoises, https://www.parc-pyrenees-ariegeoises.fr/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/bilan_ope_ardoise_1999_2010-partie_1.pdf), and schist lauze (flat stone slabs) in much of the Hautes-Pyrénées (https://www.envirobat-oc.fr/IMG/pdf/ardoise_lauze.pdf). Narrowed to "steep roofs of slate or flat stone slabs"; mark removed.
- **Macintosh:** "softened with" changed to "dissolved in" naphtha, matching EBSCO; the undated sailors' oilcloth line cut.
- **Silt jar:** "After a day: the water clears" softened to "a day or more ... starts to clear" (fine clay can stay suspended longer).
- **Scope:** spec 145 (origin/claude/project-thread-pj161v-life-matter) claims Separating Mixtures (`mt_2b6CB0w3Yx`) and lists Grouping Materials as a gap, matching this spec. Spec 154 (forces-inquiry branch) already teaches magnetic materials with the same metal list; now cited as built-on.
