**Method and limits.** Five Common Core Speaking and Listening pages (kindergarten and grades 1 to 3, 5) were fetched directly and grade 4 in part; the English national curriculum's spoken-language page was read from the DfE PDF text already in the writer's scratch (the same PDF spec 275's review opened); two web searches covered the Oracy Framework and the Thinking Together ground rules. ## Privacy and safety review

Every talk card was checked against the binding speaking rules: none asks about the child's body, family, feelings, home, school or location; feelings appear only as characters' (scene text), never as a prompt. Retellings are story days (Puff's market day, Ember's birthday, Hob's goat, the town's year for Hollis). Marble's "recount a personal experience" and "self-assess a recorded presentation" evidence is adapted in the spec, not adopted.
