Read actual Story Loft content/core/UI, reader-directed message/Changing Letters content, instruction content/UI, Notice Printer content and actual host dispatch/appenders. Loft assembles scenes and accepts optional own dialogue. Messages choose complete supplied rewrites to satisfy a reader's concrete need. Instructions already supplies two because/reversed-because help comparisons: folding versus a note too wide, and an arrow versus an unfamiliar visitor. Notice Printer changes word order, first-word capital and ending marks for ask/tell readings and six scripted replies. It does not model the relation between clauses. These are real overlap; another because explanation card alone would add little.

The proposed difference is **changing a conjunction in a clause assembly while the same scene and two facts stay visible**, then swapping clause order to hear what a reason would claim. It does not take a multiple-choice answer, execute an instruction, copy/replace a learner's dialogue, or duplicate old letter-card content. One canonical Loft route, three static cases and one in-memory Undo are enough. Post Office needs only a link. ## Why the readings stay honest

The scene is a fixed source, not a simulation that changes when wording changes. Supper explicitly gives rain as the reason for moving and rules out the reversed weather cause. Lamp gives a blocked-light explanation and an expected brightness, while withholding why the lamp was lit. Two jobs explicitly happen together and do not cause each other; contrasting jobs remains a possible speaker choice. The exact18 notes distinguish these different cases. They never equate a grammatical string with a supported world claim, or treat every but as invalid merely because no expectation was supplied. The same two true clauses can be joined into a false or unsupported explanation; the full current note must accompany visible and spoken trials.
