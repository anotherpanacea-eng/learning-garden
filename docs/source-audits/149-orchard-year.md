## Verification status: read this first

Page fetches were blocked by the session's network policy, so every confirmation below rests on **web-search result excerpts** (17 standard searches in the first draft, 3 in the fact-check pass), plus the Marble teaching-research records read locally. No page was read in full. A reviewer with ordinary web access should open the key pages before build, especially the van Helmont translation and the apple pollination pages.

No primary source, quotation, diary or eyewitness voice is invented. The single quoted word "restore" for Priestley is the conventional term used by the sources, presented as such. The orchard, the orchard keeper, her trees, *Lantern Red* and *Brook Gold*, the bean-jar timings, the 2 cm per hour dye rate, the blossom counts and all characters are fiction or made-up models, labeled "A story from Willowbank" or "A made-up model."
