## Primary teaching references and limited claims

Directly opened [CCSS3.MD.A.1](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/MD/A/1/): it includes minute intervals and addition/subtraction word problems represented on a number line. That supports the clock-reading/interval contrast and representation choice; the proposed five-minute/one-hour subset does not deliver the whole standard or imply grade placement.

Directly opened [NCETM Year3 Unit11: Time](https://www.ncetm.org.uk/classroom-resources/cp-year-3-unit-11-time/), especially digital-minute/hour coupling and duration guidance. Minute digits reset at the next hour while an interval continues; analogue and digital representations should be related. The page includes much broader notation/vocabulary/calendar work than this pack and supplies no reviewed learner-efficacy result for our design. No NCETM worksheet/prose/art/code is incorporated.

The hour hand must move between numerals. On a720minute face the hour angle is m/2degrees and minute angle6(m%60). An elapsed line is not a12hour clock face: it stays monotone0→60even while face labels wrap at12. A full minute-hand turn advances one hour and means60elapsed minutes, not zero. Counting a model interval is not measuring physical time; a later reading of the same label can already be familiar.
