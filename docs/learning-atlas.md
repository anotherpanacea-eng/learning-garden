## What the graph means

The original edge points from a topic to a prerequisite: `topicId` depends on `prerequisiteId`. Hard/soft source edges and their reasons are retained. An app invitation, a candidate pattern, or a route between games is a different relation; none is added to the source prerequisite graph. Upstream age ranges and age suffixes remain reference metadata, never placement or access rules. The taxonomy is a useful map, not a validated assessment or a complete model of human learning. Some evidence entries are empty or bibliographic rather than usable child-response criteria; authoring must examine them.

There are 144 explicit current references across 77 unique source topics: authored question references, scoped practice or supplied explanations. The other 1513 topics are **not yet mapped**. These counts describe our declarations, not topic coverage, learning gains or mastery; a live game can contain useful material not yet assigned a topic ID. Background prerequisites in the small map do not count as teaching.

## Seven patterns, many purposes

| Pattern | Reusable action | Current foothold | A possible spiral |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Search · inspect · compare | Choose where to look, reveal a clue, compare conditions, explain a useful next move. | Living Garden, Cloud Orchard | Habitat need → changed condition → survey/classification → supporting field note. |
| Assemble · allocate · check | Meet a request with limited materials; count, share, substitute and revise. | Almost-Right Café, Bramble | Equal plates → fractions of a recipe → measured ingredients → prices/change/budgets. |
| Change · measure · explain | Predict, change one thing, run or observe, compare records, explain the limits. | Storm Workshop, Water Lab explanations | Model distance → repeated trials/graphs → water/soil study → energy/sound/light. |
| Read · infer · support | Read or hear a meaningful message; find the clue, infer a purpose, identify supporting words. | English Paris, French Paris, Living Garden notes | Explicit note → sequence → inference → compare accounts → evaluate conflicting sources. |
| Compose · execute · revise | Make a message or sequence, let a reader/runner respond literally, revise for the intended effect. | Willow Post Office | Word-card directions → branching instructions → short messages → longer authored explanations. |
| Build · transform · relate | Make an object/model, transform it, connect its representation to a relation or explanation. | Number Garden models, Multiplication Garden, Bramble decomposition, Word Garden | Counters/bundles → arrays/inverse relations → fractions/area → algebraic structures. |
| Create · perform · reflect | Make something expressive, try or share it, notice its effect, choose a revision. | Story Loft puppet theatre | Rhythm/shape/voice experiment → a composition → explain or perform a revision. |

Each reuse needs a real purpose, reviewed content, suitable representations, meaningful response modes and a new-context question. It is not enough to replace nouns in a text quiz. Support-at-response, counting/deriving, retries, familiarity and unobserved abilities stay distinct. A choice question, spoken discussion, manipulation, composition, physical experiment and creative performance offer different evidence.

- **Search · inspect · compare:** Supplied pictures are not field identification or independent scientific observation.
- **Assemble · allocate · check:** Matching pretend food preferences does not assess empathy, nutrition or financial judgment.
- **Change · measure · explain:** Water Lab includes authored explanations and a manually stepped invented closed water-path machine. Models are not physical measurements; real soil, rainfall rates and runoff measurements remain absent.
- **Read · infer · support:** Comprehension selections, narration and additional-language reading do not establish independent decoding or first-language reading level.
- **Compose · execute · revise:** Finite tap-to-compose clauses are distinct from free writing, handwriting and general programming.
- **Build · transform · relate:** A successful answer does not establish recall, fluent calculation or use of an unreported strategy.
- **Create · perform · reflect:** Story Loft supports open puppet composition, performance and revision. Reviewed sound/phoneme instruction is not built. Artistic preference, authored words and personal reflection are not numeric attainment scores.
