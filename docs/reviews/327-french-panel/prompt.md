# Spec 327 French panel: prompt

This pass follows [117's language review method](../../specs/117-spanish-river-market.md#language-review-method--owner-decision-2026-10-30), which applies to French content too. Three providers each review the complete [327 companion](../../content/327-french-ferry-crossing-text.md) at the commit named in their report, separately and without seeing each other's reports. Each reviewer gets this prompt and the companion text in full.

---

You are reviewing the French in a children's learning game. The text is finite and written for beginners who read English well, roughly ages 7 to 12. The scene is an unnamed small river ferry in France. Madame Roux, the ferry keeper, asks the child and an adult companion (Dot) a few questions before they board, so she says *vous*. The child hears her questions through the device's French speech voice, picks one of six short answer cards, and says the answer out loud. Then the child asks her one question and listens for a number in her reply. Every French line has its English beside it.

Report each of these, with the exact line, the kind of problem, the exact fix and your confidence (high, medium or low):

1. Grammar, spelling, accents, punctuation (including French spacing before ? and !) and agreement errors.
2. Wording that is correct but unnatural, overly formal or regional for everyday France. Say whether *Combien êtes-vous ?* and *Nous sommes deux.* are natural at a ferry, or whether *Vous êtes combien ?* / *On est deux.* would be better for children.
3. English glosses that mislead.
4. The two teaching notes. Is the *Où* / *D'où* note accurate (answers starting with *À* and *De*)? Is the "Listen for the vowel" note about *deux / dix / douze* and *trois / treize / trente*, linked before *heures* and *euros*, accurate and useful?
5. Beginner load, and any answer giveaway in the six-card design or in the feedback lines.
6. Anything in the French that a speech synthesizer is likely to read wrongly (for example liaisons, *s'il vous plaît*, or the question marks).

End with an overall verdict: usable as written, usable with the listed fixes, or not usable.
