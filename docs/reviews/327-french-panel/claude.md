# Spec 327 French: Claude

Reviewer: Claude (Anthropic), Opus 5.5, in a cloud Claude Code session, 2026-10-31. Written before the other reports came back, and committed with the prompt before the other runs. The companion's sounds note was rewritten during authoring after the problem in item 4 below was found. This report reviews the revised text.

1. **Grammar and spelling.** No errors found. French spacing before ? and ! is present throughout. *À quelle heure part le bac ?* uses noun-subject inversion after a question word, which is standard. *Montez, s'il vous plaît !* and *C'est parti !* are correct. Confidence: high.
2. **Register.** *Combien êtes-vous ?* and *Nous sommes deux.* are correct and slightly formal. At a real ferry a keeper might say *Vous êtes combien ?*, and a family would answer *On est deux.* For beginners, *nous sommes* is the form taught first and matches the written answer. Keep it, medium confidence. *Vous* is right: she speaks to the child and Dot together.
3. **Glosses.** Accurate. *La mairie* "the town hall" is the everyday term. Confidence: high.
4. **Teaching notes.** The *Où* / *D'où* note is accurate for these answers: *Où allez-vous ? À la boulangerie.* / *D'où venez-vous ? De la poste.* An earlier draft told the child to listen for the final consonant of *dix* ("s") and *douze* ("z"). That is wrong in the actual answer: before *heures*, *deux*, *dix* and *douze* all link with a "z" sound ([døzœʁ], [dizœʁ], [duzœʁ]). The vowel is what separates them. The revised note says so. The same holds for *trois / treize / trente euros*, where the vowels are [wa], [ɛ] and [ɑ̃]. Confidence: high.
5. **Load and giveaways.** Six cards per crossing, with one right-kind-wrong-detail card and one *À*/*De* swap, avoid an elimination giveaway on step 4. The feedback lines don't reveal the fitting card. Load is fine at four questions plus one. Confidence: medium-high.
6. **Speech synthesis.** Most French voices handle *dix heures* and *trois euros* with liaison. *Le bac* should read [bak]. The risk is low, but it is untested until a device check. Confidence: medium.

**Verdict: usable as written**, with item 2 left to the panel.
