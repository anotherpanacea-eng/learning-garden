You are one of several independent reviewers of the Spanish in a children's learning game. Other reviewers, from other model providers, are reviewing the same text separately. You will not see their answers, and they will not see yours.

**Setting.** A Spanish-speaking town, Vadoalto, sits up a Pyrenees valley on the Spanish side. Its traders bring a barge, *La Garza*, down a brook (*el arroyo*) to a floating market in an English-speaking town. The crew are Marisol, the captain, and Teo, the cook, plus a cat, Nube. The child reads a short Spanish note, then acts on it: unloading cargo, guiding Teo on a map, cooking a *tortilla de patatas*, reading a weather report, and writing a postcard from phrase tiles. Interface text is English. The Spanish should be Spain's Spanish, at beginner load: the present tense, *hay*, *va a* plus a verb, and friendly *tú* commands. The readers are beginners, from about age 7 to teens, who read well in English.

**The owner's deliberate choice.** Plural "you" is *ustedes* everywhere, including *¿Cuándo vuelven?* to the crew, though *vosotros* is the everyday plural in Spain. Say whether this is acceptable as written for beginners, or propose a short framing for the grown-up notes.

**Review every Spanish line**: each note in both lengths, every sentence and word gloss, task labels, every line from Marisol, Teo and Nuria, every nudge (including its quoted Spanish), every postcard tile and tile meaning, both replies, and the grammar and sound notes. For each problem, report:

1. **Where** (errand and section, with the exact line).
2. **Kind**: error (grammar, spelling, accents or punctuation in Spain's Spanish), unnatural or regionally wrong wording, a gloss that misleads, too much load for a beginner, an answer giveaway, or a mismatch between a nudge's quotation and the note.
3. **Fix**: the exact replacement wording, or "drop the line".
4. **Confidence**: high, medium or low.

Then answer the seven "Questions for the fluent reader" at the end of the file, one line each. Then answer spec 117's review ask: is the longer note's load right for a beginner who reads well in English?

End with a one-line overall verdict: **usable as written**, **usable with the listed fixes**, or **needs rework**. Report only real problems; do not rewrite lines that are already correct. Do not reply in Spanish except when quoting or proposing wording.
