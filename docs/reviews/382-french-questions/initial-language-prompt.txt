This is the complete finite French and English teaching copy. Interface actions and safety messages below are English. No generated language, external runtime model, recording, or microphone. Standard beginner French; local browser French speech is optional, with no English fallback.

## Arrival and teaching

- Title: **Which parcel?**
- Story: A visitor is collecting supplies. Four parcels are waiting in two rooms. Find the visitor's parcel by asking questions.
- Instruction: **Ask a question. Read the reply. Choose a parcel when you want to.**
- Teaching: The visitor's note tells you who the parcel is for. A colour question and a room question can help you tell the parcels apart. A question about the recipient repeats something you already know. You can ask any question, in any order, as often as you like.
- Worked example, separate from every case: You want a green parcel in the bedroom. Knowing only “green” would not distinguish two green parcels. Knowing the room as well can help.
- Example French: **Le colis est vert. Il est dans la chambre.**
- Example meaning: **The parcel is green. It is in the bedroom.**
- Qualification: This is supported reading and question practice. Selecting supplied questions is not composing French or demonstrating spoken comprehension.

## Questions · all produce replies, none is graded

| ID | French | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| colour | **De quelle couleur est le colis ?** | What colour is the parcel? |
| room | **Dans quelle pièce est le colis ?** | Which room is the parcel in? |
| recipient | **Pour qui est le colis ?** | Who is the parcel for? |

“Who” here includes the people at a destination, such as a café. The source reply uses the destination, never an invented named person.
