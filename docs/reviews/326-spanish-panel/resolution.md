## *junco* in the River Market text

All three reviewers said *junco* (the rush) should become *carrizo* in the 117 text, both for the right plant and so the two texts don't teach two words for one plant. All three named *jardín* as the J example. The fix lands with spec 324 in PR #294 and is recorded in the [117 resolution](../117-spanish-panel/resolution.md). OpenAI also said "breathy" can suggest an English H. The sound note now calls *J* "a raspy sound made at the back of the mouth", which fits northern Spain. Google kept the old wording without marking it as an error.

## Known limit

As in the 117 panel, a model panel can miss wording that is correct but sounds odd to a native speaker. That risk is accepted for two short beginner phrases.
