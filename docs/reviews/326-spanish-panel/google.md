### Part 2. *junco* in the earlier River Market text

1. **Is *junco* (rush, *Juncus*) the wrong word for a reed used for baskets and roofs?**
   - **Yes (high confidence).** In Spain, *junco* refers to **rushes** (*Juncus* spp. and related club-rushes), not true reeds. While *junco* stems are thin, flexible, and widely used in Spanish basketry (*cestería*) and mats, they are typically much shorter (0.5–1.5 m) than a reed "taller than she is," lack the rigid hollow culms of roofing reed (*carrizo*), and translate accurately into English as "rush," not "reed."
