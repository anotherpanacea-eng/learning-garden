## Production findings that remain open

1. **Recognizer policy is not yet executable.** Claude and Google rated the literal mismatch rule P1; OpenAI rated its underspecified asset-kind expectations P2. Whole words, segmented demonstrations, isolated sounds and full narrations need different comparison targets. Recognizer errors and notation differences cannot be confused with established linguistic errors. The panel's proposed relaxation to human-only acceptance would change the owner's amendment and is **not adopted** here. Before production, specify the recognizer, expected labels/content per kind, permitted notation normalization and an honest failure/hold path. A genuine mismatch still rejects under the existing owner instruction; neither a screen pass nor this panel approves a take.
2. **Resolve h and stop performance directions on real takes.** Withholding h is already allowed. Contextual alternatives must be labeled as contextual and cannot silently become a one-phoneme sample. Natural-word endings and isolated demonstrations must not inherit one blanket release requirement.
3. **Compare the chosen voices in context.** Google proposes the same human for all single words or an A/B comparison against blends. A shared timbre is a suggestion, not a new requirement. The relevant gate is intelligible, coherent target pronunciation, followed by the owner's actual speaker/headphone listening. No voice cloning is proposed.
4. **Do not promote panel overstatements into phonics rules.** Google's categorical claims about mandatory pre-nasal vowel raising and required final releases, and broad proposed allophone equivalence lists, have not been independently established here. Variation is not automatically a changed phoneme; a normalization list must be reviewed for its actual context before it can admit an asset.

No linguistic majority vote can supply missing recordings, rights or owner listening. The narrower written inventory can inform a separately reviewed, explicitly adult-read design under the roadmap's text-first lane. That design must preserve the pending audio boundary and receive its own specification and implementation review.
