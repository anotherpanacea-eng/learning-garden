**Authoring map · 2026-10-20 · proposed teaching routes, open entry.**

This is the planning layer between the [topic atlas](learning-atlas.md) and playable lessons. It says what idea to develop, what contrast makes the next encounter useful, where it belongs, and how to revisit it. It covers priority foundations and nearby extensions across the source subjects, plus geography, arts and French. It is not an assignment of every source topic, a complete school curriculum, or a developmental placement sequence. The [all-topic index](../curriculum/topic-coverage.csv) retains the larger inventory and its unassigned topics.

## Read the statuses literally

| Status | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |
| **Built** | A specific runnable task, model or clearly labeled discussion opportunity exists. The evidence links below each strand delimit that foothold. It does not mean the whole concept/topic is taught, practiced comprehensively or mastered. |
| **Planned** | An existing written proposal specifies a named home and intended experience; the linked scope may still be an outline or require content review. It is not implemented or automatically build-ready. |
| **Missing** | No runnable or bounded planned sequence was found for this step. Its candidate home and revisit are proposals in this map, awaiting a lesson/content specification. |

A missing step describes the **product**, not the learner. A Built task can still need a better progression. A Planned idea may fit a child now; no score or prerequisite locks its door. Rows show useful authoring directions, not compulsory levels or a promise that one row must precede another for everyone.

Each revisit below is **proposed**, except the existing numerical *Carry an idea here* invitations among Living Garden, Café and Bramble. Future connections must preserve the relevant quantity/model, source text or observation, plus its support/familiarity history; the receiving activity asks its own question. They must not copy a success label or a global level.

## Is this enough games?

**Enough existing loops to deepen the next few strands; not enough modes to meet every learning need.** Game count is not the criterion. A useful home needs actions and representations that let the learner make the relevant distinction. We should add a mode when the action changes and a new destination when the setting/purpose becomes clearer there. Avoid making every subject another version of a text quiz.

| Candidate | Decision | Small first experience / need it meets | Priority and condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Café/Bramble equal-groups workbench | **Extend existing games** | Keep one quantity model, switch among total/group count/group size; reversible distribution and grouped moves | **First**: bounded increment 1 already planned; first use small quantities |
| Message reader / puppet response | **Extend Post Office and Loft** | Repair a message for a reader; compare before/after response; human reading of original text stays distinct from literal execution | **Next**: bounded increment 2; preserve the source and draft |

| Courier Map Room | **New mode in Post Office** | Make/interpret a small map with legend/reference direction; exchange a route and revise it | **Later**: map-making is an outline; scale and programmable branches each need scoped content, not one giant navigation game |

This gives us two strong near-term additions to familiar games, one notebook mode, and a distinct literacy door before proliferating worlds. The Archive and Studio become useful when their new ways of acting are ready.

## Three concrete routes to author first

1. **Prepare a community dinner:** Number Garden array (**Built**) → Café plates (**Built**) → switch among the same triple’s unknowns (**Planned**, increment 1) → Bramble fresh delivery question (**Planned connection**) → fraction of a differently sized batch (**Planned outline**, then Missing equivalence/measurement lessons). Start with known quantities and change one demand at a time.
2. **Make a message work:** Paris inference/evidence (**Built**) → Post Office literal route/clarification (**Built**) → reader asks a specific question (**Planned**, increment 2) → revise a Loft line (**Built opportunity**, connection Planned) → a second narrator’s account (**Planned**, spec 06). Longer writing is optional; compare the intended and observed effects.
3. **Choose where to plant:** Living Garden need/condition/change (**Built**) → dated plant comparison (**Planned**, increment 3) → useful learner-made table (**Planned first slice**) → construct a graph from selected observations (**Missing**) → explain the planting choice to a gardener (**Planned connection**). Keep model and physical evidence separate.

For an authored revisit, hold at least one thing fixed (quantity, text, organism, claim) and deliberately change one demand (unknown, representation, condition, audience). Help may offer a smaller step or a different example; it does not lower a global level. Offer a fresh instance after familiarity and retain each task’s own support.

## What remains outside this first map

The atlas still contains unsequenced objectives throughout its 54 domains: broader body/life processes, oceans and other ecosystems, geology/palaeontology, Earth/space, climate, advanced mathematics, wider historical periods, financial and personal/social topics. These remain **Missing** at the relevant lesson/sequence level unless the narrow Built or Planned rows above say otherwise. Prioritize a bounded purpose/content pack instead of trying to build one game per source node or claim all objectives from a familiar activity shell.

For a future build, select one row and specify the example/contrast, learner action, representation, response, help, source material and revisit. Independently review that small specification and its content before implementation. Add atlas references only for actual delivered scope; this map itself changes no runtime mappings, assessment, saved progress or recommendations.
