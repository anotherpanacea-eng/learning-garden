# 342 · Sound-changing prop labels: finite text

Proposed original adult-led content. No recorded phonics, synthetic target speech, child recording or automatic oral judgment. All child text below is authored for this room, not copied dictionary prose. General American is the adult reference for written review; adults respect other varieties.

## Story, instructions and teaching

- Title: **Labels for the show**
- Story: **Dot found a cap, a folding cot and a pretend map for the library show. Their labels got wet. Can you and your reading partner make new ones?**
- Entry: **Try a sound-changing label**
- Chooser: **Choose a label game. You can try any one.**
- Modes: **Listen and build**; **Look and read**
- Setup: **Pass this to your reading partner. They will say a word for you.**
- Partner control: **Show partner card**; **Hide card and build**
- Work instruction: **Listen to your partner. Choose a place, then tap a letter. You can change your idea.**
- Read instruction: **Try this word with your partner. Then uncover its picture.**
- Standing reminder: **Your partner can help. Keeping a label does not tell the game how you read it.**
- Teaching title: **Three sound places**
- Teaching steps: **Say mat together. Point to its three letters as your partner models the sounds.** / **Now say sat. Listen to the start.** / **The first sound changed. The middle and last stayed the same. These words have three letters for three sounds. Other words can work differently.**
- Grown-up note: **Say each whole word naturally. Help the child listen to and blend its three sounds, without replacing sounds with letter names. No app recording is provided. A letter place is a visual aid, not a measured sound boundary. We keep material and help, not oral reading results.**

## Controls and feedback

- Letter places: **First place**, **Middle place**, **Last place**, each with the literal current letter or **empty**.
- Controls: **Clear this place**, **Clear label**, **Show word**, **Show picture**, **Compare my label**, **Keep my label**, **Change one sound**, **Show an example**, **Previous step**, **Next step**, **Back to my label**, **Build this label**, **Try another way**, **Choose another game**, **Back to prop labels**, **Read the show message**, **Grown-up notes**.
- Incomplete comparison: **Some places are empty. You can keep this idea or add letters.**
- Different comparison: **Your label and our card are different. Compare them with your partner, or keep your idea.**
- Matching comparison: **Your letters match our card. Say the word together if you want.**
- Keep: **Your label is kept for the show. You can visit it again or try another idea.**
- Keeps: **First kept label**, **Latest kept label**, **Use these kept letters**. Empty places remain marked, not silently filled.
- Collection acknowledgment: **Our labels are ready to revisit. What shall we read together?**
- Partial/unreadable material: **An earlier label cannot open here. Your other labels are still waiting. You can keep a copy in Grown-up notes.**
- Capacity: **There is not enough room to keep that change. Your label is still here. Download Grown-up notes before starting a fresh box.**
- Notes summary: **Sound-changing prop labels. These are arrangements and disclosed help, not oral reading or spelling results.** Follow with readable case titles, literal slots and first/latest/help distinctions; do not emit learner levels.

## Shared show message

Title: **Dot’s note for the show**

**A cat sat on a mat. A cap is on a cot. A map is in a tin.**

Target tokens, in exact occurrence order: **cat, sat, mat, cap, cot, map, tin**. Every other word token is **adult-supplied**, including both occurrences of **on**, **is**, **in** and all **A/a** tokens. Punctuation/spaces stay unchanged. Marking a word as a target labels this workbench’s material; it does not assert the child learned or read it. The adult can supply any token, including targets.

Oral prompt: **Which label would help someone find the pretend map? Tell your partner why.** No choices, stored response or answer key. Optional invitation: **Make another scene in Story Loft**. It transfers no notebook material.
