# 605 · Act V project page and ending: finite text

Companion to [spec 605](../specs/605-act-five-projects.md). It lists every child-facing string in `dist/act-five-content.mjs` added by 605 that is not copied unchanged from the reviewed [act-5.md](120-story/act-5.md). Copied unchanged from the script are:
- each path's **Show** line;
- each path's three **limit cards (Sprout)**, stored as codes;
- the step guide lines;
- the ending's Short and Longer text, its four season lines, the almanac line and the between-acts line.

`{guide}` is the chosen guide. In the almanac line, `{project}` is the path title and `{limit}` is the chosen card without its final period.
