# 316 · Original seed-exchange invitations and finite text

Companion to [316](../specs/316-seed-exchange-table.md). New English fiction/model text, not a translation or a real transaction. Numeric placeholders always carry the stated unit. These are the defaults for new format1cards, with the three/eight-part numbers from the commissioned current rules. All variants stay with their frozen card; older readable cards retain their own rule, part counts, prose and example rather than borrowing these current defaults. No sentence names the later Ways, Hollis or Page3.

## Entrance

**Title:** The seed exchange table

**Invitation:** The orchard keeper needs a clear card for the courier. Willowbank and Vadoalto are exchanging goods and young plants. Build a trade or share the seedlings, then keep a note for the journey.

**First preview:** One full trade group sends3baskets of apples and brings back2bundles of reed. These are the two towns' agreed amounts.

**Always-visible model boundary:** A made-up exchange. The pictures and numbers are a model, not real stock or a measured harvest.

**Family choices:** Apples for reed · Reed for apples · Share the seedlings

**Size label:** Groups or seedlings in each part ·1–12

**Chooser explanation:** Choose any card. Earlier cards stay in your notes. A familiar card can come round again.

**Grown-up qualification:** These are supported constructions with visible quantity readouts. Optional help is recorded separately. A fitting model does not establish independent calculation, reading, writing or a level. Original courier notes are human-read. Local suggestions use a few first checks from distinct cards; all choices stay open.

**Rule:**5parts for Vadoalto and3parts for Willowbank:8equal parts altogether

## Courier card and common controls

**Refusal:** We couldn't keep this change. Your earlier work is still here. Your words remain in the box.

**Unknown format:** This saved card is kept unchanged. Other readable cards and the rest of the town are open. Download your notes.

Cap/counter/collision/unsafe-root reasons must name the concrete blocked action and Notes/removal option, never imply a reset or a successful save. No applause claiming goods shipped, plants grew, a plan is scientifically valid, punctuation is correct or a course is finished.
