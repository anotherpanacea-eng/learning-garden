## Chapter 2: the three Ways (the Sprout job, played here)

The three Ways are taken from Dot's words in the chapter text:

- **Map description:** The post office map on the café table, held flat by honey jars. Three Ways run from Willowbank to Vadoalto: the river path beside the brook, the bridge road in a long loop, and the high ford straight across the water.
- **Map caption:** Three Ways to Vadoalto.
- **Puzzle heading:** Which Way?
- **The river path:** Fastest, but it floods.
- **The bridge road:** Slow, but always dry.
- **The high ford:** Short, and only safe in summer.
- **Choosing the river path:** The river path is fastest, but it floods. A parcel that must stay dry would rather be slow.
- **Choosing the high ford:** The high ford is short, but it means wet feet. A parcel that must stay dry would rather be slow.
- **Kept, the whole plan:** Your exchange plan is kept.
- **Kept, the smaller plan:** Your smaller exchange plan is kept: one Way, once a week.
