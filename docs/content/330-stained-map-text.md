## Adult Notes wording

**Line:** The Stained Map keeps supplied spelling work, including unfinished versions. Written notes and offered choices make these supported activities; a fit is not evidence of independent spelling or historical knowledge.

**Details:** Earlier checks and first/latest kept work belong to their own saved words and choices. Help flags describe later hint or example requests. Local narration records no listening or spoken response. A grown-up can read the ordinary words together. This activity records no pronunciation, microphone input, age or learner name. A word-family clue does not mean every related word pronounces the letter.
