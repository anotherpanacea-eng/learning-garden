## How to read a scene

Each scene has up to three reading lengths, per spec 120's writing rules:

- **Short:** 30 words at most. Read-aloud available.
- **Longer:** 150 words at most.
- **Keeper's note:** 80 words at most, for the deepest reading. Not every scene has one.

`{guide}` is the chosen companion. `{courier}` is the explorer's courier. The explorer is "you".

## How to read a job

```
**Sprout · [Activity]** Who asks: "The request."
Kept: "What someone says when the work is kept."
```

- **Sprout, Seedling, Sapling** describe the task's depth, never the child. Any of them is a whole contribution to the chapter.
- **[Activity]** is the existing activity the job opens. The story supplies the request and the closing line; the activity supplies its own questions, hints and feedback.
- **[new]** marks a job whose activity does not exist yet. Each is listed below.
- **Keeper's Desk** jobs belong to spec 118 and the older-learner thread. Each chapter names the desk case that fits it, or a case idea where none is written yet.

## Review needs

- **Spanish and French:** every line in [letters.md](letters.md), the Spanish lines in Act III and Act V (the travelling show), and the French warning card in Act VI need a fluent reader. The Spanish uses *ustedes*. letters.md carries specific questions for the reader, including how French should handle Hollis (they/them) and a courier who is she or they.
- **Facts:** lines marked *(check)* need a source before build: weather and climate (Act II), rain soaking in or running off (Act I), reeds slowing water (Act III), the Paris 1910, Valencia and Dutch claims (Act IV), a real night-opening flower and its visitors, and Moon phases (Act V). Each should match its Marble topic. The fiction (Willowbank, the Sponge, Vadoalto, Les Saules, Hollis's ledger) needs no fact check, but its numbers must agree with page 2 and Nuria's rain table.
- **Consistency:** names and pronouns follow spec 120's cast. The miller is she, the baker he, the librarian they, the orchard keeper she.
