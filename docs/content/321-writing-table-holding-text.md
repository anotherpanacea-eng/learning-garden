## Tap tasks

**1. One parcel, four words.** Prompt (277): **Tap every word that means the parcel.** Draft (277), with tappable words in bold:

> **Pip** found a **parcel** by the **bridge**. The **package** was wet. **He** carried **it** to the **bakery**, and the **baker** dried the **bundle** on the **windowsill**.

Key: parcel, package, it, bundle. Fit (277): **Four words, one parcel. The reader never loses it.** Feedback for a tapped word that is not the parcel:
- Pip: **Pip is the one who found it.**
- bridge: **The bridge is where Pip found it.**
- He: **"He" points back to Pip.**
- bakery: **The bakery is where Pip took it.**
- baker: **The baker is the one who dried it.**
- windowsill: **The windowsill is where it dried.**

Missing line: **Some words that mean the parcel are not tapped yet.**
Hint: **Find the parcel. Then look for other words that mean the same thing.**

**2. Which word is unclear?** Prompt: **Tap the word the reader could mix up.** Draft (277), tappable in bold:

> **Pip** set the **bun** on the **plate**. Then **it** slid off the **table**.

Key: it. Fit: **"It" could be the bun or the plate. That's the word to fix.** Feedback:
- Pip: **Pip is named. There is only one Pip.**
- bun: **The bun is named. Look for a word that points back.**
- plate: **The plate is named. Look for a word that points back.**
- table: **The table is named. Nothing else could be the table.**

Missing line: **The unclear word is not tapped yet.**

Hint: **Which word points back to something already named?**

## Help

Hints are each task's own. **Try a separate example** uses 277's "Holding items" line for tasks 1 to 3 and C1, with one commission correction: its middle sentence "Only one thing can be red." is replaced by **"It" points back to the hat.**, because Dot could be red too. The line reads: **Try this one first: "Dot has a hat. It is red." "It" points back to the hat. Now check yours.** Commission additions for the others:
- Joins (tasks 4 to 6): **Try this one first: "Dot was tired. As a consequence, she went to bed early." She went to bed early because she was tired. Now read your two paragraphs.**
- Shorter versions (tasks 7, 8): **Try this one first: "Pip can whistle, and Dot can too." The reader fills in "whistle." Now check yours.**
