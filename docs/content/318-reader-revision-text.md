# 318 · Finite reader cards

Original English copy for [spec318](../specs/318-reader-revision.md). Authoring proposal, not deployed content. All three are authored questions to try; none has read or evaluated the learner's draft.

**Invitation:** Wren wants a note the town can question. Keep a picture of this draft, try a reader's question, then decide what you want to change.

**Intro label:** Questions to try, not a score for your writing.

| ID | Name | Role | Question | Return prompt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| costs | The orchard keeper | Someone who may bear a cost | Who would give up something under your proposal? What would you want them to know before asking for their support? | Look at your reason and your answer to an objection. You may acknowledge a cost and still keep a provisional preference. |
| evidence | The librarian | A reader checking the evidence | Which record supports your reason? What can that record tell us, and what can't it settle? | Look at your evidence and uncertainty. A useful fact can support part of a case without proving the whole proposal will work. |
| next | Wren | A reader who needs a next step | What should the town do or find out next? Which answer could make you change your recommendation? | You can name an investigation, a question or a provisional action. Say what it would help you decide. |

**Controls:** My note; Reader's question; Compare versions; Try a reader's question; Return to my note; Keep this revision; Hear this question; Hear these versions.

**Before title:** Before the reader's question.

**After title:** Latest kept revision.

**No after:** Your earlier draft is safe. Return to your note, then keep a revision whenever you want. An unfinished or unchanged note is welcome.

**Keep reply:** Your revision is kept beside the earlier draft. Keeping it doesn't say the argument is finished.

**Closing / human invitation:** Ask someone to read both versions. What is clearer? Which question is still open? You can keep your choice, change it, or leave it undecided.

**Grown-up limit:** These fixed reader questions are made available when requested. They are not generated from or matched to the child's writing, and no algorithm grades the prose. Raw drafts, supplied starters, requested support and exact versions remain separate in the local notebook.
