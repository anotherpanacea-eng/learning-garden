## Packet and teaching

- Entry/title: **Garden-stage labels**.
- Story: **Dot is helping the puppets put on a garden show. Make labels and read their little note together.**
- Teaching button: **Meet letter sounds**.
- Teaching introduction: **Read this card with your partner. Your partner can say the sounds. You can point, join in, or listen.**
- Back: **Back to my labels**.
- Abstract case titles, in order: **First sound 1**, **First sound 2**, **First sound 3**, **First sound 4**, **Middle sound**, **Last sound**.

Four direct letter buttons, r/l/w/y, open these contextual cards, with the relevant first position marked. An adult supplies the sound; the child may point, join in or listen.

| Glyph | Context word | Position | Exact adult guidance |

## Shared stage note

Title: **Dot's garden-show note**.

> Put the yam on the mat. The ram has a red rod. The cup is wet.

Prompt: **What does the ram have? Read together, then show or tell your partner.**

Target lexical tokens, exact lowercase occurrences: `yam`, `mat`, `ram`, `red`, `rod`, `cup`, `wet`. All other lexical tokens, including `Put`, `the`, `on`, `The`, `has`, `a` and `is`, are adult-supplied. Preserve punctuation/spacing outside tokens. Uppercase The is not a letter-recognition result. The adult explicitly supplies Put; its vowel is not the short-u model in cup. Every target gets a word source; this note is shared reading, not an independently decodable text.
