# Presentation styles: draft frame copy

Companion to [spec 244](../specs/244-presentation-styles.md). **Draft for review, not build-ready copy.** Every Storybook line below is the string shipped on main on 2026-10-26, quoted so each variant has its exact twin; Storybook copy is not changed here. Field Journal lines are first drafts for the CR1 comparison (spec 244 phase 1) and need independent content review and the Push C child-screen check before use. Mystery & Adventure lines are concept samples only; they are not commissioned (spec 244 §4.3).

Rules every line follows (spec 244 §3): frame layer only; any goal, quantity or action in the Storybook line survives in the variant; control labels come from the shared glossary and do not vary; second person, US spelling, no em-dashes, 30 words at most.

## 2. Home parcel area (phase 1: no copy changes)

The parcel scene, its title, **Keep it safe**, the reminder and every Keeper's Pages string are spec 120 §16.1 story text and stay identical in every style. In Field Journal only the chrome changes: the scene sits on a notebook page held by two strips of tape, the parcel art gains a paper luggage tag, and the panel border becomes a ruled margin. No new label is added to the art, so nothing on screen competes with the story text.

## 3. One café turn

Guest arrival and thank-you lines are frame copy. The guest, their wish, ingredient properties and every number stay as they are.

| Guest | Storybook (shipped) | Field Journal (draft) | Mystery & Adventure (concept) |

Notes for review:

- Field Journal keeps each guest's joke in a quieter key rather than deleting it (spec 244 §4.2: quieter humor, warm, never gray).
- The Mystery column shows the risk named in spec 244 §4.3: even a light "who comes up the road" tone suggests danger or a secret. Any real Mystery copy needs narrative review against spec 120 §11 so it never implies a threat or a villain.
