Title: Copy Frame

The workshop is making new sign panels. Can a panel change size and keep its shape?

## Grown-up disclosure

This open rectangle model connects matching-side scale factors to area factors, including a smaller and a turned copy. Shapes, lengths and sign panels are invented. The grid uses abstract units, not device centimeters or physical measurements. Unit squares, a length-unit segment and original-sized example panels have different jobs. Simplified factors and interpretations are supplied support. Constructing a match is not independent explanation, fluent ratio arithmetic, readiness or mastery. No lesson here covers all polygons, centre-of-enlargement constructions, map-unit conversion, standard-unit conversion or every geometry method. Work is temporary and creates no new Notes or assessment record. All cases and support remain open.
