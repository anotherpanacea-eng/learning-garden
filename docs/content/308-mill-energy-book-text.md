## Expandable explanations

**About this paused model**

- **Energy is a quantity we can account for, not a material made of little chips. The chips stand for equal amounts; no joule value is chosen here. We track one batch's extra energy, not all the energy already in the room or Earth.**
- **The gravitational store belongs to the high water and Earth. The wheel has a kinetic store while it moves. Grinding and rubbing add to thermal stores in the stones, flour, axle and nearby surroundings. The two warmth tags say when energy was transferred; they are not two new kinds of energy.**
- **Each tap advances one model step. Arrows show motion in a paused picture; drawn movement does not measure speed, force or time. Opening the gate only allows the next portion to be poured. The picture does not model continuous flow.**
- **Pouring is idealized. Real mills also spread energy out while water arrives and flows away. During grinding there is rubbing too; the grinding tag counts the whole tracked thermal increment during that step. It does not measure useful output or efficiency. Real grinding has other small effects, such as making new grain surfaces; our simplified book counts these small effects in with the warming.**
- **Grinding warms flour; it does not supply flour's chemical food energy. That came from sunlight captured by the growing plant. Resetting starts a different tracked batch in the picture; it does not erase earlier thermal energy in the world.**

**Why can the wheel keep going?**

- **Closing the gate stops the next pour; it does not remove energy already in the moving wheel. Grinding or rubbing transfers some out of its kinetic store. When that store is empty in the model, the wheel stops.**
- **A force is a push or pull. The wheel's kinetic energy is not a stored push. As the water falls and pushes the wheel, energy is transferred by mechanical working. Rubbing forces also transfer energy while surfaces move, warming the mill.**
- **Thermal stores still hold energy after the wheel stops. That spread-out warmth does not set the wheel turning again by itself.**

**Grown-ups: sources and limits**

**Conservation alone does not rule out ideal lossless motion. This limited model does not test perpetual-motion claims.**
