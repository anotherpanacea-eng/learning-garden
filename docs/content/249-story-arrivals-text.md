Longer:

> A storm came through Willowbank. It knocked down the footbridge, jammed the mill wheel and scattered library books. The neighbors have been mending ever since, and they held a supper to welcome everyone back. There is still plenty to fix, if you'd like to help.

> Pip found a wet parcel addressed to the Keeper of the Ways. One page has dried: an old map with a marsh called the Sponge where the orchard is now. The parcel and the supper belong to the story before you came. The map is here for you to read.
