2. **{opener} Pip found the path to the orchard. He followed the blue signs.** First paragraph: Pip ran onto Willow Bridge; next is the far side that same afternoon.
3. **{opener} the stalls sell seedlings for the garden. You can ask the gardeners about them.** First paragraph gives the first reason, bread; this is the second reason.
4. **{opener} you can meet friends by the fountain. There is room to sit and talk.** First paragraph gives the third reason, music; this is the last reason.

## Feedback and keeping

Complete fitting draft: **This arrangement helps the reader follow the draft.** Incomplete: **Some parts are still waiting. You can keep this version or keep arranging.** Different: use each selected277 task's specific feedback, followed by **Your draft is still here. You can change it.** For three trays, name each mismatched strip's current and expected heading in an expandable explanation; do not automatically move it. Show the exact current report on every state, including unfinished drafts.

Keep: **This version is in your notebook.** Snapshot labels: **First kept version** · **Latest kept version** · **Not checked in this version** · **Checked before your last change** · **A draft you have seen**. Check label: **Practice with this draft**. No success star, percentage, test-level estimate or numbered completion gate.

Invitation: **Which part helps the visitor? Try a short report on paper, then ask someone to read it.** Next is always open. Returning: **Your draft is waiting.** Unknown saved task: **This earlier draft cannot be changed here. It is still in your Notes download. Try another draft.** Unknown notebook version: **This writing notebook needs another version of the game. It stays in your Notes download. Other places are open.** Full notebook: **There is no room for another draft in this writing notebook. Your earlier work is still here. Download your Notes.** No automatic eviction/reset offered.

Grown-ups: **This table uses drafts we wrote. All checks are practice with supplied material and teaching, not independent writing or reading assessments. Talk, point, read together or leave a draft unfinished. Your child's own report stays on paper or aloud, read by a person. First and latest kept versions show changes; checks keep the first and up to 23 recent checks per task, not every try. The table does not certify paragraph writing or unlock a story chapter.**
