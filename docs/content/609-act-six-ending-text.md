Companion to [spec 609](../specs/609-act-six-ending.md). It lists every child-facing string in `dist/act-six-content.mjs` under `ending` that is not copied unchanged from these reviewed sources:

- the [120 companion](120-keeper-of-the-ways-text.md)'s Page 6, *Hollis's letter*: its title and every line of the letter.

## Page 6

- Read Hollis's letter
- **Reveal** (the 120 companion says Hollis gives it to the explorer at supper): After supper, Hollis takes a folded letter from their coat and gives it to you.
- Go down to the bridge
- Reread Hollis's letter

## The ending

- **Scene description:** Three ribbon posts along the Ways: a blue ribbon on the river path, a gold one on the bridge road and a red one at the high ford. Bramble ties them one by one.
- **Caption:** A ribbon on each Way.
- Reread the ending
- This page opens when the Ways are open.
- Back to the garden

The ribbon colors are the script's: blue for the river path, gold for the bridge road, red for the high ford.
