# Out and About: the mission cards

Companion to [spec 128](../specs/128-out-and-about.md). Use as written, after the same independent review and a child-safety read of every card. Nothing on these cards is ever recorded. Lines marked `[check]` need a fact or safety check before build.

**Needs key:** 📄 paper and pencil · 🍳 kitchen · 🌳 outdoors · 🧑 a grown-up · 👥 someone to do it with
