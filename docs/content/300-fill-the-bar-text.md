## Fixed pattern text after Offer

These six supplied diagrams/labels and paragraphs are readable without playing or hearing anything. They are fixed text, not reconstructed from the timed lyric. They may sit in an accessible native disclosure titled **The song's patterns**, available as soon as the turn opens; opening a disclosure never changes support to “understood.” Read the whole bar as four quarter-note beats. The same text supplies each diagram's accessible description.

1. **One whole note:** One note takes all four beats. It fills this whole bar.
2. **Two half notes:** Each takes two beats, one half of this bar. Two sound beginnings.
3. **Four quarter notes:** Each takes one beat, one quarter of this bar. Four sound beginnings.
4. **Eight eighth notes:** Two in every beat. Each is one eighth of this bar. Eight sound beginnings.
5. **Three quarter notes, then a quarter rest:** The written bell part leaves one quiet beat. The rest takes one quarter of this bar, as long as a quarter note. Other parts can keep going, and the bell can ring out briefly.
6. **A half note, a quarter note, then two eighth notes:** Two beats, one beat, then half a beat and half a beat. Four beats in all.
