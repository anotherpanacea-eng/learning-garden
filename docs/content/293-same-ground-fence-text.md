## Separate worked support

Shortcut for an empty plot: There is no plot yet: no covered squares and no outside boundary. Lay a row first.

Shortcut for a nonempty plot: [h] rows of[w] squares gives[h×w] square units. The outside has[w]+[h]+[w]+[h] length units. These are two different things to count, even when the numbers happen to match.
