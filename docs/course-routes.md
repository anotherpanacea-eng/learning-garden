# Course routes: from activities to a coherent year

Planning baseline, 2026-10-25. This is an authored course framework, not a completed curriculum, a school placement, or an automatic learner profile. The [roadmap](roadmap.md) owns delivery priorities; the [26 pathways](six-act-strands.md) own the long horizon. These two optional routes organize a first year of that horizon. They can be mixed by subject, shortened, repeated or entered later. No child names, school reports, ages, account activity or private responses belong here.

## What a complete course will mean

The first target is a coherent year of mathematics, English, science and inquiry, history/geography, with an optional French course and arts/computing/community extensions. It is not every Marble topic, every proposed pack, or six story acts in a year. Six blocks below suggest roughly four to six weeks each, with space for holidays, reading, discussion and investigations; this is a planning envelope, not tested lesson duration or a delivery promise. Establish actual session counts after the first block is played. Off-screen work is part of the course design, not a missing game to disguise.

A course is ready to use when every included objective has a reviewed teaching sequence, a meaningful action, an explicit contrast, a later return in a different purpose, accessible help, and an appropriate opportunity for original work or human observation. It also needs a suggested session plan, materials and sources, saved-work behavior for digital tasks, and a clear endpoint. A short selection question, a supplied model, or a story ending alone cannot close an objective.

Each subject can start separately. A capable thinker may want concrete number support, mature English texts and short French sentences at the same time. Reading aloud is available without lowering the intellectual task. A conversation about comfortable examples can choose an entry; no timed test, inferred global ability or compulsory prerequisite gate is proposed.

**Status vocabulary:** **Built foothold** means a bounded runtime action exists; its limits remain. **Authored** means a proposal exists, not necessarily build-cleared. **Missing** means a teaching sequence or essential bridge still needs authoring. **Off-screen** means an explicitly planned human/material activity; it is not machine-verified. None means a learner has mastered a topic. Runtime changes listed here remain separate commissions.

**A1 first-block lesson order (candidate, not a runtime commission):** (1) notice and make equal groups; (2) arrange/rotate the same collection; (3) change the unknown at the Café and Bramble; (4) build a rectangle in one stated square unit; (5) compare equal-area shapes with different boundaries; (6) use the relationship in a planting plan. In parallel: choose a short whole text, discuss meaning with a detail, privately notice decoding needs, teach only selected reviewed spelling contrasts, write and test a note, revisit the pattern in another text. Add one supervised observation and one library/account-safety discussion. Each numbered teaching episode may span several visits; no six-click course claim.

**B1 first-block lesson order:** (1) compare two changing quantities with units; (2) make a table and ordered pairs; (3) compare equal horizontal changes with vertical changes; (4) connect slope to rate, including zero/negative examples; (5) contrast constant proportion with a starting fee; (6) compare two feasible crossings and explain an assumption. Offer a concrete ratio/coordinate bridge before any abstract step. Alongside: annotate a complete short text/excerpt in its whole-work context, test an interpretation, write/revise a sourced paragraph; teach net force and observation/claim contrasts before a collision design; teach/revisit French room/preposition meanings before writing directions. All remain proposals requiring bounded subject/model/content review.

## French course within either route

Do not equate sentence length with maturity or language attainment. Proposed function sequence: locate/describe a place → request/clarify → recount recent events → explain near-future intentions → compare and justify → correspond/present and respond. Revisit vocabulary and grammar across rooms, cafés, couriers and source visits. Teach sound–spelling/listening alongside reading; spelling, agreement, verb forms, negation and questions recur in purposeful writing. Each block includes comprehension, a meaningful action, an original utterance/text, a later encounter and a human conversation option. Fluent review, licensed/native-quality audio where used, and human evaluation of spontaneous production remain necessary. This is not a CEFR certification or a replacement for a language class.

## Story and audience are separate axes

The six acts remain participation → belonging → reciprocal exchange → evidence/disagreement → chosen inquiry → shared agency, with the actual names and closures owned by [spec 120](specs/120-keeper-of-the-ways.md). Course blocks can revisit a place during any act. A1 and B1 can both use the café, but do different mathematical/literary jobs. Finishing B6 does not fabricate story contributions; finishing the supper does not complete A1. Later-act arrivals remain unbuilt.

Choose [Storybook or Field Journal presentation](audience-presentation.md) separately from course route, reading help, French and guide/avatar. A simple mathematical model may have a mature presentation; a sophisticated investigation can retain playful guests.

## Completion audit and sizing checkpoint

Before labeling either course complete, reconcile each included unit to its concept/contrast/return, sources and actual activity/off-screen guide in the existing strand/topic records; mark remaining objectives honestly. The 1,590-row pinned ledger is an ownership inventory, not 1,590 finished lessons. Its generic placement gaps need unit-by-unit reconciliation; do not bulk mark them covered because this document exists. The runtime atlas's narrow counts are not course-completion percentages.

First review C0 and the first-block scopes; size and review C2/C3/C4/C7 as independent packets. After two representative digital builds and one human/off-screen sequence have been tried, estimate work per remaining block and publish an updated budget. Earlier estimates of days for planning and months for complete courses were provisional; no calendar promise follows from this file. Story, subject and media reviews remain distinct. A complete year needs sustained books, writing, discussion, investigations and language interaction as well as games. This is not a school-standards equivalence audit: before claiming alignment to a named course or jurisdiction, choose its actual standards, crosswalk the selected objectives, and resolve remaining gaps. The selected-unit science/arts surveys do not certify completion of every domain.
