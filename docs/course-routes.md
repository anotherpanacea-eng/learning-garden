# Course routes: from activities to a coherent year

Planning baseline, 2026-10-25. This is an authored course framework, not a completed curriculum, a school placement, or an automatic learner profile. The [roadmap](roadmap.md) owns delivery priorities; the [26 pathways](six-act-strands.md) own the long horizon. These two optional routes organize a first year of that horizon. They can be mixed by subject, shortened, repeated or entered later. No child names, school reports, ages, account activity or private responses belong here.

## What a complete course will mean

The first target is a coherent year of mathematics, English, science and inquiry, history/geography, with an optional French course and arts/computing/community extensions. It is not every Marble topic, every proposed pack, or six story acts in a year. Six blocks below suggest roughly four to six weeks each, with space for holidays, reading, discussion and investigations; this is a planning envelope, not tested lesson duration or a delivery promise. Establish non-identifying authoring estimates for session counts after the first block is tried; do not publish per-child timing or responses. Off-screen work is part of the course design, not a missing game to disguise.

A course is ready to use when every included objective has a reviewed teaching sequence, a meaningful action, an explicit contrast, a later return in a different purpose, accessible help, and an appropriate opportunity for original work or human observation. It also needs a suggested session plan, materials and sources, saved-work behavior for digital tasks, and a clear endpoint. A short selection question, a supplied model, or a story ending alone cannot close an objective.

Each subject can start separately. A capable thinker may want concrete number support, mature English texts and short French sentences at the same time. Reading aloud is available without lowering the intellectual task. A conversation about comfortable examples can choose an entry; no timed test, inferred global ability or compulsory prerequisite gate is proposed.

The named blocks explicitly host the endpoints below; surveys may be book, discussion or physical units and need authored human guidance before course completion. Choosing among survey examples keeps the defined unit purpose; it does not require every candidate science/history pack.

**Status vocabulary:** **Built foothold** means a bounded runtime action exists; its limits remain. **Authored** means a proposal exists, not necessarily build-cleared. **Draft (unmerged)** means a proposal remains in an open draft PR and is not on main; [PR154–163](https://github.com/anotherpanacea-eng/learning-garden/pull/154) contain the candidate math specs 181–212 mentioned below (154:181–183; 155:184–187; 156:188–191; 157:192–195; 158:196–198; 159:199–201; 160:202–203; 161:204–205; 162:206–209; 163:210–212). **Missing** means a teaching sequence or essential bridge still needs authoring. **Off-screen** means an explicitly planned human/material activity; it is not machine-verified. No status implies mastery. Runtime changes listed here remain separate commissions.

## French course within either route

Do not equate sentence length with maturity or language attainment. Proposed function sequence: locate/describe a place → request/clarify → recount recent events → explain near-future intentions → compare and justify → correspond/present and respond. Revisit vocabulary and grammar across rooms, cafés, couriers and source visits. Teach sound–spelling/listening alongside reading; spelling, agreement, verb forms, negation and questions recur in purposeful writing. Each block includes comprehension, a meaningful action, an original utterance/text, a later encounter and a human conversation option. Fluent review, licensed/native-quality audio where used, and human evaluation of spontaneous production remain necessary. This is not a CEFR certification or a replacement for a language class.

## Story and audience are separate axes

The six acts remain participation → belonging → reciprocal exchange → evidence/disagreement → chosen inquiry → shared agency, with the actual names and closures owned by [spec 120](specs/120-keeper-of-the-ways.md).
