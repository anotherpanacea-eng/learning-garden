# Phase 1 decision rules: PROPOSED, not in force

**Status: proposed on 2026-11-15. The owner has not approved these rules.
Nothing is decided by them until this page says they are approved.** Fleet card
LG-80 asks for owner approval before Phase 1 (card LG-81, the free-choice
fortnight) starts.

The rules decide which toy prototypes are kept after the fortnight. They are
written down first so that the result of the fortnight cannot bend them.

## The proposed rules

These three are the rules on card LG-80.

1. **Survive.** A toy that is asked for unprompted on 3 of days 6 to 10 survives.
2. **Drop.** A toy that is not asked for by its day 5 is dropped.
3. **Front door.** If the front door alone works, fix the interface instead.

## Proposed readings

The card states the rules in one line each. These readings fill in what the
line leaves unsaid. They are part of the proposal and need the same approval.

- **Asked for unprompted** means an explorer asks for the toy, or opens it,
  without an adult suggesting it, in the fixed free slot when the adult
  proposes nothing.
- **Days** are the toy's own days, counted from the first day it is on offer.
  When every toy starts on day 1 of the fortnight the two counts are the same.
- **Who asks.** One explorer is enough for rule 1. Rule 2 applies when no
  explorer has asked. Tallies are kept per explorer and are not compared.
- **The front door** is the control from card LG-78: five tiles of existing
  activities, rotated. Rule 3 applies when explorers ask for the front door
  under rule 1 and no new toy survives. The conclusion is then that the
  existing activities are wanted and the way in was the problem, so the next
  work is the interface and no toy is hardened.

## What the rules do not yet cover

These need an owner answer. Each has a recommended answer in the approval
question.

- **The middle case.** A toy asked for by its day 5, but on fewer than 3 of
  days 6 to 10, neither survives nor is dropped by the rules as written.
- **Nothing survives.** The rules do not say what follows when no toy and no
  front door passes.
- **The two withheld days.** Card LG-81 puts the favourite away for two days
  near the end. Those days should fall outside the toy's days 6 to 10, or
  they cannot count either way.

## Where the tally lives

The tally is kept on paper, one [tally sheet](tally-sheet.html) per explorer.
Filled sheets and any notes about an explorer stay out of this repository, its
issues and its pull requests. Only the owner's keep or drop decision for each
toy is written here or in a play receipt.
