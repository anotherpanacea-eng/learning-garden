## 1. Problem and bounded outcome

Newcomers can choose numbers and reading amounts independently, and enter subject lessons directly. They cannot yet join Willowbank's story after its beginning. Replaying a first supper is not a useful requirement for everyone. Let a newcomer choose **Begin with the storm**, **Arrive after the mending**, or **Arrive with the letter from upstream**. Earlier town events become readable backstory labelled **Before you came**, without pretending this explorer served meals, offered artifacts, completed an act, watched a film or demonstrated a skill.

This is not age placement. No age, grade, gender, task count, support history or reading preference selects/recommends an arrival. Math, English and French choices and independent subject entries remain separate. No new diagnostic, level, campaign engine, route registry, profile, reward, date framework, translation, film, calendar, chapter pack or ActIII ending is commissioned. Storm Day remains deferred: this slice stores no arrival date and makes no anniversary promise.

Mandatory behavior is stated below. Suggested module names/layouts are implementation guidance, not requirements for new infrastructure. The complete child-facing commission is [the finite text](../content/249-story-arrivals-text.md). Existing approved Page1/spec248 Page2/English letter bodies are reused without changing their measurements or scientific limits.
