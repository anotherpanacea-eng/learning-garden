# Gather the cupboard

Status: implemented; spec and implementation independently reviewed. Replace instant restocking and automatic top-ups with an actual exploration loop. Preserve all saved orders, records, discoveries, settings, and the current public origin. No identities, remote learner records, timers, punishment, or locks on learning destinations.

## Play loop

The café starts with its existing modest cupboard. Remove Restock all jars and Top up and serve. Serving must require enough actual stock for every chosen ingredient; no support/skip route or correct arithmetic answer may create inventory. A waiting guest, frozen order and plate remain intact during exploration, reload and return.

Every known ingredient (eight starters plus any discoveries) is gatherable from the orchard. Existing discovery investigations still give their first six bites once. Later supplies require a separate repeatable gathering visit, not replaying discovery or pressing an all-stock button. A trip explicitly starts for one known food, shows three pictured trail choices, asks the child to find the target food, then shows countable baskets of that food. Wrong trail choices remain playful and unscored. Only a correct find opens the harvest. A short quantity question has optional step counting/reveal and a Pack with help route. Packing requires a found patch and either a correct quantity or explicit support. It gives the pictured quantity to that one jar once. No automatic opening, gathering, stocking, serving or new guest on route/render/reload.

Harvests have2–6baskets and2–6bites each (4–36). Make the smallest useful number of trips feasible for large orders: favor6baskets×6bites for a shortfall above36; otherwise select a deterministic combination at least as large as the target gap when possible. This is an authored pretend harvest, not a measurement or source of independent fact recall. Jar capacity180 permits a full12×12order plus leftovers; disclose capacity and prevent waste by allowing trips only while stock<=144, or reject collection that cannot fit without consuming the trip. Existing valid stock0..30 survives unchanged. Missing/bad saved stock on an established café recovers to0 rather than silently refilling; new café bootstrap alone initializes stock.

## State and evidence

Use a versioned bounded foraging extension under café with unique trip IDs, target ingredient, stage/search choice, fixed basket quantities, attempts, permanent help/reveal, collected flag and bounded history (24). An explicit start creates a trip; collecting is idempotent; a stale collected trip never awards twice after reload/history expiry. Persist the current trip, including failed responses/help/input. Invalid or unknown ingredients do not unlock discoveries. A completed trip can be viewed but needs a fresh explicit trip to gather again. All reported trail/number attempts are guided play, never fact-recall observations. When a basket×bites question or its count/reveal explanation is shown, feed that pair through the established exact/commuted exposure callback, including paused baseline/broad/story drafts. Record which trip supplied which jar and how much; no fabricated off-screen work.

Freeze the shortfall question's per-jar stock snapshot/target when entering the cupboard stage. Gathering must not reinterpret an earlier arithmetic answer. On return, show both the original plan and current readiness. Serving requires the existing arithmetic check/explicit support plus sufficient current inventory, and consumes actual inventory exactly once. History distinguishes the original question snapshot from actual stock at serving; legacy history retains its original semantics. No supported work becomes independent.

## Interface

Add visible Gather ingredients links at the cupboard and shortage step, with a direct pictured route to each needed ingredient. The orchard includes known-food gathering alongside the three discovery places. A waiting trip can resume; choosing another trip is explicit and can retain the old unfinished trip within the bound. Original food art and chosen guide remain present, short spoken prompts optional, no microphone or external speech. Find, count and pack use taps and keyboard controls with44px targets, visible focus and text equivalents. Group baskets clearly rather than drawing144loose items. Back to the café stays available throughout. Returning from a trip never forces serving or loses the guest.
