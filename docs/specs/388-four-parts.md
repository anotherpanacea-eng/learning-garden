### The mathematical instrument

Authored cases start x=6, larger than either strip, so the initial picture distinguishes the regions. Do not use a minimum-size artificial strip in the mathematical SVG; independent native region-selection buttons supply44px targets.

Four regions have exact area expressions, in order: square x², right ax, bottom bx, corner ab. All values are numbers of square centimeters. Child notation explains that x is the numerical side length in centimeters, x² means x×x, and2x means2×x. The collected whole is x²+(a+b)x+ab. We explore positive integer lengths; this is not a claim that negative numbers are lengths or that these cases teach signed double brackets.

Learners label any region using a tile kind (x², x, or1) and a count. A missing label is null. Counts are integers0..144 (the largest selectable square area is12×12, while strips/corners are no larger); zero remains literal work. Separate ±1/±5 buttons change counts, clamped only by disabling operations that would leave the stated instrument range. Changing a kind keeps an existing count; electing a kind for an unlabeled region starts at count1. Clearing a label returns null. Region selection is view state, not a mathematical action. Opening the mode creates no notebook record.

Choosing the Collect page does not fill or correct them. Display the four proposed labels beside the tray; missing labels remain visibly missing. Show the tray expression and, when all four labels exist, the separately named sum of labels without collapsing x² into x or a constant just because the current numerical values coincide. Zero terms may be omitted in a derived display, but never normalize the stored counts or labels.

**Compare** is optional and ungraded. It shows three separate structural comparisons, plus current numerical values:

1. Each proposed region label versus that region's dimensions. Compare coefficient/power form, not its value at one x. At x=2,2x and x² both equal4 but remain different labels.
2. The learner's collected counts versus the sum of their own four labels. If a label is missing, say the collection is not yet comparable; never invent it.
3. The collected expression versus the whole rectangle's coefficient vector[1,a+b,ab]. Equal coefficients describe the same expression for every permitted x; a numerical equality at the selected x alone does not establish this.

Print numerical agreement separately from structural agreement. Never reduce these to one score or a required correction. Whenever the tray matches the whole but any region label does not match its dimensions, say those two separate facts without attributing a particular error. Show the specific strip-swap message ONLY when a≠b, right is exactly bx and bottom exactly ax; it describes those two labels, not the other labels or the tray. Display it only after the general tray-matches-whole message; do not display it when the tray differs from the whole. Swapping equal-width strips is not observable and never triggers it. Compare uses arithmetic from the frozen source, not today's catalog or an expression evaluator. It does not store outcomes or attempts. It sets referenceShown=true for the working version before displaying the given four products. That flag stays true after edits. The numeric/letter teaching is always available from a working copy without a correct-answer requirement. Opening it sets teachingShown=true for that working version.
