## Exact geometry and proof

Let L=a+b and draw a square frame [0,L]×[0,L]. Array points below are model coordinates; SVG y increases downwards. Four distinct patterned/color-labelled pieces are the same congruent right triangle, with legs a,b. Frame thickness/paper gaps are illustration only. Do not fill the central square by pretending whole unit-square tiles can rotate into it without cutting.

Initial polygons (piece ids persist through every view):

- T0: (0,0),(a,0),(0,b), right corner(0,0).
- T1: (a,0),(L,0),(L,a), right corner(L,0).
- T2: (L,a),(L,L),(b,L), right corner(L,L).
- T3: (0,b),(b,L),(0,L), right corner(0,L).

The empty middle polygon is (a,0),(L,a),(b,L),(0,b). Each edge has length c, the original triangle's hypotenuse. Why it is a square needs explanation, not an appearance claim: the other two angles in a right triangle total90° because its angle sum is180°. At each middle corner those acute angles lie along a straight180° edge; the remaining middle angle is90°. All four sides are the same c. Supply this supported angle bridge inline with a labelled corner inset; do not assert the full triangle/polygon unit is learned.

Move1 translates T0 by(0,a). Move2 translates T2 by(-b,0). Move3 translates T3 by(a,-b). T1 stays where it is. The final empty squares are [0,a]×[0,a] and [a,L]×[a,L], areas a² and b². There are still four unchanged triangles inside the unchanged outer frame. Subtracting the same triangle areas from the same frame leaves the same area: c²=a²+b². This is why the area relationship works for arbitrary positive a,b, not just the6 invitations.

The separate side-length disclosure always refers to the original right triangle, never the intermediate empty space. Compare only completed endpoints; no collision solver, puzzle-fit grader or freehand placement is needed. Reduced motion uses immediate completed positions; no autoplay. Borders/ghosts do not count as mathematical area.

## Computations, lengths and contrast

All numerical teaching values come from the current positive integer legs. Preserve exact integers for a²,b²,N=a²+b²,L² and combined four-triangle area2ab. Whole paired areas suffice; wider fractional-triangle-area instruction remains elsewhere. Never round an area or compare rounded values for a match.

For the long side, teach **the positive length whose square is N**. Find k with k²<=N<(k+1)² using exact integer arithmetic. If k²=N, c=k length units; otherwise c=√N length units, strictly between k and k+1. The radical is an exact name, not a decimal approximation; do not simplify radicals or teach irrational-number proofs here. Show the two bounding squares. N is square units; c is length units. c²=N does not mean c=N, and the negative root is not a side length. Every case checks c>a and c>b using squared comparisons. This connects the square-number rule in391 without changing that board.
