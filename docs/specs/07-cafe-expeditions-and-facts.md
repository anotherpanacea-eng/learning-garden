# Café expeditions and the multiplication garden

Status: implemented; independently reviewed before build and release. Inserted ahead of the planned Post Office increment following play feedback. All examples and records are fictional or synthetic.

## Outcome and scope

Extend the existing café with one illustrated Cloud Orchard, three discoverable foods, contextual repair questions, pictured ingredient qualities/orders, optional device-local speech, and an independently accessible multiplication garden covering every ordered fact 1×1 through 12×12. Preserve all prior science, story, café and math records, the public origin and browser-local privacy controls. No timers, grades, placement claims, accounts, uploads, live AI or server learner records.

## Café contract

Keep eight starter ingredients always available. Add three authored orchard foods with names, fixed warm/cold, crunchy/soft, sweet/tangy qualities and original distinct vector drawings. The orchard has three tappable locations. Tapping a location opens a close-up; explicit investigation steps reveal qualities with visible text and simple pictures; an explicit collect action adds that food to discoveries and cupboard exactly once. Replay remains available; discovering/collecting is not assessment evidence. No real tasting/food experiment. Undiscovered foods never become a prerequisite for satisfying orders: eight starters span all quality combinations. Discovery persists across pause/reload/export/reset/save-off. Orchard trips never replace or advance a waiting order or silently restock jars.

Offer small orders and all facts 1–12 as open number-range choices; default new orders to all facts. Preserve a current order when changing range; apply it to the next guest. Cycle all144 ordered guest/bite pairs across144 new orders in all-facts mode. Party size and bites per plate each range1..12; recipe totals and per-ingredient counts bound12, per-ingredient need up to144. Quantities remain pictured with rows/trays; no dragging required. Freeze parameters when an order is created, including guest, wishes, guests and bites, so changing range or code does not reinterpret saved current/history records. Legacy records retain their original2–4guests/2–3bites parameters. Closed orders cannot consume stock again. Discovery ingredients are not allowed in saved recipes until discovered; old recipe and stock data survive extension normalization.

On an explicit unsuccessful plate check, open one repair question about the actual mismatch. Missing wishes: choose a pictured available ingredient supplying a missing quality (multiple valid answers accepted). Bite count mismatch: choose how many more bite slots are needed (0..12). A response gives feedback and a concrete suggestion; returning to the plate allows edits. Questions are part of fixing an order, not unrelated quizzes or punishment. Help reveals a useful example and remains recorded as support. A visible leave-for-now control returns to the plate; no infinite question lock, penalties, negative reactions or mandatory correct answer. Number mistakes in total/stock similarly offer the existing concrete model immediately; child can revise or continue with support. Repair attempts and support are guided practice and separate from fact recall.

## Multiplication garden contract

Separate route #facts, available from café, Number Garden and home. Full12×12 selectable matrix with row/column headers and keyboard/touch controls. Use a labelled contained horizontal scrolling region with sticky headers/row labels and44px cells on narrow screens; the page itself must not overflow. A row selector may offer a second narrow-screen entry. Never mark a reversed cell learned from its partner. Every cell opens an optional practice visit to that ordered pair; a six-question mixed visit is also available. Suggested sets mix up to2 less-observed/less-secure focus facts with familiar/review facts and rotate coverage; selecting any fact remains open. All144 facts must be reachable/scheduled; no permanent mastery inference from one answer.

Records retain per-attempt correctness, method reported by child/adult (recalled/worked/counting/together), help/reveal, prior encounter, visit and day. Optional method selection; unanswered/skipped remains unknown. Do not infer method from correctness, time or using pictures. A reported recall requires correct first attempt, explicit recalled report and no model/reveal/support; display it as reported recall, not mastery. One successful attempt never establishes enduring fluency. Parent view shows latest observation and repeated observations across distinct days; unknown cells remain unknown. Records bounded and disclosed; saved drafts restore input, step, attempts and permanent support/exposure for each question. No prior assessment records rewritten.

Each fact has original tappable equal-group/array support and a split into useful known parts (for12×7,10×7+2×7). Opening any model marks support permanently for that visit. Models use countable rows/cells up to12×12; text equivalents. Explicit reveal available, permanently recorded. Changing fact starts a fresh practice visit, retaining old observations. Finishing without a method leaves method unobserved. Supported and repeated practice stays separate from baseline. New fact questions, model/reveal use and split-example factor pairs update existing exact/commuted exposure stores and paused baseline/broad/story drafts through the established callback. Ordered fact observations never imply recall of the reversed pair. Existing small-pool practice remains reachable, but its UI links the comprehensive garden.

## Multimodal and privacy contract

Use original vector food shapes with repeated shapes on jars/plate/repair choices, quality symbols plus labels (never color alone), pictured guests/plates, and manual visual investigation changes. Speech is explicit opt-in per utterance, with stop control, visible text equivalent, no automatic sound and no microphone. Use only voices identified by the browser as localService; if unavailable show read-together fallback. No external speech API or learner records sent. Cancel speech on navigation and reset. No sound-only question or instruction. Retain guide/explorer choices. Targets44px, responsive375px/768px, keyboard focus restored after actions, no overflow or giant undifferentiated counter wall.
