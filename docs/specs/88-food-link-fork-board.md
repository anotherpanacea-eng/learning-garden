## A small new action

At `#living/fork`, extend the existing [Food Links](66-living-food-links.md) visit from reading a supplied three-organism path to **constructing a small branching picture**. Put source-backed arrows on four named cards: broad bean plant → black bean aphid → seven-spot ladybird larva, and black bean aphid → marmalade hoverfly larva. Both predators are larvae. The two routes share the aphid; they are not a complete food web, a field observation, a garden forecast or evidence of what every individual eats. The first two relations reuse the reviewed original Food Links pack; the new relation is bounded by the [source audit](../source-audits/88-food-link-fork-board.md).

A learner selects the food and eater for each of three evidence slips and sees **Our proposed link** drawn as a dashed arrow on one board. A check compares each proposal to its **own displayed slip**, marks supported arrows solid, and leaves other proposals editable. The third slip initially says only that some hoverfly larvae eat aphids. It does **not** support the exact named pair. An explicit **Read the closer study** control replaces that one displayed slip with a named study summary; the draft arrow stays visible, and a second check can now reach a different evidence judgement. Earlier checks keep the slip version and recorded outcome. This is a revision after a meaningful change in evidence, not three independent multiple-choice questions.

The board is freely available from Living Garden after Food Links; it never requires a previous answer, changes `foodLinks`, delivers stock, or suggests a science level. It has no timer, score, reward, penalty, adaptive selector or population simulator. Reuse the local inspect → connect → keep/revise rhythm and existing SVG art for the first three cards where their saved drawing descriptors match. Add one original still SVG of a **legless, tapered hoverfly larva** with a text label, not an adult yellow-and-black fly; the drawing is schematic and not an identification key. Do not generate or import an image. Art/science review must inspect the rendered fourth card before build clearance.

### Feedback and help copy

Feedback is evidence-specific; a proposal that a slip cannot support is **not** called biologically impossible. An incomplete pair creates no arrow and an unobserved outcome. Every check stores the exact displayed sentence selected from these templates with the saved source labels inserted safely:

- Supported exact pair for `sap` or `ladybird`: **“This slip supports our proposed food-to-eater arrow. It is one possible named link.”**
- Proposed pair not supported by the displayed slip: **“This slip does not support that exact named arrow. Look again at the food and eater it names; a different link may still be possible.”**
- “Not enough evidence” on either of the two specific first slips: **“This slip does give a named food-and-eater example. You may draw that arrow or leave this row undecided.”**
- `hoverfly` initial slip with “Not enough evidence”: **“The general note does not establish this exact named pair. Leaving its arrow undecided fits the evidence shown.”**
- Any proposed named pair on the `hoverfly` initial slip: **“This general note cannot support this exact named pair yet. Read the closer study before deciding whether this proposed arrow has support.”**
- `hoverfly` closer slip with exact `aphid → hoverfly-larva`: **“The closer study supports this named possible link. It does not establish every food or predict population change.”**
- `hoverfly` closer slip with “Not enough evidence”: **“The closer study now gives a named feeding example. You may draw its arrow or leave the row undecided.”**
- Blank/incomplete row: **“No complete proposal was checked for this row. Its draft remains editable.”**

Help fold text: **“Choose the food on the left and the eater on the right. The arrow says food supplies energy to this eater. The stage arrow in Life Stages means something different. Read each evidence slip before checking; a general statement about some hoverflies cannot name this one larva-and-aphid pair. You can keep a row undecided.”** Opening help, asking the existing local Hear control, or reporting **“We thought together”** is recorded as supplied support, not an independent explanation or verified listening. There is no automatic reading or speech; local narration never uses a network service.

## Flow, board and revisits

Direct `#living/fork` entry shows a short invitation and **Start this board**; it does not create a root or expose the full card pack until Start. Pages **Cards / Build / Kept checks** are all open after Start. Cards shows all four source cards and the evidence slips; Build keeps the immediate slip beside its editable row and a four-card board on screen; Kept checks shows exactly what was proposed, which slip version was visible, recorded support and feedback. The three rows can be edited in any order. Native food/eater selects list all four saved card labels with blank first; selecting either alone saves a partial draft without drawing an arrow. A separate **Not enough evidence for this named arrow** choice clears the pair only on that explicit action. Selecting a card clears that row's Not enough state. No drag or automatic completion.

Each complete proposed pair draws one **dashed, labelled “Our proposed link”** arrow; it does not assert a real feeding fact before a check. An arrow from a card to itself may be proposed, then receive evidence-specific unsupported feedback without fabricating cannibalism. Blank/uncertain/incomplete rows draw no arrow. The three rows share the same four board nodes rather than displaying three isolated mini-quizzes. After Check, a supported exact pair becomes solid and labelled **“Supported by this slip”** in the current view; unsupported proposals remain dashed and editable. The app never silently draws the expected arrow. Text below the board lists each proposed food → eater pair, evidence slip and current support judgement for keyboard/screen-reader access. Colour is redundant with line style and text.

**Read the closer study** is always available; it switches the `hoverfly` slip from general to specific without changing the proposed endpoints or old checks. **Compare the earlier general note** switches back with the same draft; this also does not rewrite history. A new check is an explicit action. A result label is current only while the row responses and slip version still match the last readable check; editing a row or changing slip version makes the old check **earlier** and the live proposal **unchecked**. No automatic regrade on reload. With the closer study, a valid three-arrow fork can be assembled, but it is never required to leave, replay, or visit another game. A later revisit can start at the exact saved page and draft, compare both evidence versions, revise one edge, and check again. The original Food Links visit and Life Stages records remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

At 320 and 820 CSS pixels, show readable node/arrow labels without horizontal page overflow; a stacked card/arrow list is an acceptable phone rendering of the **same** graph. Native selects, buttons, summaries and links have at least 44px targets. Focus returns to the changed row after selection, to the displayed slip after switching evidence, and to the check result after Check. Preserve partially selected native controls on reload. Each action has one visible status announcement; no auto-speech or decorative motion is required. No grade, age placement or physical-species identification claim is made from a tap.

## Own-source save, recovery and limits

One optional `foodFork` root, absent until explicit Start and independent of `foodLinks`, `lifeStages`, `livingExploration`, Café stock, garden parcels and other roots. The readable v1 shape is `{version:1,source,page,evidenceView,closerSeen,answers,checks,nextCheck,support,retainedRaw?}`. `page` is `cards|build|kept`; `evidenceView` is `initial|closer`; `closerSeen` is a sticky boolean after the closer slip was actually displayed. `support` holds only `helpOpened`, `together`, `narrationRequested` booleans. `answers` is an object keyed by saved source row IDs. Each readable value is `{food:null|cardId,eater:null|cardId,notEnough:boolean}`; Not enough excludes non-null endpoints. A partial pair is valid. Unknown answer keys and malformed answer values remain raw, escaped in notes, and cannot be silently overwritten; another readable row remains open. A malformed known row is visibly unavailable until an explicitly confirmed separate board, while other rows can still be edited and checked.

`source` is the complete immutable snapshot. It holds `{version:1,id,cards,rows,copy,credits}` in saved order. Each card has `{id,label,scientificName,passage,pictureText,drawing}`; `drawing` is a versioned descriptor, never a URL. Each row has `{id,prompt,initial:{text,expected,feedback},closer?}`; `expected` is either `null` or `{food:cardId,eater:cardId}`, and each slip's `feedback` holds its complete bounded sentences for exact, different, Not enough and incomplete responses. `copy` holds the introduction, legend, limit, stage contrast and help. `credits` holds source titles, roles and HTTPS links, including each slip's attribution. These fields contain every sentence needed for cards, live checks and old check interpretation. The first pack's feedback table above determines its exact text; the implementation must not regenerate that text from today's catalog.

Admission accepts a supported v1 source with **1–8 uniquely identified cards and 1–8 uniquely identified rows**, bounded text, own card references, one initial slip per row and an optional closer slip/expected link. It does not require today's four IDs, three rows, wording, row order or exact content count. IDs use `[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9_-]{0,63}`; per-card label/scientific name ≤160 UTF-8 bytes, passage/picture text ≤1,200, per-slip text/source-credit ≤1,000, all shared teaching/feedback lines ≤1,000, and serialized source ≤16 KiB UTF-8. Known drawing descriptors use matching original art; an unknown drawing uses the saved picture description and label as the safe visual fallback without substituting current art or rejecting the otherwise readable visit. The new authored pack has exactly four cards, three rows, and one closer slip; an exact-pack source test checks those authored facts separately from v1 save admission. Escape every source/raw string in page and notes. No learner name, custom biology claim, photo, recording or upload is stored.

A check has positive safe local `id`, `evidenceView`, `closerSeen`, a snapshot of the readable row answers, per-row recorded `outcome:null|boolean` and complete displayed feedback, and copied support booleans. Fresh outcomes compare each row with **its own frozen source and displayed slip**; for an initial slip with `expected:null`, only explicit Not enough fits. For a slip with a named expected pair, only that exact food/eater pair fits. An incomplete answer is null; Not enough on a specific slip is a definite nonfit, not a science impossibility. A historical check's outcome/feedback is displayed as recorded even if it contradicts today's authored pack or its saved expected pair; loading never rewrites or regrades it. A check with one malformed row answer can remain readable for its other saved rows; raw malformed check slots remain unchanged and summarized, not mass-rendered. No checked result is labeled independent science understanding: sources and example are supplied, support is separate, and both slip versions are familiar after inspection.

Keep the latest **twelve readable checks**, with a visible oldest-readable-removal notice before the thirteenth. Preserve every malformed check slot verbatim and keep readable siblings open; do not trim while merely loading or rendering. `nextCheck` exceeds every safely parsed stored check ID, including an otherwise unreadable record, and refuses at safe-integer exhaustion. Each state write is atomic. New Start, Check, evidence switch, answer edits and confirmed separate board preflight both the candidate state and the **actual pretty-printed Grown-up notes report** with the existing `prepareProgressImport` limit; a refusal leaves all saved work intact and gives a clear download/separate-explorer action. Use the app's existing bounded progress helper, not another limit registry. Generic transfer allowlists the optional `foodFork` JSON through real import/export; save-off, explorer switch and reset remain generic. Notes give source provenance and recorded check limits while preserving complete raw JSON in the download; raw HTML is never executed.

Invalid/future root or unreadable source/current enum is **read-only recovery**, with an explicit focused notice, Grown-up notes link, Living Garden return and a confirmed **Start a separate board**. The new root's `retainedRaw` nests the **entire old root unchanged**, including any prior `retainedRaw`; refusal due depth/size keeps the old root. Readable roots with one malformed answer/check sibling stay usable elsewhere. Neither import nor view mutates or repairs old records. A new separately started board uses today's approved authored source; existing saved boards always use their own source.
