## Purpose and encounter

The Prop Box has three short-vowel families and 342's first/middle/last sound changes, but lacks short e/u and several consonants. Add a **second packet in the same room**, “Market labels,” for a pretend market in the theater's show. Dot needs useful labels for the props. This is the next bounded foundation commission recommended by [the literacy audit](../literacy-next-steps.md); it does not complete that commission or skip to250's letter teams.

The familiar loop stays: choose a packet and card, hear an adult or try print first, arrange three letter places, reveal/compare when wanted, revise or keep any arrangement, return later. Add a compact adult-led letter encounter for e, u, d and g. Letter identity and one context-specific sound are taught together here; uppercase/lowercase matching and all sounds of a letter remain separate work. Word meanings are explicit, not assumed from a picture.

Spec49 §5 encounter:

1. **Teaching:** “Meet letter sounds” opens one tactile card at a time for e/u/d/g, with a large glyph, a three-letter contextual word, its relevant position and short adult guidance. The adult supplies actual sounds. Four direct letter buttons plus Back retain the selected card. This is available before card work and at any later turn, not an entry test.
2. **Contrast:** six literal pairs in the finite text. Different vowels can occupy the middle while the start/end remain; later e/u words revisit first/last changes. The g is hard in bug/bag, not every g-word. Three letters happen to represent three phonemes in these selected words; this is not a universal rule.
3. **Supported practice:** reuse342's adult partner card, starting word, picture, manual changed-position comparison and worked example; retain disclosed help cumulatively and return to the exact arrangement. Letter teaching is a source-level disclosure, not an independent attempt.
4. **New use:** read Dot's short shared note with an adult and place/keep a useful label for the pretend market. Its fixed target tokens are named in the finite text; all other tokens are adult-supplied. No text-comprehension or oral-reading score.
5. **Return:** any of six cards, modes, letter cards or the note can be revisited in any order. First/latest arrangements remain distinct, with their own disclosed help. There is no finite-content-to-mastered conversion or automatic difficulty ladder.

This serves E1-I/II word reading/transcription in spec49 and the optional Market Fair and Show rhythm of spec120 §8. The story purpose is a new show, not another town repair. It neither reveals a canonical Keeper's Page nor completes a chapter, festival, ending or real market commission. No video/reward is needed; quiet Keep feedback is enough.
