## Purpose and finite boundary

A visitor wants a smaller drawing of the same route. Change the distance represented by one grid **edge**, and watch the whole right-angle route redraw. Compare both drawings openly. This is a spatial representation change, not a text quiz or a second route finder. The physical route in the invented story stays fixed; the drawing and its edge counts change. Every numerical readout is supplied explanation, not an independently measured distance or a learner answer.

These drawings cannot predict real travel. Screen edges are not measured centimeters.”** Use American child-facing spelling; m means meters. Two keys only: 1 map-grid edge represents 2 m, or 1 map-grid edge represents 4 m.
