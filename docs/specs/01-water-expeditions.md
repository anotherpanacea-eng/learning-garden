# 01 — Water Lab expeditions

Status: implemented; spec and implementation independently reviewed. Future roadmap steps remain planned.

## Goal and content

Build a complete guided science visit around Marble's public Water Lab, without copying or embedding its implementation. Three independently available missions: (a) compare ice with liquid water; (b) compare cooler and warmer liquid water; (c) investigate surface evaporation before boiling. Connect each mission to the garden after rain, with a short purpose, optional prediction, focused exploration instructions, reported observations, and an original step-by-step explanation. Predictions, uncertain observations, reading together, skipped steps, and stopping are all welcome. There is no score, mastery/placement inference, or answer gate.

Each mission has original title/story, a prediction prompt with two hypotheses and an unsure option, three numbered exploration instructions, four report choices (including not noticed/uncertain), a three-frame explanatory illustration and captions, one discussion/transfer invitation, and an optional paper or talk project. Missions are fixed and reviewable, not generated. The interface describes only observations the child/adult reports, never verifies them. All explanation sequences remain available without visiting the external site. No physical heat, ice preparation, real food, or equipment is required.

Content boundaries: ice molecules continue moving around positions rather than becoming motionless; liquid molecules can move past one another. Ordinary ice has an open structure, so do not illustrate freezing as molecules packing more tightly. Warmer liquid generally has more vigorous molecular motion; molecules do not grow or change identity. Evaporation can happen at a liquid surface below boiling; water becomes water vapor, not nothing. Tiny diagram dots represent molecules and are explicitly not to scale. The diagrams are authored explanations, not live physics or measurements. Keep boiling pressure/latent-heat detail out of this small scope, while avoiding universal statements that heating always increases temperature.

## Child flow

Add a Science invitation on the home page and a #science hub with three mission entries and retained visits. #science/play shows the current visit, and safely falls back to the hub if absent. Selecting another mission while a visit is unfinished requires an explicit continue/new choice and retains the old visit. Each mission can finish independently.

Stages: wonder (optional prediction), explore (external link or stay-here choice), notice (optional report choices), explain (three controllable frames), ending. Explicit continue/leave-for-now actions advance one stage; no route, render, background timer, or external event completes a stage. Explicit explanation shortcuts, including the stay-here pictures button, open explain directly; the optional observation slip remains available through Previous step. A child may finish with no prediction and no report. Frames have previous/next/reset, optional small before/after motion, reduced-motion handling, text equivalents, and never require dragging/hover. Explain is teaching: all visit records are guided learning, never fresh assessment evidence. A discussion answer or off-screen project is not automatically scored or marked completed.

The chosen guide is present and equally capable on every mission; explorer choice stays independent. Keep reading mode separate: not observed / read together / read independently. Explicitly record whether a prediction was selected before or after explanation was opened, so revisiting earlier stages cannot turn a taught answer into fresh evidence. Use a permanent explanationOpened flag. There is no automatic correctness check of free talk.

The standard anchor remains usable on touch/keyboard and without programmatic window.open. Save the draft before leaving. Label it an external Marble site and tell the child to return to this garden tab; include a local 'We’re back / continue here' control. An explicit Water Lab link click at any current-visit stage records only externalLinkOpened, not site load, time spent, experiment controls used, completion, or correctness. Explain that no garden answers are sent, while the external site handles its own visits. Do not append identifiers or answers to the URL, use cross-origin messaging, embed, proxy, scrape child activity, or require a login. If the page is unavailable or confusing, 'Stay here and explore the pictures' keeps the full local visit usable. Never automatically open the site.

## Implementation/state interface

New original modules science-content.mjs, science-core.mjs, science-ui.mjs and science.css. Content exports missions [{id,title,invitation,story,predictionPrompt,predictions:[strings],instructions:[strings],observations:[strings],frames:[{title,body,model,phase}],talk,project,sourceLinks}]. Three mission IDs: melt, warm, evaporate. Three frames each; model IDs water/ice/heat/evaporate are finite authored illustrations. External URL is a constant, never taken from saved state.

state.science={version:1,current:null|visitId,visits:[visits]}. Visit has unique visit-N ID, missionId, stage ('wonder','explore','notice','explain','ending'), prediction:null|index, predictionAfterTeaching:false/true, observations:[indices], reading:'unobserved'|'together'|'independent', externalLinkOpened:false/true, stayedLocal:false/true, explanationOpened:false/true, frame:0..2, completed:false/true. explanationOpened stays true across navigation/reload. Visit IDs remain unique. No assessment observations or math exposures are created by these missions. Retained visit records are bounded to the latest 24 with the current visit retained; only explicit start makes a visit. This cap is disclosed in the grown-up notes; no claim to an exhaustive permanent history.

Exports ensureScience(state), startScience(state,missionId), resumeScience(state,id), currentScience(state), setPrediction(state,index|null), toggleObservation(state,index), setScienceReading(state,value), advanceScience(state), scienceBack(state), scienceFrame(state,delta|'reset'), openScienceExplanation(state), noteScienceExternal(state), stayScienceLocal(state), missionById(id). Each mutation validates input and stages. scienceBack returns one stage earlier (ending may revisit explain) without clearing completion or teaching flags; subsequent forward actions return to the same ending without creating another record. ensure normalizes only the science extension, safely recovers malformed additions, never creates a visit, never opens a link, and never discards valid legacy math/garden/café state. Invalid saved stage resets the visit to wonder with conservative teaching/support flags; ending with completed=false becomes completed=true; completed=true means the visit has reached an ending before, and a valid earlier stage is retained for intentional review. Only an invalid stage in a completed visit is forced to ending. Unknown mission IDs are dropped, arrays cannot stand in for object records, and prediction/report/frame indices are bounded. All UI content from saved state is escaped. Render functions are read-only; bindScience(state,{save,render,focus}) handles user actions and saves before rerendering/link departure.

## Storm-recovery framing

The hub and three existing mission stories now begin with a storm-flooded low path: a cold night freezes a puddle edge, sunlight warms liquid water, and evaporation is one possible reason a puddle shrinks. Keep runoff, infiltration and evaporation distinct; these molecular pictures do not establish which route actual stormwater took, test drainage or certify that a path is ready. The optional recovery sketch invites hypotheses without scoring or requiring real floodwater. No mission IDs, predictions, reports, frame indices or saved observations change. A later controllable runoff/soil/drainage experiment needs its own specification; it is not implemented by this narrative update. Runoff and evaporation context checked against USGS Water Science School on 2026-10-18. No new external implementation/assets copied.
