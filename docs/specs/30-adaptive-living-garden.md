| Strand | Depth 1 | Depth 2 | Depth 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Science | Choose a suitable pictured place and the animal's need | Choose a place and the supplied observation supporting it | Choose the original suitable place, then reason about an explicit changed-condition picture: shelter leaves are now dry, or flowers are now closed |
| Reading / note comprehension | A short note explicitly identifies a parcel tag | Follow a short first/next sequence to identify the café parcel | Infer which parcel serves hungry neighbors from its contents, and select the sentence supporting that choice |
| Math | Count bites in equal packs | Given total bites and bites per pack, find number of packs | Plan tomorrow's hypothetical delivery with one fewer pack: subtract a pack, then multiply; today's actual parcel remains unchanged |

Science depth 3 is an authored before/after question, not a live experiment or a claim that a log's removal/closure inevitably causes a particular real outcome. The changed picture explicitly supplies dry leaves or closed flowers. Answer: recheck/look for the moisture or nectar now absent, rather than assume every place changed or that the original need disappeared. Habitat matching uses the before picture; the second answer concerns the pictured change. Never teach that caterpillars share adults' nectar needs.

Reading help identifies and explains the needed text; narration is always optional and separate. A note question observes comprehension selection, never decoding or fluent independent reading. No correct tag is exposed by the parcel-button label before submission/help.

## Small, conservative suggestion rule

Each strand has a stored suggested depth (initially 1), an epoch start visit ID and an independent manual choice (auto/1/2/3). Use only known v2 visits that have closed (carried or explicitly left) and actual submitted responses in that strand. An unattempted strand, skipped/left question, legacy visit, or merely opened help is unobserved, not a failure. Never infer science from math success or reading from a science match. Choose by the latest assessed closed visit outcome per task key, not the last response within a visit or a tally of duplicate visits. A later visit can replace an earlier outcome for that key under the familiarity rule; wrong→right within one visit always remains a retry outcome. Look at at most the latest six distinct keys at the current suggested depth since its last change.

At the next explicit new visit, invite one deeper depth after either two distinct new task keys matched on the first submitted response without scoped answer help/narration/vocabulary, or three distinct familiar task keys did so. The latter is labelled familiar practice and is a weaker basis for an invitation, never independent transfer/mastery evidence. Order those outcomes by visit ID. For mixed evidence, use a coherent recent run: a retry/mismatch ends the preceding clean run; a clean match ends the preceding retry run; neutral supported outcomes do neither. Count promotion matches only after the latest retry outcome, and count retry outcomes only after the latest clean match. Two distinct retries in that current run suggest one smaller depth; a single latest retry holds the depth and offers another example. Thus older retries cannot overrule a later successful run, and promotion/demotion cannot occur together. Match-after-help or audio-only support keeps the depth and help available; choosing narration alone never lowers depth. Repeated tries at one key count once, with its latest observed outcome replacing the old one. Cap depth at 1–3. Manual tasks above/below the automatic depth do not change that strand's automatic suggestion. Reset only that strand's evidence epoch when its suggestion changes, so old responses cannot repeatedly promote/demote it.

Record help/audio/vocabulary at the time of each submitted response. Later opening an explanation does not rewrite how an earlier answer was obtained. Reported math method (knew/worked/counted/together/unreported) remains separate; together is supported, and counted/derived matches never become recall evidence. Familiarity stays separate from first-try matching. Suggestions summarize this small practice history, not a validated educational assessment. Show a concise rationale per strand in settings and full distinctions in grown-up notes.

Familiarity keys are bounded known catalogs: science case+depth (27), note depth+one of three authored tag variants (9), math depth+ordered packs/bites (432). Mark a key offered only when that strand becomes visible: science on start, note after the pictured comparison is resolved, math after the parcel is found. Freeze prior familiarity in each new visit. For direct-product math, prior shared fact exposure also makes the question familiar. For inverse/hypothetical tasks, retain a prior-product-seen note without equating familiarity with the product to familiarity with the new relation. No already seen product becomes independent recall evidence.
