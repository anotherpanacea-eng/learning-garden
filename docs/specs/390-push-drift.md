## Physical and mathematical model

Two independent model carts start with the SAME source-supplied signed velocity v0. Right is positive, left negative. They receive the SAME selected net horizontal force in each one-second interval. Each mass is the TOTAL cart-plus-load mass in kilograms; force cards are measured in newtons. Mass is constant within a run; changing either load or any force returns the local clock to0, preserves all other inputs and starts a new model run. No variable-mass dynamics, momentum-transfer event, friction, air resistance or position calculation is modeled. Vertical forces balance. A zero net horizontal force leaves velocity unchanged; it need not be zero. The cart pictures are **Velocity displays**, not position diagrams.

For cart mass m and interval force F: a=F/m in m/s²; Δv=a×1s; v_after=v_before+Δv. Keep exact rational values: for integer source v0 and program F1…Ft, velocity numerator is v0×m+ΣF, denominator m. Comparisons cross-multiply these small integers; do not grade with floating tolerances or repeatedly round a state. Display reduced fractions where needed and exact signed integers for current cases. Narration states numerator over denominator, units and direction. Zero has no direction. Acceleration magnitude and speed are distinct: an opposing force can reduce speed before reversal, and the heavier cart's smaller velocity change does not always mean a smaller speed at that time.

The velocity-time graph uses straight segments BETWEEN second-boundary predictions because each interval has constant acceleration. Its vertical quantity is velocity, so a horizontal nonzero line means constant moving velocity, not rest. The whole chart is a prediction from the stated law, not measured data or evidence validating Newton's law. Equal end velocities do not establish equal histories or equal masses; zero force can give coincident histories for unequal masses.

One fixed symmetric velocity scale per SOURCE is used for BOTH displays, graph and all choices/steps: bound=max(1,ceil(abs(v0)+steps×max(abs(forceChoices))/min(massChoices))). This includes every allowed program/load. Never auto-rescale on a load/program/frame change; all teaching examples in a source share one separate labelled scale: max(1,ceil(max absolute predicted velocity over every frame of every readable frozen example in that source)). It remains fixed across teaching pages/frames and never reads current work. If no example is readable, use1 for the empty diagram range. Mark velocity signs/directions, unit m/s, graph time seconds, zero line and both cart names. Distinguish traces with shape/dash and names, not color alone. Decorative cart art is original inline SVG; no implied position animation or timed playback. No drag, timer, frame-rate physics or audio recording.
