Nothing is scored or auto-corrected. A lower power can be chosen at any time. Objective changes preserve center/focus. Focus changes preserve objective/center. Convert pointers through the actual rendered SVG mapping, including letterboxing, not a naive full-element rectangle. Arrows provide a keyboard/touch alternative; no dragging, hover, gesture or fine motor precision is required to keep any view. Stop focus at own-list endpoints without wrapping.

Use the fixed400-unit circular aperture at center(200,200), radius200. World point(x,y) projects to `(200+(x-center.x)×400/diameter,200+(y-center.y)×400/diameter)`. For source focus blur S in drawing units, projected blur standard deviation is **S×400/diameter**, preserving relative softness when power changes. Blur applies to the projected pattern only. Keep the circular clip on the final field. There is no RNG, timer, autofocus or hidden target scoring. Clip marks AND target annotation to the circle. Responsiveness uniformly scales this SVG; it never changes the saved world center or asserts actual-size measurements.
