## The missing action

The signed-rates lessons translate exact rules between equations, tables and graphs. Paired Points plots supplied observations and distinguishes slope from tightness, but explicitly excludes fitting and prediction. Here an explorer **changes a candidate line against scattered records, inspects its discrepancies, and uses it as an estimate**. A point off this line is not a plotting mistake. There is no unique correct line, regression calculation, fitted-line score or inferred proficiency.

Dot wants a rough time estimate for a courier errand. All records, route blocks, times and routes are invented. They are not measurements of a child, real delivery promises, river predictions or safety advice. Keep both observations and model visible and distinct. A recorded time never moves when the line moves. An extrapolated number is calculable without becoming well-supported evidence.
