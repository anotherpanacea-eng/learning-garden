## The new relationship and story

The market's wildlife corner needs a picture-story label. The adult-animal key taught that a second body clue matters; now very different bodies can belong to stages of the **same individual**. A child inspects four original stage diagrams, chooses a neighbouring stage on a supplied life-cycle chart, and distinguishes growing/changing from the beginning of another generation. This is a concrete intermediate lesson before life cycles across species or food chains, using the existing Living Garden inspect → compare → keep/revise relationship at **#living/stages**. No new world/router, outdoor observation, real specimen, clock, rearing activity, private writing, live AI, stock reward, mastery gate or required film.

An always-available overview says: **“Our pictures show a possible life story. Egg, caterpillar, chrysalis and adult are different stages. The caterpillar, chrysalis and emerging adult can be the same individual. An adult female's eggs begin offspring: another generation.”** A brief source note names Pieris rapae and distinguishes this species from all butterflies. No child needs to read the Latin name independently. The app supplies factual passages; it does not grade the child for deriving unseen biology from a drawing.

Forward biological chart links are fixed:

1. egg → larva: “A caterpillar can hatch from an egg.” Meaning **same individual growing/changing**.
2. larva → pupa: “A grown caterpillar can form a chrysalis.” Meaning **same individual growing/changing**.
3. pupa → adult: “An adult can emerge from its chrysalis.” Meaning **same individual growing/changing**.
4. adult → egg: “Adult females can lay eggs; offspring begin another generation.” Meaning **offspring / another generation**, not that the adult becomes its own egg.

The last arrow must be visually/verbally distinguished, for example dashed with the explicit word **offspring**. Ordinary life-cycle arrows are stage relationships, not distances, time durations or an immortal individual's circular journey. No insect literally develops backward when we look back through a chart.

## Eight complete supplied questions and two separate judgements

All eight are available in a labelled native chooser, without a prerequisite lock. All share the stable frozen source `chartId:"small-white-life-stages"`; `source.id` remains the individual task ID. Their wording is about chart position, not biological reversal. Options are all four stage IDs plus **Not sure yet**; rotate their deterministic visual order by case so a fixed button position is not the answer. Stage choice is freely revisable. At first no neighbour or meaning is selected. A blank or uncertain response remains unobserved, never an incorrect absence.

Keep the reference picture and exact question together. After choosing a neighbour, display **Our proposed forward link** with a clear arrow. For a before-question, the chosen earlier stage is drawn on the left and the reference on the right; do not draw a backwards biological transition. Until chosen, show an empty card rather than silently supplying the answer. The app may show any proposed pair, but must label it proposed rather than presenting an incorrect pair as established science.

A second question asks **“What would the correct forward link mean?”** with supplied options **Same individual growing/changing**, **Offspring: another generation**, **Not sure yet**. Child choices are never parsed from prose. Check/keep is always available, including a blank/uncertain trail; no answer locks the pictures or next case.

Keep separate `neighbourMatch:null|boolean` and `meaningMatch:null|boolean`. Neighbour blank/unsure gives null. A selected wrong neighbour gives false. Grade meaning only when the chosen neighbour fits this case AND the meaning selection is one of the two substantive options; otherwise meaningMatch is null and the original selection remains recorded. This avoids treating an interpretation of a different proposed pair as an independent biology error. Do not clear meaning merely because the neighbour changed. Correct neighbour with wrong meaning is true/false; correct neighbour with unsure meaning true/null; wrong neighbour with any meaning false/null.

Complete feedback preserves these distinctions: blank/uncertain neighbour invites a closer look without supplying an observation; wrong neighbour says the proposed pair is not the requested chart neighbour and leaves its meaning unassessed; correct neighbour confirms that relation and independently addresses its selected/uncertain meaning. For the adult→egg boundary always explain offspring, not an adult turning into its own egg. A fitting link is a useful label for the fictional wildlife corner, not observed independent explanation, retelling, scientific inference or mastery. Any trail can be kept/revised; all destinations remain open.
