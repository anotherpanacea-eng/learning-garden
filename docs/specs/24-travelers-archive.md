# 24 — Travelers' Archive

**Status: Initial roadmap scope; not build-ready or implemented.**

## Purpose and first playable slice

A misdirected parcel arrives at the Paris bookstall with a clue from another time. Explore one carefully bounded historical setting through an original map, a simple timeline and two short, attributed or original accounts. Inspect an artifact illustration, locate its setting, put events in order, compare what the accounts say, and compose or discuss a reply. Different accounts can notice different things without either being dishonest.

Choose the setting during detailed specification after reviewing Marble's history topics and their prerequisites. Do not collapse a civilization into one costume or fictional voice, invent primary sources, or call a modern reconstruction an ancient artifact. Label fiction, historical facts and uncertainty. Geography will need reviewed content beyond Marble's limited subject inventory. Keep accessible short passages and optional deeper text independent of a child's age or French ability.

## Build gate

Minimum: one place, one time period, one map, one timeline, two accounts, three linked investigations and an unscored response invitation. Review sources, chronology, scales, accepted interpretations and representations independently before build. Compare Jane's timeline/ordering/evidence formats for reuse. Structured composition is not independent writing; supported reading is not decoding evidence. Preserve local-only progress, frozen drafts, open entry and existing touch/keyboard and privacy controls.
