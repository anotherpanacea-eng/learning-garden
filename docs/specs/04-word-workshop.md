# 04 — Word Workshop

**Status: Planned; not implemented.**

## Goal and play loop

Explore how sound changes connect to letters and meaningful word parts. Hear a reviewed example, tap sound/letter pieces, blend at the child’s pace, change one piece, and read the resulting real word in a short shared line. Prediction, explanation, and an optional paper-word transfer remain invitations. This is practice, never an age/grade placement test.

## Content minimum

Offer independent doors for sound/letter practice and meaning-changing word parts; neither is required before the other. Provide three reviewed sound/letter families: `-at` (cat, mat, sat, bat), `-in` (pin, tin, fin, bin), and `-op` (hop, mop, top, pop). Supply three short connected-text passages with target words; adults can read other words. Add two small word-part families: re- (read/reread, pack/repack, build/rebuild) and un- (pack/unpack, tie/untie, lock/unlock). Show their effects in meaningful scenes and distinguish meaning-bearing parts from individual sounds; these can be explored with adult reading support. Only authored real-word combinations are selectable; unsupported swaps receive an explanation, not a made-up pronunciation.

Local audio must have verified rights and adult human review of each word, isolated sound, segmentation, and blend in a declared pronunciation variety. Do not generate guessed pronunciations or score dialect differences. Without reviewed audio, label an adult-read text mode and withhold audio-dependent checks. Offer replay, separate sound taps, visual letter highlighting, and optional explanation without auto-advancement.

## State and evidence

A versioned `words` extension stores pack/item, selected pieces, playback/support flags, attempts, and separate reading observations. Keep the latest 24 practice summaries. Suggest `-at` from observations of short-a and constituent consonant connections, or `-in`/`-op` from corresponding vowels/consonants; unknown means unobserved. Parents can choose any pack. Refresh exact [Marble prerequisite IDs/descriptions](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy) before mapping. Listening or matching does not establish independent decoding; structured selection does not establish free writing. Record no child audio. Project acknowledgement proves no off-screen work.

## Accessibility, privacy, and non-goals

Preserve both legacy storage keys, old records, pause/reload, saving-off, export/reset, and conservative recovery. Equal guides and independent avatars remain. Use large labelled touch/keyboard buttons, visible focus, text alternatives, and no timed response. No grades, locks, identity fields, uploads, shared learner data, analytics, remote speech service, AI backend, exhaustive phonics curriculum, or new framework.
