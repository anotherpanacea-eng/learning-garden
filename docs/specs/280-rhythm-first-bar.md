## Outcome and teaching

At `#studio/rhythm`, make a four-beat bar with whole/half/quarter notes and quarter rests; hear different attacks span the same total time; reproduce three existing authored echoes or keep an invented bar. All four source choices are open from entry: `own-bar`, `echo-1` (`q q h`), `echo-2` (`h q q`), `echo-5` (`q rq q q`). No random target, microphone, timed tapping, singing assessment, prerequisite or mastery/independence label. Nothing requires a full bar or match before Keep, leaving or another choice. Every activity stays open, without story progress.

After a source is explicitly selected, one optional replayable teaching drawer presents a whole note, two halves, four quarters, then `q rq q q`, one step per Next. Each step has its own explicit Play example. Step selection/Next never starts audio. Teaching drawer and echo answer drawer are distinct; the selected echo's source blocks/target-order prose are hidden until **Show the example**. No automatic reference playback or answer reveal. Close teaching returns the unchanged draft. Visiting/replaying teaching supplies support when explicitly opened during a visit; it is not completion or a skill observation. Entry before a source choice shows no worked pattern, avoiding an unrecorded teaching exposure that would later be mislabeled absent.

The whole four-beat duration is always visible:2 seconds at120 quarter-note beats/minute, one beat=.5s. A half and two quarters occupy1 second but have one versus two attacks; a whole and two halves/four quarters occupy2 seconds. Timing slots include a short articulation gap; “lasts” refers to scheduled note duration, not nonstop acoustic energy. Contrast pitch versus duration: every Rhythm note has the same330Hz source frequency, while Bell Studio varies pitch in fixed slots. Keep fraction labels tied to **this four-beat bar**. Music quality is discussed with a listener, never judged by literal sequence equality.

## Editing and finite checks

Four equal beat cells and rectangular labelled blocks; fraction text always visible (`whole bar`, `1/2 of this bar`, `1/4 of this bar`), hatched quarter rest labelled rest. Unfilled cells stay visibly empty. Palette buttons append if total≤4; otherwise refuse with needed/remaining beats and leave state exact. Tap selects a block; Move left/right swaps neighbors, Remove deletes selected block, Swap uses selected palette choice if resulting sum≤4. Clear asks once before deleting the current sequence. Each edit stops audio first and clears only current feedback; earlier frozen checks/kept work remain. No check is needed to edit/Keep. Max4 events because shortest event1 beat. Selection is optional local UI state, not learner evidence.

**How full?** displays deterministic facts without creating a check: used beats, unfilled beats, sound attacks, explicit rests and total occupied duration. It does not recompute historical outcomes. **Compare with the example** explicitly freezes an echo attempt; any sequence including empty/incomplete is allowed. Outcome code `match|different` compares ordered codes to this visit's frozen target at that moment, including rest. `own-bar` has no target/match assessment; its Check action is **Keep the bar facts**, freezing only the same literal facts. Match feedback reveals no target order; it points to deliberate Listen/Show. A prior match/difference comparison counts as feedback exposure for subsequent attempts on this same visit. Reopening a kept response/check cannot refresh its flags or count as a new attempt.
