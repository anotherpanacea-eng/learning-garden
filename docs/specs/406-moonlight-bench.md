## Diagram contract

Primary paired material: **From above** and **View from Earth**. This is a simplified non-eclipse model with parallel sunlight from the RIGHT. Do not draw the Sun as a nearby ball on the same scale. Show three separated arrows pointing left at the diagram's right edge and a plain HTML sunlight-direction caption. Earth is central, Moon places form a ring. Eight balls mean eight alternative places for ONE Moon, not eight moons; the always-visible caption says so. Every small Moon ball has its RIGHT half lit toward the light, regardless of selected position. Mark the selected position by outline and a visible “Selected place” label, not color alone. A line from Earth toward selected position is a VIEW direction, never a sunlight/shadow ray; its label says so. Do not put an eye/person at a special real latitude.

Geometry: drawing square340×340, Earth center(170,170), radius112 for position centers `(170+112*cos(i*pi/4),170-112*sin(i*pi/4))`. This is layout geometry only, with no distance/angle arithmetic labels. Original small Earth disk and eight constant right-half Moon disks; ring/selection-view guide in SVG, position controls native HTML buttons over their corresponding centers. Buttons min44×44 CSS pixels even at320; use percentages plus fixed minimum targets, no scaled44px SVG hit targets. Keep the board at least260px when parent allows; ensure no overlapping44px targets in actual phone layout (at260, adjacent centers about65px). Controls should remain within board bounds. Orbit figure has text description, buttons have finite location names and pressed state; all labels16px normal HTML, not tiny SVG type. Next/Previous place are native alternatives to tapping around the ring. No drag, auto-orbit, animation loop or clock.

The Earth-view disk is a qualitative phase illustration, not a photograph, lunar map or percent-measuring chart. Reuse existing `moonArt(code,orientation,...)` from sky-notes-core for the seven lit codes. Add only a source-owned dark disk for new: the Moon is still drawn, unlike323's dashed `not-seen` observation marker. Never add new to SKY_CODES or offer it as a saved observation. On northern-style pictures, growing is right-lit and shrinking left-lit; south mirrors only the Earth-view disk. Half-growing and half-shrinking are two different places with equal half-lit visible disks. Full/new are orientation invariant. `flip` never rotates the orbit diagram or changes the phase/amount. Existing323 shapes are schematic (crescent ellipse radius14 against disk radius20), so do not print exact illuminated percentages or claim precise geometry from their pixel areas.

When Earth view is hidden, omit its phase art, name and description entirely from DOM, aria and Hear; show only an unfilled question/prompt, not a dark disk that looks like new moon. The space diagram still shows the givens. No competing hidden phase/card is mounted. Show/Hide is an exploration aid, not an independently assessed prediction or evidence of a response. Teaching's example is separate and openly shown, never relabelled My Moon.

**Essential flattened-view caveat**, always visible in explore and teaching: sizes/distances are not to scale; the real Moon's path is tilted, so most new/full moons do not cause an eclipse; this flat model does not simulate eclipses. Do not render Earth-shadow cones or occlusion at aligned positions, and never say this proves eclipses cannot happen. The phase cause is how much of the sunlit half is visible. Earth's shadow causing a lunar eclipse is a separate event. The model's Sun-facing half is idealized, excluding eclipses and faint Earthshine. No real date, moonrise, weather, actual visibility, orbital-speed or sky-orientation prediction is made. A real Moon can appear tipped; north/south are simplified drawing styles, not a location or universal photograph.
