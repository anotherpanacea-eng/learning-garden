# 02 — Willow Post Office

**Status: First flying-courier increment implemented. Independent spec and implementation review completed; GitHub merge requires exact-candidate Claude clearance.**

## Goal and play loop

Make instructions useful to a reader. The explorer becomes a courier for Willow Post Office, with an original flying-witch option and independent guide/avatar choices. Pip and Dot help at the sorting counter. The child reads or hears the request, predicts where a draft message will lead, builds instructions by tapping word chunks, runs one instruction at a time, observes the literal result, and revises. Prediction, discussion, and transfer are optional and unscored; every delivery can be left for later.

## One game, developed in stages

**Sky Post is the flying-delivery part of Willow Post Office, not a separate destination or game.** The first slice uses an illustrated courier flying between the same four landmarks, one instruction at a time. No flight physics or reflex controls are required. Reading the order, packing parcels, planning the route and revising instructions form one delivery loop; math can enter through how many parcels or supplies the recipient needs.

Spec 25 adds girl witch, boy wizard and nonbinary sorcerer courier presets, a freely available ribbon example, and Bramble’s optional challenge route. A miss leads to short illustrated practice and a retry or another route, preserving the same parcel. Deliveries are the play; the earlier Play Center and play-credit proposal has been removed. [Spec 25](25-sky-post.md) records the implemented encounter and remaining expansion decisions. Use the same delivery catalog, map and `postOffice` save extension. Keep math attempts and structured-composition observations distinct within that shared state.

## Content minimum and behavior

Provide six changing deliveries across four named, connected landmarks, with an explicit start, recipient, and destination. Use a finite, reviewed grammar: “Go to [place]”, “Then go to [place]”, “Go there”, and “Give the parcel to [character]”, up to six clauses. “There” refers to the most recent named place in an earlier clause of the same message; without one, execution asks which place before moving. It never borrows a referent from another delivery. A character follows the stated route, highlights the active words, and stops with a specific clarification request when a recipient is absent or “there” lacks a referent. It never guesses intent. Direct routes and routes through optional waypoints are equally valid when they meet the request. Tap-to-add, remove, and move-up/down controls replace dragging. Rerunning resets the character, not the draft. Each delivery supplies a shorter visual example and optional paper-message invitation. Parent reading or dictation is welcome; no microphone is required.


## First release contract

Use four landmarks with all destinations reachable directly by flight: Willow Post Office, Almost-Right Café, Cloud Orchard and Lantern Hill. Dotted connections are possible flights, not roads or required waypoints. Use six original orders, all freely selectable and repeatable; starting one preserves earlier visits within the latest-24 bound. Each visit starts at the Post Office with its own recipient locations and frozen order snapshot. A character can be present at only one location in that visit. Original delivery recipients: Clover the bunny, Ember the dragon, Purl the kitten, Moss the gardener, Wren the bird and Dot the ladybug. Show each visitor's current location with text and pictures, and leave the request visible while composing.

Six order/packing examples: Clover at Café needs 5 seed buns plus 3 berry buns (8); 9 lantern ribbons are ready at the sorting counter, reserve 2 for repairs and deliver the remaining 7 to Ember at Lantern Hill; Purl at Cloud Orchard needs 3 baskets of 5 soft blankets (15); Moss at Cloud Orchard needs 4 bundles of 6 seed packets (24); Wren at Lantern Hill needs 6 blue flags plus 4 yellow flags (10); reserve 5 of the counter's 12 paper stars for its sign and deliver the remaining 7 to Dot at Café. These are finite authored math examples, not an adaptive multiplication library or fact assessment. Packing pictures must show the exact addends, visibly reserved/crossed-out removals or equal groups. Record number attempts separately from route checks. A correct count allows packing; an equally available "Pack together" explains the count and packs with support. Help and retries preserve the parcel and order. Route composition remains editable before packing; starting execution clearly requests packing if it is not yet done.

Instruction IDs encode go/place, then/place, there, and give/recipient. Any of the four named places and all six known recipients can be selected; incorrect deliveries ask a specific clarification and never transfer the parcel. "Then go" has the same movement semantics as "Go"; the word marks ordering and is not an extra condition. "There" uses the most recent earlier named go/then clause within this run, including a named stop at the current place. It does not change on a give clause, carry over from an earlier run, or use the order's printed destination as an implicit referent. Moving to the current place is a valid no-op. Only the requested recipient at the printed destination can accept the parcel; a different recipient present elsewhere does not silently receive it. On a valid handoff, stop execution and identify any remaining unused clauses. The number of route steps does not affect success, and reaching the destination without a give clause prompts adding one.

Start/restart runs from the visit's explicit start and empty referent; each "Fly the next instruction" button executes one clause, highlights it and updates the courier position. A clarification stops the run and leaves the route, parcel, packing and positions readable. Editing/reordering/removing a clause resets execution to the start without clearing the order, packing, math attempts, support or earlier checks. Restart leaves the draft intact. After a completed delivery, replay can inspect the same route without creating another completion or summary. A new visit is a new delivery; there is no reward balance in this release.

The first release offered flying witch and sky rider using the explorer portrait. Spec 25 extends these with pictured witch, wizard and sorcerer presets plus independent Post Office portrait/pronoun choices; all share identical competence and controls, and the garden explorer/guide stay separate. Legacy saves retain the explorer portrait until a courier override is chosen. Default to the witch. Use original SVG scenery and witch/broom art, manual positions and an optional short user-triggered flight transition respecting reduced motion; motion is never needed to follow a step. A visible text flight log and location labels provide equivalents. No borrowed film names, artwork or characters appear in the app.

Every order has a separate two-clause helper demonstration (Post Office → a different landmark → a different recipient), drawn in its own small scene. Help never moves the real courier or edits its draft and is always marked support. An optional prediction selects an expected final place without correctness scoring; retain whether it followed route execution/help. Local read-aloud can read the request, full route or help. Record the request for narration, not assumed playback. Separate reported reading modes: unobserved, together, independent; a later independent-reading report never erases narration/help flags and is never treated as demonstrated independent decoding. A paper-message invitation is unscored and never required.

## Save and validation details

Use `postOffice` version 1 with current visit ID, monotonically increasing next ID, selected courier style, and visits. Each visit includes content revision, frozen request/recipient-location/math snapshot, ordered clause IDs, optional prediction and after-support/run flag, packing input/attempts/support/packed state, route playback (cursor, location, referent, status, feedback, active clause), support flags, reading mode, revision count, up to 24 run-check snapshots, and a completion flag. Keep latest 24 visits, retaining the current one. Rendering starts no visit, step, narration or completion. Completion is recorded once per visit even if a route is rerun. Cross-destination invitations and explorer switching preserve exact pending work. A changed/unsupported content revision or malformed draft is retained as closed work with exact values, an explanation and a fresh-visit option; do not silently reinterpret it or drop it. Notes disclose retention and distinguish packing, route composition, support and reported reading. Reset/export/save-off use the existing explorer controls and storage keys.

If the whole `postOffice` schema version is unsupported or its root cannot be safely read, preserve the exact original payload in a clearly labelled `kept` field of a fresh version-1 container. Show a kept-work notice and include the complete payload in notes/export; do not reinterpret the old fields as current state. Starting a fresh visit must retain that payload. Test this path with a future-version paused draft and verify exact round-trip preservation. "There" is intentionally a modest local reference rule, not a broad pronoun or language assessment.
