## Optional try-it cards

Each subject has one short optional preview, independent of remembering a goal. A child may skip it, ask for help, change an answer, or choose any destination regardless of the result. This is an example and route suggestion, not a diagnosis or comprehensive assessment. Store raw choice, shown help and outcome separately, no score/percentile/mastery or global recommendation level.

- **Math:** pictured table x=0,1,2 with heights6,4,2 above/below a reference mark. “From x=0 to x=2, how does the height change?” Options up4 / down4 / down2, correct middle. Help follows height6to2; feedback explains change is4down and the new sequence teaches signed change/rate. No multiplication fact, no inferred fluency. Suggested optional bridge is Across zero; a fitting response may invite equations/plans, but neither route is locked.
- **English:** original preview: “The bell rang once at noon. Mara's log says she heard two strokes. Her entry was written the next morning.” Ask what the two statements let us say. Options “The log proves two strokes occurred” / “There is nothing here to compare” / “The statements disagree about the count”, correct third. One overclaim and one evidence-specific underclaim; balanced wording, not length/tone cues. Help distinguishes a disagreement from proving a narrator lied. Invite whole-story evidence work or two-account support; no reading-age inference.
- **Science:** show two opposite arrows, each4N, on one cart. Ask the net force, with0N /8Nright /4Nleft, correct first. Help explains opposition; feedback says zero net force means no acceleration in the model, not necessarily rest. Invite the force bridge or motion investigation. No physical experiment/safety claim.
- **French:** source “La lampe est sur la table. Le livre est sous la table.” Ask where the book is, using three small pictures/labels under/on/beside; correct under in varied position. French meaning is hidden until **Show English meaning**, recorded as support before reveal. A French Hear button records a request before playback; no English fallback or listening-quality inference. Invite room directions or Paris support independently from English/math.

Every preview freezes its complete question, pictured model/text, ordered options, key, hint, worked explanation, optional meanings and suggested route descriptions when explicitly started. After Check display the saved explanation and two route invitations with the reason tied only to this one example. A wrong response never automatically changes the remembered goal.
