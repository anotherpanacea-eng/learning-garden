## Why this is a new experience in the same game

[PR47](https://github.com/anotherpanacea-eng/learning-garden/pull/47) suggests designed parent–child information gaps. Use the existing Cartographer’s Table, where a child already authors and revises native direction commands. One role has the map; another knows a parcel’s required stop. Communicating changes the route that serves the job, rather than merely decorating another arithmetic question.

These are optional **Map reader / Dispatcher / Both clues** convenience views on one device. Take turns viewing cards and pass the device, or open both. Either card is always freely revealable. This is not secure secret information, proof that a partner participated, a two-device service or a social-skills assessment. Solo play with both clues is complete. Any participant can take either role; no names, age, gender, relationship, partner profile, recording, typed conversation or private disclosure is requested. Speaking, signing, AAC, pointing and discussion are welcome and unobserved.

The [source audit](../source-audits/61-map-reader-dispatcher.md) refreshes Jane/Marble and retains the existing narrow coordinate/route references. Role labels do not establish listening, cooperation or communication proficiency. All jobs and the town map are original fiction. No new shell, source pin, economy, API, learner model, attainment label or research-effect claim.

## Complete original job content

All three jobs use this supplied intersection map: Post Office **(1,1)**, Bridge **(4,1)**, Café **(4,4)**, Garden **(1,4)**. Across first, then up; north is up, east right. Each adjacent interval is one fictional model metre. The six labelled point markers from0–5 span five intervals. No physical map scale or real-world geography claim.

**Map-reader card**, identical in all three packs: “I have the town map. Post Office is at (1,1), Bridge at (4,1), Café at (4,4), and Garden at (1,4). Read across first, then up. North is up and east is right. Each move travels one interval.” Show the corresponding labelled static grid and key. These are points, not covered plots. The other role can always be opened; do not infer memorisation or independent decoding from a hidden card.

**Job1, Collect the market sign** (`sign`): Dispatcher says, **“Start at Post Office. Collect the market sign at Bridge, then bring it to Café.”** Required start Post, stop Bridge, destination Café. EEE NNN serves it in6 intervals. NNN EEE also arrives at Café in6, but misses Bridge. That contrast is the reason to ask about the job, not a required route or shortest-path answer.

**Job2, Bring welcome flowers** (`flowers`): **“Start at Post Office. Collect the welcome flowers at Garden before taking them to Café.”** Start Post, stop Garden, destination Café. NNN EEE serves it in6; EEE NNN reaches Café but misses Garden.

**Job3, Carry the garden notice** (`notice`): **“Start at Garden. Collect the notice at Post Office, then take it to Bridge.”** Start Garden, stop Post, destination Bridge. SSS EEE serves it in6; EEE SSS reaches Bridge via Café and misses Post.

All supported alternative routes within12 commands are welcome, including detours/retracing. Deliver only when the command list finishes. Merely walking past the destination earlier does not deliver anything: Job1 NNN EEE SSS NNN is12 intervals, visits Café, then Bridge, then finishes at Café, and serves the request. Do not wrongly require the stop before the first destination pass or the author’s preferred6-step route. No shortest-route badge or scarce stock is involved.

The independent content reviewer must inspect all three requests, map-reader wording, geometry, equal-arrival counterexamples and the earlier-destination-pass case before build.

## Minimal state extension, frozen input and recorded output

Reuse optional `cartographer` version1 and its12-readable-map/8-check rules. Add optional `round.job` version1: `source` frozen pack and `view:map|dispatch|both`. Pack: version1, stable id≤60, title≤120, mapReaderText≤800, request≤600, places (all four known distinct bounded points), from/via/to three distinct known mark IDs. Every explicit job start copies its complete original pack; rendering/resuming never refreshes it from today’s catalog. Default view is both, for fully open solo access. This is practice, not a fresh assessment; do not introduce a familiarity/mastery model or infer partner participation from view choices.

A live job sheet’s places/from/to must agree with **its own frozen source**, not with today’s catalog. This is an input-coherence rule for a fixed supplied task, distinct from regrading old output. The normal geometry commands and support rules remain. `placeMapMark` and `chooseMapEndpoint` refuse edits for a job sheet without mutation; ordinary sheets retain their existing behavior. Role changes update only job.view; never rewrite frozen source, routes, checks, support or base page. Unknown fields remain intact. A present unreadable/future job makes that sheet read-only in the existing earlier-work path, retaining valid siblings; it must not be silently treated as a normal task and praised without understanding its request. Future/invalid whole envelopes retain the existing read-only behavior.

New explicit checks on a job sheet add optional `check.job`: frozen `source` and separate A/B records `{visited:boolean,firstVisit:integer0..12|null,fulfilled:boolean}`. `visited` means the required stop appears anywhere in the **actually travelled** point list, including its final point. `firstVisit` records that first index; otherwise null. `fulfilled` separately requires every command completed, the final endpoint is the requested destination, and firstVisit is strictly before the final point index. A route ending at the required stop has really visited it even when it cannot serve the delivery. This small semantic fold passed separate targeted review. A blocked prefix may have visited the stop but does not serve the job. No stop is inferred from adjacency, command intent, an untravelled suffix or destination arrival alone. Compute from the current fixed task and actual `walkMap` output only on an explicit new check.

Old checks without job facts remain readable and say **“This earlier check did not record the job’s required stop”** if shown alongside a job; do not regenerate their job outcome. New/historical job records validate only source/output shapes and local ranges. Do not compare stored visited/firstVisit/fulfilled with regenerated routes, current source wording or today's catalog. Even contradictory historical booleans remain recorded results. Their displayed clue text comes from that check’s own source, alongside its own frozen map/commands. Current/earlier labels compare inputs only; role-view changes do not make a route check stale. Catalog updates never relabel an unfinished original job.

New map IDs, readable/raw sibling retention, exact capacity disclosure including oversized imports, unsafe-id refusal, ordinary export/import/save-off/explorer switching and views-without-trimming use the existing map rules. Starting a job is the same explicit new-sheet operation, so disclose how many oldest readable sheets it removes before the click. Do not call `startMap` and then patch a failed allocation or quietly evict twice. A failed start must be nonmutating and give truthful feedback; ordinary old maps remain usable. No new universal state, task-source or workflow registry is needed.
