## Teaching and story job

Extend the existing `#words/props` room, not another destination. Dot is readying the library theater’s props for a small show: a cap, a folding cot and a pretend map need labels. The child and an adult supply the voices. This continues spec120’s Act I mend-the-village purpose without adding a chapter contribution, page reveal, unlock or film. Any arrival can enter; previous labels and every other room remain open.

The target is an adult-supported investigation of initial, medial and final sound changes, spoken-word segmentation/letter construction, print-first reading attempts and meaning in a short shared message. It is not an automated observation of hearing, reading, independence, pronunciation, spelling ability or unfamiliarity. Changing one letter is not a universal rule for changing one phoneme: these particular reviewed CVC examples support the contrast in the declared General American reference. Respect other accents; the app hears nothing.

Spec49 §5 encounter: teach with three-position demonstrations and a worked tin/pin comparison; contrast first/middle/last changes; practice listening/building or print-first exploration on four open cards; new use in a shared prop-delivery message; return by choosing a different card/mode or comparing an earlier kept arrangement. E1-I/II; later reading/writing bridges stay separate. Short e/u and remaining consonant correspondence teaching are the scheduled next foundation series, not silently supplied here. This does not commission250 or recorded phonics.

1. **Arrival:** add “Try a sound-changing label” to the existing Prop Box chooser. Entry opens a four-card chooser with generic titles/icons, never target-word pictures or labels. Opening the chooser alone does not allocate case work. A generic opening explains the adult’s role without naming any target; omit that story entirely from the active listen/build page. Worked teaching/examples are available inside an explicitly allocated case, never from the unallocated chooser.
2. **Choose how to try:** every case offers “Listen and build” and “Look and read.” A case is allocated on explicit mode choice, freezing its own source. Entering another case never erases an unfinished draft. There is no mandatory order or requirement to succeed before another mode/message opens.
3. **Listen and build:** show “Pass this to your reading partner” and **Show partner card**. Before that press, no target word or picture is rendered. The partner card explicitly displays the right-hand target and right.guidance. **Hide card and build** removes it and opens three empty/current letter places and the letter bank. The partner says the word naturally, helps isolate three sounds if needed, and hears the child; none of that performance is captured. When all slots are empty, **Start with the first word** can copy the retained left word into them, setting help.base before display; it cannot replace a nonempty draft. The child can then change one or more letters, rather than always building from nothing. This is a supplied starting model, not self-generated spelling. The child can tap a place then a letter, clear a place/all, reveal the word, reveal its picture, compare the arrangement, keep it unfinished or visit another case. Do not prefill the answer. The currently built letters remain visible and usable.
4. **Look and read:** display the target’s complete lowercase printed word before its picture/meaning. Invite a try with the partner, then **Show picture**. Never offer “I read it correctly.” **Build this label** opens the same draft with word-help already disclosed; it must not pretend this is an unseen dictated attempt. Partner and child may choose this mode directly without previewing the partner card.
5. **Compare:** an explicit **Compare my label** reveals the retained target word beside the literal arrangement. Say only that it matches the card, differs from it, or has empty places; never “correct reading.” Keep the arrangement unchanged. Unexpected complete arrangements can still be kept, including when no authored picture exists. No dictionary lookup, invented definition or rejection for making a different word. Editing hides an obsolete comparison; saved snapshots of older comparisons remain as originally made, not regraded.
6. **Change one sound:** explicit help opens the pair, with three printed positions on each side and the named changed position highlighted by shape/text. Manual Previous/Next cycles the whole left word, the differing position, and the whole right word. The adult says both naturally. Opening this panel sets help.word and help.contrast before displaying its pair. This is disclosed comparison help, not a hidden-answer test. Close returns to the waiting draft. No timed acoustic/letter animation, sound concatenation or clap count.
7. **Keep:** permit incomplete or unexpected material. First Keep is immutable; later Keep replaces latest only. Explicit “Use these kept letters” restores the chosen snapshot’s slots without erasing current help or first/latest snapshots. A quiet acknowledgment says the label was kept for the show. No other room receives a label or supplies stock. A matching label is a prop-card match, not oral evidence. Keeping all readable offered cases may show “Our labels are ready to revisit”; it must not count opaque/unreadable cases as complete. No video or mastery milestone.
8. **Carry the message:** always-open “Read the show message” displays the frozen message with target tokens marked and every other token adult-supplied. It has one oral meaning question, ungraded and unstored. The text and picture can be revisited at any time; nothing is sent to Post Office/Story Loft. A plain link to Story Loft remains an invitation only.

Each card retains its mode, slots, active place, page, disclosed help and manual comparison frame. record.page has distinct setup, partner, work, comparison, contrast and example values: Compare my label opens comparison; Change one sound opens contrast; Show an example opens example. Every close/Back to my label returns to work without clearing material/help. Mode choice closes any help page, returning to setup for listen or work for read. Reload resumes that exact page. frame belongs to contrast/example manual steps; opening either starts at0, step buttons clamp0–2, and closing retains its last frame until the next explicit help opening. Returning/reloading resumes the actual turn; no auto-clear or automatic next question. Selecting a new mode in an existing case keeps its draft and cumulative disclosures. **A different mode does not create a new independent attempt.** These are familiar repeatable cases, not unlimited new curriculum.
