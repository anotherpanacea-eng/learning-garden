## Version1 clock mathematics and original art

Represent a12-hour clock face by `slot:integer0..23`, one slot per30minutes; slot0=12:00,1=12:30,2=1:00, through23=11:30. These24 slots are **not24hours**. Digital labels use h:mm with hours1..12 and minutes00/30. A leading-zero hour may be admitted in a historical supplied label if it denotes the same slot. Do not reinterpret30 as decimal hundredths.

Coupled geometry, degrees clockwise from12: short hour hand=`slot*15`; long minute hand=`(slot%2)*180`. At9:30 the minute hand points6 and hour hand lies halfway from9 toward10. At10:00 the minute hand points12 and hour hand10. Use an original clear circular face with12 numerals/ticks, unequal hand lengths and visible hand-name labels/legend; colour is supplementary. No copied clock art. Show a fixed preparation figure on Read and a separately labelled **Our model / Proposed departure** figure on Set. Do not label a wrong model position as the true departure.

Native **Back30minutes / Forward30minutes / Back1hour / Forward1hour** change model position by−1/+1/−2/+2 slots, modulo24. They automatically coordinate both hands. Only actual supported positions are available; no dragging, hover or independent minute/hour controls. No shortest-path or fewest-click scoring. There is no timed animation: manual snapshots must not suggest the minute hand runs backward on a forward wrap. Optional supplied step diagrams/text may show the stated30/60-minute relationship. One full minute-hand turn corresponds to an hour-hand advance of one numeral; half a turn is30minutes. The app does not measure30 physical minutes.

Model movement can pass12, but it is **face exploration**, not movement of a real appointment/day/timeline. A face position alone cannot identify morning/afternoon or elapsed cycles. Source cards avoid noon/midnight; no a.m./p.m.,24-hour notation, day conversion, arbitrary five-minute/nearest-minute readings, seconds, stopwatch measurement, independent duration/endpoint calculation, timetables or rates are assessed. Broader travel planning follows separately.

Every source freezes the accessible description of its fixed preparation picture: e.g. hour hand at9, minute hand at12; for9:30, hour hand between9/10, minute hand6. Do not remove useful accessible text to create an appearance of independent assessment. Narration/description can supply spatial clues. For known v1 working pictures, a deterministic slot-to-hand description is a mathematical view of the model; saved checks freeze their exact displayed labels/descriptions.
