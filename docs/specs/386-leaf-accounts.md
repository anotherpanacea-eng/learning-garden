## The new operation and boundary

Build a **net chemical summary** by changing molecule counts, inspect an atom account on each side, then give a separate energy arrow its two captions. Unlike374's cell-part selection or379's gas-path labels, the learner constructs quantities and checks conservation. Unlike308/372, the energy account is qualitative and paired with a chemical matter account; there are no invented joules, efficiencies or plant-growth predictions. Unlike41, these are explanatory models, not measurements of seedlings. A source reference is separate from the child's construction; empty, unfinished, unbalanced and unexpected constructions are keepable.

The narrow slice uses photosynthesis and aerobic cellular respiration as two independently authored summaries. It does not derive one by reversing the other. They involve different mechanisms, enzymes and locations. It does not teach all respiration stages, ATP stoichiometry, electron transport, limiting factors, leaf anatomy, fertilizer quantities, isotope experiments or a complete carbon cycle. No physical experiment, chemicals, body measurement, microphone, external AI or new media production.

This is beyond a word-only plant-needs encounter: optional spoken names and molecule pictures support entry, while literal formulae and atom arithmetic introduce a more advanced representation. Do not claim independent balancing ability or reading proficiency from a kept construction.

## Teaching encounter49 §5

1. **Teach:** a whole molecule stays together when its coefficient changes. Two water molecules are two groups, each with two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom; they are not one new molecule with a rewritten formula. One tapped explanation at a time shows this with two separate group outlines. Formula names and atom legend always available.
2. **Contrast:** matter molecules versus the separate light/chemical-energy arrow; balanced counts versus evidence that a real process happens; photosynthesis versus respiration. All-zero totals are arithmetically equal but describe no molecules. A doubled balanced summary has the same ratio, not a faster measured plant.
3. **Supported practice:** build the sunlit-leaf summary. Optional reference counts and explanation frames remain explicit support, never a newly independent attempt. Atom totals are shown only after **Count atoms**; controls clear that comparison when edited. No grading of caption choices.
4. **New use:** a living leaf respires in light and darkness; a selected non-green root cell respires using transported sugars. Change molecule counts for the respiration summary and choose the energy captions. The root has no chloroplast in this selected example; the leaf has mitochondria as well as chloroplasts.
5. **Return:** carry an elected frozen construction back to the selected cell illustrations. Which parts help explain the two processes? The record does not verify the learner's cell drawing or establish plant growth. Link to Seedlings after the storm to distinguish a mechanism model from measured/supplied observations.

Species counts per molecule: carbon dioxide C1 O2; water H2 O1; glucose C6 H12 O6; oxygen O2. Formula display is generated from the saved atom counts, in C,H,O order, omitting zero and omitting subscript1. These are composition diagrams, not molecular shapes or bonds. Every current reference gives **C6 H12 O18 on each side**. A doubled photosynthesis summary12,12,2,12 gives C12 H24 O36 on each side and remains within the controls' range. Repeated atoms are counted, not weighted: atom counts are not masses or amounts in grams.

Photosynthesis is a simplified **net** summary: carbon dioxide plus water, with light energy, form carbohydrates represented here by glucose plus oxygen. The cell does not make a whole glucose molecule in a single step. Net water use omits water made and reused along the pathway. The oxygen gas released in oxygenic photosynthesis comes from water; the net equation alone does not trace which individual oxygen atoms follow which route. Do not animate atom identities from carbon dioxide into oxygen gas, or suggest that each oxygen molecule becomes a carbon dioxide molecule in respiration or call the coefficients a measured rate. A source note states these limits in short child-facing language; deeper detail belongs under About this model.

Aerobic respiration is a net sugar/oxygen-to-carbon-dioxide/water summary. Some energy is transferred for cell activity via ATP and some to surroundings as heat; ATP/ADP and intermediate reactions are not represented in this net matter account. Glycolysis starts in cytoplasm; the mitochondrial icon must not imply every step occurs inside it. Do not say breaking a bond alone releases energy, that energy is created/destroyed, that plants respire only at night, or that sunlight turns into matter. A balanced equation alone proves neither mechanism nor rate nor sufficiency of conditions.

Soil supplies water and mineral nutrients; sugars supply much of the material used to build plant structures, and the carbon in those sugars comes from carbon dioxide. Do not infer that soil is irrelevant, that all plant mass is carbon or that the present count board measures where all plant mass comes from. No willow-pot/soil-mass experiment or unsupported causal conclusion.
