## Learning and play

The café needs flour; the miller asks where its mill's energy goes. A large original illustrated mill and three-store energy book share one set of controls. Pour a portion, grind a step, let the wheel rub without grinding, or close the gate. The wheel can still drive a step after the gate closes because it has kinetic energy. Energy transferred during grinding or separate rubbing appears in thermal accounting; it does not disappear or become the food's chemical energy. A tracked-total marker stays visible. All actions and destinations remain open without scores, timing, rewards or mastery claims.

Eighteen finite requests use three freely selectable batch sizes (8/12/16 chips), with six request families each. Choosing a size keeps that size's six partial pictures and the other twelve; it changes the current index to the same family in the selected size. Scene/Energy book, one optional kept picture and open explanations are shared across requests. The request chooser is a separate turn, preserving all work. Nothing is locked behind a request. Next request visits the next unmet family cyclically in this size, or the chooser if all six were met. Six met requests produce a quiet local milestone and **Start six fresh requests**, which explicitly replaces only this size's six pictures. All other sizes, representation, kept picture and explanations remain. This is participation in supplied model tasks, not evidence of independent explanation.

Keep any picture, including its starting state, for comparison. A kept picture is an immutable copy of size/counts/gate and request identity, not a pointer into the active work. Display current and kept totals separately; different batch sizes do not isolate one cause. Start this picture again resets only the current ledger/gate/history-of-closed-grinding, while retaining its earlier met flag and the shared kept picture. Explain when an earlier picture met a request but the current picture does not, whether after continued exploration or a restart. Leaving the route, reload, explorer chooser/change, notebook import/selection or global reset discards the visit only. One visit owns `{book,owner,model,details}`; model owns `current:0..17`, `page:'work'|'jobs'`, `view:'scene'|'book'`, optional `held`, and18 work entries. Fresh defaults: current=0,page='work',view='scene',held=null,details={}. No persisted schema/validator or generic engine.

Each work has `waiting W`, `moving K`, `grinding G`, `rubbing A` (integers), `gate` (open boolean), `closedGrind`, historical `done`, and request-owned `lastAction:null|'pour'|'grind'|'rub'|'gate'|'restart'`. Initially W=N,K=G=A=0, gate open, flags false. Thermal increment **T=G+A**; tracked total **W+K+T=N**. G/A are bookkeeping tags for energy transferred while doing two processes, not extra stores to add again or measured useful/wasted efficiency. All reachable values are0..N. One chip denotes an equal unspecified energy amount, not a joule, water molecule, temperature, grain mass or physical particle. A portion is assigned two chips in this toy; no real volume, head, mass, angular speed or yield is predicted.

## Science boundary and teaching copy

Use [exact308 text](../content/308-mill-energy-book-text.md). Main labels are **Waiting · gravitational**, **Moving · kinetic**, **Warmth added · thermal**, and **Tracked total**. The gravitational store belongs to raised water and Earth; the kinetic store is the wheel. Thermal entries include the stones, flour, axle and nearby surroundings. The total is the extra energy budget associated with this batch, not all pre-existing energy of the Earth/room. Restart/fresh-six begins a different tracked picture, not destruction of earlier real thermal energy.

The model is paused between taps. Every action advances one bookkeeping step, not an equal time, force, rotation or real mill operation. Scene motion arrows mark motion at that frozen moment; K>0 means moving even when the illustration is paused. Closing the gate does not remove K. A grinding step includes warming during grinding/rubbing; G is not pure useful output. A separate rubbing step models motion without grinding. Pour is idealized; real water transfer has additional losses. Real grinding also has small effects such as new grain surfaces; this simplified book lumps the tracked output as eventual thermal increments. No exact temperature rise, physical efficiency, power, Sankey, universal no-heat-recovery or perpetual-motion proof. The child line says spread-out warmth does not set the wheel turning again by itself; conservation-alone limitations stay in grown-up copy. No force is stored as a push, no light/current/sound is a store, and grinding does not provide flour's chemical food energy.

Always show the brief paused-model/chip boundary; put fuller distinctions in expandable model/why/source sections. An optional separate8-chip worked example shows8/0/0→6/2/0→6/1/1→6/0/2 (thermal's grinding/rubbing tags1+1 at end). Reading it leaves the active picture untouched.
