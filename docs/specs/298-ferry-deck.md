## Need, operation and boundary

RouteB1/B2 needs the same motion described in different reference frames. Existing force bridge and collision units already teach signed net force, acceleration, constant velocity and velocity change; Gap Line distinguishes signed positions, changes and unsigned gaps. Another force playground would duplicate them. None lets the learner keep one physical moment fixed while changing the observer, then watch one second of that same journey from another frame.

Switching view changes coordinates and relative velocities, never the underlying event, world velocities or clock. ## Exact six crossings

World coordinates use meters relative to a fixed bank marker at0; right is positive. Clock t is an integer second0..6 inclusive. For every case, ferry origin starts at−4m and other-boat origin at+6m. Bank marker x=0,v=0. Let f be ferry velocity relative to bank, w courier velocity relative to ferry and o other-boat velocity relative to bank. Courier's initial local offset d is measured from the ferry's origin. Then:

- `xF=-4+f*t`, `vF=f`;
- `xO=6+o*t`, `vO=o`;
- `xC=-4+d+(f+w)*t`, `vC=f+w`;
- courier's deck coordinate is `d+w*t`.

| id / title | f (m/s) | w (m/s) | o (m/s) | d (m) | Contrast |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| riding / Riding together |2|0|1|2|Courier moves relative to bank, stays at one position relative to ferry.|
| with-ferry / Walking with the ferry |2|1|−1|2|Courier-bank+3; courier-ferry+1; courier-other+4.|
| against-ferry / Walking toward the back |2|−1|1|8|Courier-bank+1 but courier-ferry−1; courier-other0.|
| side-by-side / Boats together |2|0|2|2|Both boats move relative to bank but their relative velocity is0.|
| opposite-boats / Passing the other way |1|0|−2|2|Ferry relative to other boat+3, not−1.|
| hold-bank / Walking to keep a place |−1|1|0|2|Courier-bank0 while courier-ferry+1. Bank and other boat share velocity but have different origins.|

Ferry's deck occupies local−1..+9m, a10m platform. Courier remains between2 and8m throughout all seven moments, including the walking-back case. Other boat is drawn in a parallel lane; equal horizontal coordinates do not mean collision. Vertical lanes/sizes are schematic, not measured. No water feature moves as a supposed current; no hydrodynamic claim is made.

For selected observer a in bank/ferry/other, compute `q_i=x_i-x_a`, `u_i=v_i-v_a` for all four named objects. Same clock and same meter scale in every view. At the observer's origin q_a=0 and u_a=0. Distinct origins with equal velocities give equal relative velocity accounts but shifted positions: they are not two physically different motions. Relative speed is `abs(u_i)`; signed velocity also includes direction. Do not subtract unsigned speeds as a general rule. Positive position does not imply rightward velocity. Over one second, `q_i(t+1)-q_i(t)=u_i`; source/world pairwise separations are invariant under view switching.

At6s there is no next clock step; finite clipping is not a stopping event. The supplied velocities still describe the motion at that moment, not a forced halt. Say the little model ends there. Reset returns this case to t0/bank with optional readings/explanation hidden.
