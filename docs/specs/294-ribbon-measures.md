This is not the first fraction division in the game. Missing: construct how many copies of one fractional length measure another, including a fractional final group, without stretching either length or confusing the reference units.

One open native-control material board at `#workshop/ribbon-measures`, linked from Workshop and Common Cuts292. The reusable measuring strip is a ruler/template, not consumable ribbon stock. Copies of its pieces mark a fixed ribbon; subdividing a measure never creates more ribbon.

## Six finite cases and exact units

The original whole is one abstract length unit. Every case has a fixed ribbon amount A and a fixed whole measuring-group length B in those original units. The quotient counts groups, not original units, visible pieces or complete deliverable objects. No cups, money, food or physical cutting/safety promise.

| ID | Ribbon A | One group B | Target group count | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| unit-quarter | 1 unit | 1/4 unit | 4 groups | Whole÷unit-fraction bridge; smaller measuring units yield more groups, not more ribbon. |
| three-quarters | 3/4 unit | 1/4 unit | 3 groups | Same group, smaller ribbon. |
| eighth-group | 3/4 unit | 3/8 unit | 2 groups | Non-unit-fraction divisor; compare to previous amount. |
| partial-group | 1/2 unit | 3/4 unit | 2/3 group | Less than one group;2 third-group pieces fit. |
| mixed-groups | 5/6 unit | 1/3 unit | 5/2 groups =2 1/2 groups | Two complete groups plus half a group; original-unit gap1/6 is half this group. |
| beyond-one | 3/2 units | 3/4 unit | 2 groups | Amount past one original whole. |

Initial live case is unit-quarter, empty construction and unsplit group. Switching cases retains each case’s pieces, template cuts and optional-readout-open flag during this visit. No progression requirement or automatic advance. Numeric targets in this spec are model truth, not prefilled learner answers.

## Material actions and conservation

The template starts as one full group (q=1). “Split each piece in 2” or “Split each piece in 3” subdivides the template equally. Factor history uses only2/3, product q must remain≤6, giving only q∈{1,2,3,4,6}. No fifths/eighths/ninths of a group are offered by this finite board; this is a board limit, not mathematical impossibility. “Undo last split” removes the latest factor. Split/Undo never change B, the original unit, A, or already laid pieces. Explain that previously laid pieces keep their sizes even when the reusable template changes. Label Undo last split separately from Lift last piece.

“Lay one piece” places a copy of the currently selected1/q-group piece end to end after existing pieces, only if it fits wholly before or exactly at the ribbon’s fixed endpoint. “Lift last piece” returns exactly the last placed piece, regardless of the current template cuts. “Return all pieces” empties this construction but leaves its chosen template cuts intact. No gap, overlap, automatic clipping, rounding, overshoot, resize, snapping, forced solution path or lost piece. The model is a reusable measurement mark, not a depleting inventory.

Store each laid piece’s own q; changing the template does not reinterpret it. Covered C=Σ(B/q_i); gap=A−C≥0. Current group count=C/B=Σ1/q_i, never the raw count of pieces. A full measurement is exactly C=A. Example: for A=1/2,B=3/4, two1/3-group pieces and four1/6-group pieces are both valid;1/4+1/4+1/6 group also measures the same amount. For5/6 measured by1/3, two whole groups plus one half group is valid. There is no canonical partition/history answer to grade.

Use exact internal coordinates with original unit288, ruler0…576 (two original units). A and B are their exact rational values times288. All reachable piece lengths B/q are integers, including108/4=27. Maximum24 laid pieces follows from these finite amounts and the smallest reachable piece; it is not a separate arbitrary failure gate. Coordinates are a private implementation choice, not a saved schema or visible count of tiny squares. Fractions/readouts derive from exact integer ratios using reduced forms. Format original-unit amounts below1 as “a/b of one length unit”, exactly1 as “1 length unit”, zero as “0 length units”, and amounts above1 in mixed form (“1 1/2 length units”); no improper-length alias is required. Format a group count below1 as “a/b of one measuring group”, exactly1 as “1 measuring group”, and above1 in mixed form; a noninteger group count greater than1 also shows the equivalent improper fraction (“2 1/2 measuring groups (5/2 groups)”). Empty constructions have the no-pieces invitation rather than a displayed zero quotient. At q=1 say “one whole measuring group”, never “1/1 of a group”. For the piece tally, group counts by each q in fixed ascending order1,2,3,4,6: “N whole-group pieces”, “N half-group pieces”, “N third-group pieces”, “N quarter-group pieces”, “N sixth-group pieces”; omit zero counts and use singular “piece” at N=1. Tally physical pieces separately from their group-count sum. Every dynamic quantity phrase includes its own unit noun; templates never append a duplicate fixed noun. At q=1 Undo says the measuring group is whole again, not1 equal pieces. Simplification is supplied, not an entry requirement.

Lay is disabled at a fully measured ribbon or when the next piece would overshoot. Give an adjacent reason. Full coverage: “The ribbon is fully measured; lift or return a piece to try another way.” For a positive gap smaller than B/6, no offered piece fits: “The gap is[gap-group-phrase], smaller than any piece this board offers. Use Lift last piece to try another way.” This reachable exploration is allowed, not a failed answer; Lift remains available. In this finite model it is a1/12-group gap, including the partial-group path1/4+1/3 group. Show the exact original-unit gap in HTML even when its drawing is tiny; never round it to full coverage. For other overshoots, advise splitting only if a fitting smaller piece is reachable by further legal splits from the current cuts. If a smaller offered piece fits but reaching it requires Undo (e.g. q=4 with a1/6-group gap), explicitly invite Undo last split to change the template. Offer Lift as an alternative only when pieces have been laid. At an empty unsplit partial-group case, invite splitting, not an unavailable Lift. A fitting smaller piece is not promised to finish the gap; mixed paths can require further changes. Do not say that division has no answer, that no group fits when a fractional group does, or display a floor-only result as the quotient. At the finite cut limit, disabled split controls explain the offered whole/half/third/quarter/sixth choices and invite Undo; keep other actions available.

## Readouts and completion

Always distinguish: ribbon amount A in original length units; one complete group B in original length units; selected piece1/q of a group and B/q in original units; covered C and remaining gap in original units. Show “No pieces laid yet” for an empty construction, not a success or a division answer of zero. If incomplete, say “The ribbon is not fully measured yet.” Do not announce the target quotient as a result before the construction covers A.

When fully covered, supply “This measures all of the ribbon ([A-length-phrase]). Together these pieces make[total-group-phrase]. Each whole measuring group is[B-length-phrase].” Supply Q×B=A with labels identifying group count and length per group; it is an interpretation of the actual construction, not a submitted answer. Show a compact tally of pieces by their own q (e.g.2 quarter-group pieces; 1 sixth-group piece), separately from the reduced total number of groups. A correct supplied label or completion of this finite model is not independent explanation, fluency, readiness or mastery. No celebratory badge, score, timed reward, penalty or access gate.

“Read the measurement” is an optional explicit button: reveals the current group count C/B, compact piece tally and inverse check for C, even while incomplete; keep “not fully measured” qualification until C=A. Any live Split, Undo, Lay, Lift or Return hides that case’s optional readout until requested again, even when the covered length is unchanged. Case selection and the separate worked example preserve the stored flag for each case. This gives support without writing an attempt or installing pieces. A full construction automatically receives the factual full-coverage line above but no assessment status. The piece tally and inverse check are supplied by explicit Read; they do not appear automatically. Nonzero tally categories are separated by semicolons. Empty Read invites Lay only when the selected piece B/q fits; otherwise it gives the current fitting/repair guidance. No quotient result for an unmeasured target. Empty main instructions use selected B/q, not whether the whole group B fits; cuts persist through Return.
