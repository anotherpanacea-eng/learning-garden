Reuse original tasks, pictures and support rather than create a shared mastery score or duplicate observation ledger. First release connects equal-group fact families in both directions among Living Garden, café and Bramble. ## Small, explicit connection

A read-only adapter finds the latest usable source within each game’s own retained history; IDs from different games are never compared as a global clock. Sources: closed known v2 garden visits with actual parcel responses or a worked explanation; served/skipped café orders with actual total responses (unanswered skips are excluded); terminal generated Bramble multiplication rounds with an actual response or solve-together resolution (bypass-only is excluded). “Latest” follows retained completion/append order, never allocation-ID order (Bramble rounds can finish out of order). Do not use legacy/unknown or malformed source records. Familiarity is unknown for café/Bramble work that never froze it; absence in a trimmed history/exposure set does not establish novelty. Retain source, local ID, relation, quantities, response/support/retry category and familiarity in a small frozen provenance object on the newly started target task. Do not copy attempts into another game, claim independent recall, or count imported evidence toward garden depth changes. Starting a connected garden visit preserves the automatic math suggestion and epoch; only that target’s later actual responses may count, when its effective depth equals the automatic depth, under the existing support/familiarity rules.

Each destination has an optional “Carry an idea here” card for the other two sources, with a specific pictured equal-groups proposal. Recent retries/supported work suggest the same fact family with different objects; a first-response match without scoped support suggests one neighboring fact. This is a practice suggestion, never evidence of a general level. All ordinary new orders/searches/rival puzzles remain unchanged, including Bramble’s fair full-range rotation and explicit table choices. A connection is a one-off, explicitly chosen task; it does not change those preferences or automatic depth suggestions.

Garden inverse work supplies its original packs×bites family, labelled a related direction rather than a repeat of the inverse question. Tomorrow work supplies the actual hypothetical (packs−1)×bites family; a zero-pack plan connects to one pack as an explicitly related positive example, never a disguised identical task. A nearby proposal increases the first factor if below 12, otherwise the second if below 12, otherwise uses 11×12. The entire target stays within ordered 1–12 facts. Show source and proposed quantities and the reason in the card; do not expose an unsolved target’s answer.

Connected café: start a real next guest with the proposed guest/bite counts, preserving the normal fictional guest/wishes selection and actual plate, total, stock and serve loop. Do not replace a waiting order, change stock, award a meal or advance the supper at start. A source’s café parcel is still ordinary inventory, consumed only by serving under existing rules. Connected Bramble: start a real generated multiplication round at the current delivery with the proposed bundles/ribbons; preserve flight, parcel, helper behavior, optional methods and archiving. Do not replace an unresolved encounter. If there is no current Post Office delivery, invite the child to choose an order first; no invisible delivery is created.

Connected Living Garden: start a real v2 search using the proposed packs/bites with a one-off **equal-packs** math form, clearly named in the button/card. Science and note depth still follow their separate existing choices/suggestions. Preserve automatic math depth, manual choice and quantity cursor rotation for ordinary visits; connected tasks at another depth do not move the automatic suggestion. Do not replace an open original/renewable visit. Preserve stock/ingredient choice/case rules; all-facts quantities are used for this one search when outside the small pool. Shared product exposure is familiar, distinct from independently answering a new question form.

Outgoing links on a finished source invite the other destinations; arrival displays that source’s offer. No URL payload or persistent pending queue is needed. If a target has waiting work, link to continue it and explain that the connection can start afterward. The source remains in its existing bounded notebook; no claim of permanent availability after it ages out. Repeated ordinary clicks cannot replace open work or duplicate food.

## Evidence, state and compatibility

Use known source records read-only, with conservative classification: any retry remains a retry; response-time garden support includes immutable together support, and later together reports remain support. Café’s older coarse support is conservatively scoped to its whole total task. Bramble’s existing support is conservative and monotonic. Familiarity does not become independent success. A supported source does not fabricate target help; the target records its own actual attempts/help. Connection provenance identifies a previously explored or related fact; it is not an assessment result at the target.

Add optional versioned provenance on new garden tasks, café orders/history and generated Bramble rounds. Preserve the café quantity cursor and the garden quantity cursors on a one-off connection. One optional lifetime connected-round count in Bramble keeps inserted connections from consuming its ordinary operation/new-example turns; this is a selection counter, not a second observation ledger. Legacy records without it keep their exact shape and continue normally. Validate known provenance/target quantities when used; unknown metadata remains raw/exportable and is not used as a source or invitation. New connected starts refuse malformed target work instead of resetting it. Failed starts are atomic; use a temporary copy to validate before assigning the affected destination root. Rendering/notes never normalize café state or advance IDs/cursors. No new network, identity fields, analytics or learner-data transfer. Existing explorer switching, export, reset and save-off apply.

Grown-up notes explain the source, relation and selection reason, support/retries, one-off override and limits. Science/reading discussion prompts are explicitly unscored, not inserted as observations. User-facing copy avoids level/weakness labels and keeps suggestions optional. Keep 48px touch targets, visible selection/focus, plain text equivalents and phone/tablet fit.
