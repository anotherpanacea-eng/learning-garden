## Four explicit models and one-trial contract

Each trial starts with a fresh matching **horizontal** model spring anchored at its left end. The applied pull is a nonnegative magnitude in newtons(N), not kilograms or net force. Pictures show settled lengths; no oscillation, motion, friction, energy, temperature or timing model. The anchor balances the pull. Lengths are centimeters(cm), always measured from the same fixed ruler zero. Starting unloaded length is L0; extension relative to that original reference is x=L−L0.

| ID / title | L0 cm | Offered force N | Settled loaded lengths cm, ordered by force | Length after removing that test pull |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| A / A steady stretch |6|0,1,2,3|6,8,10,12|6 after any offered force|
| B / Less stretch per newton |8|0,1,2,3|8,9,10,11|8 after any offered force|
| C / A changing rate |6|0,1,2,3|6,7,9,12|6 after any offered force|
| D / A change that stays |6|0,1,2,3,4|6,8,10,12,15|6 after0–3N;7 after4N|

These numbers define the screen models at the offered settled states only. They are not universal material data. **Each fresh matching spring is pulled once, then that pull is removed.** To try another force after applying a pull, start a fresh matching spring. This intentionally avoids inventing a loading/unloading law for repeated damaged-spring cycles. Other cases remain open. A completed trial is not a score or requirement.
