# Town, seasons and almanac: words and problems

Companion to [spec 33](33-rebuilding-the-town.md). Everything here is child-facing text for the build to use as written, after the same independent review as the spec. `{guide}` is the chosen companion's name, as elsewhere. Pip is a fox (he/him); Dot is a ladybug (she/her). No text here is scored, and no number moment feeds evidence or suggestions.

## The village

The village is **Willowbank**, the same willow as the Post Office.

**First visit, top of the scene:**
> The storm passed over Willowbank too. The garden is growing again, and the rest of the village could use some help. Anything you finish, anywhere, can help mend something here.

**Hear it / grown-up line under the scene:** Every finished visit, even one with skips, can be sent to a place in the village. Repairs never depend on right answers.

## Places

Each place has a damaged caption, three job cards, a repaired caption, and one open number moment that appears once the place is mended (open-task style: **We tried it** / **Not today**).

### Footbridge

- **Damaged:** The little bridge over Willow Brook lost three planks and most of its rope. Pip says he can jump it. Dot says nobody is jumping anything.
- **Jobs:**
  - *Measured planks.* "Planks need measuring before they're cut." Fed by a Ribbon Workshop walk or adventure.
  - *Rope bundles.* "The handrail needs rope, bundled in equal coils." Fed by a multiplication garden visit.
  - *A crossing notice.* "Someone should write down when the bridge is safe." Fed by an English story or Paris task.
- **Repaired:** The bridge is whole again. Ducks have opinions about it, but they use it anyway.
- **Number moment:** "The bridge has 12 planks. Pip walks across stepping on every second plank. How many planks does he step on? What if he steps on every third plank?" *(Open: let the child count on the picture; both answers are kept on screen.)*

### Mill

- **Damaged:** Mud jammed the mill wheel, and the water is too low to turn it. The miller's cat has moved into the flour bin in protest.
- **Jobs:**
  - *Water level.* "Someone needs to understand where the water went." Fed by a Water Lab mission.
  - *Grain sacks.* "The mill needs grain to grind." Fed by a café gathering trip.
  - *New spokes.* "The wheel lost some spokes. They have to be spaced evenly." Fed by a Shape Pavilion walk or adventure.
- **Repaired:** The wheel turns, slow and steady. The cat has returned the flour bin, mostly.
- **Number moment:** "The wheel has 8 spokes, spaced evenly. If it turns until the top spoke is at the bottom, how far around has it turned? Half? A quarter? Show it with the wheel." *(The wheel picture turns in eighths.)*

### Bakery

- **Damaged:** The bakery oven is fine. The bakery has no flour, no tables, and one very lost recipe card.
- **Jobs:**
  - *Flour.* "Bread starts with flour, and flour starts in the orchard fields." Fed by an orchard harvest.
  - *Tables set.* "Neighbors need somewhere to sit." Fed by a café dinner table served.
  - *The recipe card.* "The recipe is in French. Luckily, someone here reads a little French." Fed by a French story.
- **Repaired:** The bakery smells like warm bread. A line forms. Dot is at the front of it, for research.
- **Number moment:** "A tray holds 4 rows of 6 rolls. The baker sells one row. How many rolls are left? Is there more than one way to see it?" *(The tray picture lets a child cover a row.)*

### Library

- **Damaged:** Rain came through a window and scattered the books. Some shelves have lost their number labels.
- **Jobs:**
  - *Returned books.* "Stories go back on the shelves after they're read." Fed by two story visits of any kind.
  - *Shelf labels.* "Shelf 300 comes before 310. Someone has to know why." Fed by a Bundle Burrow walk or adventure.
  - *New signs.* "Every library needs a few good words on the wall." Fed by a Word Garden visit (spec 34).
- **Repaired:** The books are home, in order, and the reading corner has a new cushion. Pip has already fallen asleep on it.
- **Number moment:** "These shelves are out of order: 245, 254, 205, 250. Put them back so the smallest number is first. Which two were easiest to mix up, and why?"

### Bell tower

- **Damaged:** The storm knocked the bell rope loose and stopped the clock at a quarter past three.
- **Jobs:**
  - *Rope lengths.* "The new rope is cut into equal lengths." Fed by a Picnic Clearing walk or adventure.
  - *The clock face.* "Someone who likes patterns should check the numbers go in the right order." Fed by a Stepping-stone Path walk or adventure.
  - *A notice for the bell.* "The bell should ring for good news. Somebody has to say what the good news is." Fed by any story ending.
- **Repaired:** The clock is right again, and the bell rings whenever something in Willowbank is mended. Tap it to hear.
- **Number moment:** "The clock struck 3, then 4, then 5. How many rings is that altogether? How many rings will the clock make from 1 o'clock to 6 o'clock?"

### Post office

- **Damaged:** Letters blew everywhere. The sorting shelves are fine. The letters are less fine.
- **Jobs:**
  - *Sorted parcels.* "Parcels need sorting by size before they go anywhere." Fed by a Puzzle Hollow walk or adventure.
  - *A delivery route.* Hidden until Willow Post Office is built. "The first route since the storm."
  - *(Third job hidden until the Post Office is built.)*
- **Repaired:** The post office is open. Dot is wearing the hat. The hat is no longer under discussion.
- **Number moment:** "A parcel weighs 3 kilograms. Two parcels weigh the same as one big crate. How much does the crate weigh? What if the crate weighed the same as three parcels?"

## Send to the town

Shown on an end screen when at least one place wants that kind of work.

- **Heading:** Send this to Willowbank?
- **Body:** Your work can help mend something. Choose where it goes.
- **Each choice:** a small picture of the place with its job name, for example *Footbridge: measured planks*.
- **After choosing:** "{guide} carries it over. The footbridge has its planks." (Use the place and job.)
- **When nothing wants that kind of work:** Willowbank has plenty of that for now. Your work still counts in the garden.
- **Keep it for later:** a secondary button, **Not now**. The work waits in the pending basket on the village scene: "A basket of finished work is waiting to be sent."

## Weather log

**Prompt on the village scene, once per day:** What's the weather doing outside today?
**Choices:** Sunny · Cloudy · Rainy · Windy · Snowy
**After choosing:** "Noted. Dot has written it in the almanac."
**Already logged today:** "Today's weather is in the almanac. Come back tomorrow for another look outside."

## Almanac

**Cover:** The Willowbank Almanac. *Everything we've noticed, found and mended.*

**Page intros:**

- **Town:** "What we've mended, and when." Each entry: *The footbridge, mended in autumn.*
- **Garden:** "Everything that has grown in the garden since the storm."
- **Café and orchard:** "Guests we've served and foods we've found." Undiscovered entries are soft outlines with "Not met yet."
- **Words:** "Words we've met, in English and in French." Each French word shows its meaning on tap.
- **Weather:** "What the sky has been doing." A bar chart per month with one bar per weather kind and a number on each bar.
- **Thinking stickers:** "Ways of thinking we've tried. Every one of these is a good way to learn."

**Weather questions.** Shown one at a time under the month's graph, in this order as more days are logged. All unscored; a **Show me** button highlights the bars involved.

1. (3+ days) "Which kind of weather did we see most this month?"
2. (5+ days) "How many more sunny days than rainy days?" *(Use whichever two kinds have entries.)*
3. (8+ days) "How many days did we write down altogether?"
4. (2 months) "Was this month sunnier than last month? How can you tell from the bars?"
5. (2 months) "If next month is like this one, what do you think we'll see most? Why?" *(Prediction; any reasoned answer is good.)*

## Thinking stickers

Each is earned once. The sticker art is a small badge in the garden palette.

| Sticker | Earned when | Line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Picture Thinker | First used a picture or model | "You used a picture to think. That's what mathematicians do." |
| Second Look | First tried again after an answer that didn't fit | "You looked again. Second looks find things first looks miss." |
| Smaller Step | First used a smaller example | "You tried a smaller version first. That's a clever way in." |
| Step by Step | First opened the worked steps | "You followed it one step at a time." |
| Rest and Return | Finished something after skipping part of it | "You knew when to rest and when to keep going." |
| Came Back | Returned to a garden on a later day | "You came back to an idea on a new day. That's how ideas grow roots." |
| Worked It Out | First chose "Worked it out" as a method | "You worked it out your own way. Pip is taking notes." |
| Many Ways | Made three different rectangles in a Many Ways task | "Three ways! There's almost always more than one." |
