## Complete finite model

Choose P photosynthesis groups and R aerobic-respiration groups, each whole0–6. One group is the gas entry associated with **one glucose in the selected net equation**, not a single chemical step or a timed cycle. The familiar net summaries are6CO2+6H2O→glucose+6O2 using light energy, and glucose+6O2→6CO2+6H2O with energy transferred for cell activity and heat. They are independently authored accounts, not reversible mechanics. Water and glucose participate but are not counted on this gas board. The equations summarize many reactions; this diagram traces no individual atom or gas molecule changing into another species. Assume enough starting sugar and other materials for the chosen entries; no stock or availability is inferred. Choosing zero entries does not switch a living process off.

For oxygen: photosynthesis makes6P, respiration uses6R, net=6P−6R. For carbon dioxide: photosynthesis uses6P, respiration makes6R, net=6R−6P. **Net means made minus used in these two chosen accounts**, not transport across a leaf surface, total gas present, a measured rate, energy balance or real net ecosystem exchange. Both processes can occur in a green leaf in light; plant cells respire in light and darkness. Do not let an Oxygen/Carbon dioxide view toggle become a day/night switch. No time or light controls.

All49 pairs are permitted, including(0,0). Positive net means more made than used; negative means more used than made, never a negative number of molecules present. For each gas, opposite entries can be linked in6-molecule bundles: min(P,R) linked pairs, each still visible on both rows. Pairing changes presentation only; it neither deletes matter nor identifies the same individual molecules. Energy does not circulate back because the gas totals balance.
