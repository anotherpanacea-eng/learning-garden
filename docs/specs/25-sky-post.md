Sky Post is the flying-courier adventure within [Willow Post Office](02-willow-post-office.md). Delivering parcels, exploring routes and helping neighbors reconnect after the storm are the play. There is no separate Play Center to earn access to, no play-credit currency and no earned-time countdown. Choose an explorer and guide independently. Use original characters, places, prose and art. Extend the existing Post Office map, delivery catalog, instruction runner and `postOffice` save extension.

The first slice uses six fixed, reviewed packing examples; it has no number-range selector or procedural/adaptive math. The broader generator/range direction below needs its own reviewed follow-up spec.

### Bramble's ribbon gate

Bramble is an original girl witch (she/her) with a patchwork hat and a magpie companion. She is helping repair a storm-tangled ribbon gate, and playfully challenges the courier to count supplies before flying through. Show an illustrated encounter at the current flight position; the parcel and route stay paused exactly there. She is a friendly rival, not a threatening or punitive adult.

Offer “Meet Bramble” and “Practice first” on any open delivery, even before packing. This is a separate supplies puzzle; its answer never packs the real order. There is one frozen encounter per visit, with no currency, hearts, time limit or locked destination. Flight steps pause while its question or practice is visible. “Take the quiet route” bypasses the encounter immediately and returns to the same pending instruction. Route editing/restarting and courier changes keep the rival's task, attempts and support; they do not manufacture another encounter. A completed delivery cannot start a new rival encounter, but existing work remains readable. Bypassed and continued encounters remain read-only on later visits and replays; choose a fresh delivery visit for a new encounter.

Choose from three finite original tasks: addition (6 blue ribbons + 5 gold ribbons = 11), subtraction (10 ribbons, reserve 3 = 7), and equal groups (3 bundles of 4 ribbons = 12). If this delivery has a packing miss or was packed together, use its operation with different numbers. Otherwise use a neutral deterministic addition/subtraction/multiplication mix indexed by the six delivery IDs: Clover/addition, Ember/subtraction, Purl/multiplication, Moss/addition, Wren/subtraction, Dot/multiplication. Freeze the chosen task and selection reason on first opening. This uses one observed task as an invitation, not a diagnosis, grade, mastery claim or full 12×12 practice system. No other learning records are altered.

Each question shows exact pictures and accepts a finite numeric total, retaining raw input. On a miss, keep the answer and attempt, then open a different worked example of the same operation: 4+2=6; 8−2=6; or 2×4=8. The practice picture has previous/next steps (0: supplies, 1: combine/reserve/group, 2: equation and explanation), with text equivalents and narration on request. Practice is freely available before any answer and has no required question toll. “Try Bramble's puzzle” returns to the same question. After support has been opened, all subsequent attempts are recorded as after support. “Solve together” shows the actual task's explanation and resolves it with support without fabricating a numeric attempt. Correct numeric answers resolve the encounter; the rival congratulates the courier and untangles the gate. Both paths show a manual “Continue my delivery” action. Quiet-route bypass and continuation never execute a route clause or mark a delivery completed.

Retain it through pause/reload, explorer switches, other destinations, export and save-off; existing reset and latest-24-visit retention apply. “Continue my delivery” changes `resolved` to `continued`, releasing flight blocking without dropping the resolution. `question`, `practice` and `resolved` block flight; `continued` and `bypassed` release it. Bypassing preserves any prior attempts and support but has no resolution. Unknown or malformed rival data closes the visit with its exact payload kept, following existing recovery behavior. Visits without a rival remain valid without migration or implicit encounter creation.

Grown-up notes distinguish rival attempts, support, narration requests, bypass and resolution from packing, route checks and reported reading. No automatic independent-recall claim is added. Hearing either task or example marks rival narration support; replaying a resolution adds no new attempt or completion.
