# The Sharing Counter: cut, compare, pack, revise

## Problem and bounded outcome

The café's equal-group bench teaches whole-object sharing, but it does not make a half or quarter of a whole visible. Add an optional bakery counter where neighbors pack imaginary picnic parcels after the storm repairs. The learner repairs cuts, chooses portions and rearranges buns. The meaningful action is making a parcel fit a pictured request, not answering a text quiz before play. All cases, help and later pages remain open. This is visual, supported fraction practice, never a placement assessment or a claim of independent fraction proficiency.

Entry `#cafe/sharing`; link after the existing café task, alongside the equal-group invitation. Use a separate six-case chooser and compact **make → compare → keep/revise** pages. The request, original whole and current model remain available on each task page. Back, routes, changing cases and reload preserve each case's work. No stock cost, serving reward, hunger penalty, supper progress, credit, timer or ending-film trigger comes from this counter. Existing café guest, plate, multiplication question, cupboard and all other destination drafts stay unchanged.

## Six original picnic requests

Content model ID `sharing-counter-v1`. Rectangular moon-loaves have uniform thickness and density in this explicitly simplified picture model. The one pictured loaf is the whole; its fixed width is 12 internal geometry steps, not centimetres, grams or a real recipe. This is an exact authored geometric model, not simulated food physics. All loaf cases use the same dimensions. In set cases the whole is the enclosed collection of 12 identical imaginary buns.

| Stable case | Reader and reason | Family and pictured request |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `repair-halves` | Purl and Clover are sharing one picnic loaf. | Repair an unequal two-piece cut, then pack one of two equal shares. Initial cut at geometry step 4 makes widths 4 and 8. |
| `repair-quarters` | Four lantern-menders want one equal loaf share each. | Repair cuts at 2, 5 and 8, then pack one of four equal shares. Initial widths 2, 3, 3 and 4 deliberately do not qualify as quarters. |
| `half-parcel` | Ember is saving half a loaf for a later picnic. | Select half from an equally partitioned loaf; compare one half with two quarters of the identical whole. |
| `three-quarter-parcel` | Wisp keeps one quarter and packs the rest for the picnic. | From four equal shares, choose three; compare the packed and remaining amounts against the same whole. |
| `quarter-basket` | Clover reserves a quarter of 12 buns for the bridge crew. | Make four equal groups, then pack one complete group. |
| `three-quarter-basket` | Purl sends three quarters of 12 buns to the picnic. | Make four equal groups, then pack three complete groups. |

These are six openly selectable, finite cases, not endless generated content. “Another picnic request” cycles in stable catalogue order, with a replay labelled familiar; it never changes a paused case. Preserve one working draft and one latest explicitly kept parcel per case, so revisiting is useful without a growing archive. Different guests do not imply different invented learning levels. A later release can add different wholes, thirds/eighths, measured recipes or new cases after reviewing their representations.

## 1. Make and repair

**Repair cases.** Show one loaf outline, its current piece boundaries and all pieces in their original positions, including unequal pieces. Use 11 evenly spaced, unnumbered cut guides. A labelled button can add or remove each cut; a join-again button removes cuts. Keyboard and touch work without dragging or precision pointing. A parallel list names each piece and whether it is currently in the parcel; a piece button packs or returns that whole piece. Cutting/joining first returns any packed pieces to the loaf with a visible explanation; no food disappears or duplicates. Toggling the same cut twice restores the original geometry. The request specifies two or four equal shares; any other piece count stays editable but does not match that request.

Do not call pieces halves or quarters until their widths are equal and their count is two or four. The initial two unequal pieces remain “two unequal pieces,” even if one was selected. Equal-sized pieces may appear in different screen positions, but this increment does not claim to teach equal shares with different shapes. Checking distinguishes **number of shares**, **equal sizes** and **number packed**. It describes a mismatch and leaves every cut/selection in place. A matching repair requires the requested equal partition and exactly one selected share.

**Compare cases.** Start `half-parcel` with two equal empty halves; explicit **two equal shares / four equal shares** buttons repartition the same loaf. Start `three-quarter-parcel` with four equal empty quarters. Switching partition returns selections, explains the return, and keeps earlier checked snapshots unchanged. Piece toggles select/deselect portions. A half request can fit one half or two quarters; a three-quarter request requires three quarters. Selecting all, none or a nonmatching portion is allowed and editable. No auto-completion after a failed check.

**Basket cases.** Show 12 unique bun tokens and four empty group mats inside a whole-basket enclosure, plus a parcel. Tap a destination mat then **move one bun here** / **return one bun**; a reversible round-share control puts one bun on each mat atomically when four remain in the tray. The four groups are a supplied scaffold and are named as such. When all 12 have been shared into four equal groups of three, each complete group can be toggled into/out of the parcel. Moving a group means its three actual bun tokens, not a copied picture. Grouping stays visible in the whole enclosure even when its group is marked packed; a separate parcel summary does not imply additional buns. Before any bun redistribution, including a move involving an unpacked group or a round share, return **all** packed groups and clear every selected-group index, with a visible explanation. Then apply the requested move. This prevents a remaining packed group from keeping a quarter label after the overall equal partition has changed.

The child may check unequal or incomplete grouping; feedback distinguishes **all buns accounted for**, **four equal groups** and **one/three complete groups packed**. Packing is available after the mathematical group is actually formed; the rest of the activity, support and keep-draft control are never locked. State/text makes this a model constraint, not an achievement gate. A provided “try selecting groups” example can show a separate eight-bun/two-group case, marked supplied help. The exact request remains editable rather than silently filled by the example.

## 2. Compare representations

Keep a clearly outlined whole beside its portion. For an equal loaf partition, show **selected equal shares / all equal shares**, with words and notation together. For an unequal partition say why counting pieces alone cannot name its fraction; do not generate a false fraction label. The repair model can still show exact relative lengths without a claimed half/quarter label.

For the half parcel, provide a manually opened comparison: identical whole outlines show **one of two equal shares** and **two of four equal shares**. Both fill the same amount. A step button reveals the cuts, then the selected portions, then `1/2 = 2/4`. The comparison is explicitly supplied support and does not rewrite the learner's parcel. The three-quarter case shows three selected quarters and the remaining quarter, with the same whole outline. Do not introduce addition algorithms or simplification rules in this slice.

A matching unit strip and 0–1 number line keep 0 and 1 whole labelled, with quarter ticks. In the compare cases, display a marker for the total amount currently selected and a contiguous accumulation strip of that amount. Label this as **how much is packed**, so selecting two separated quarters does not falsely claim their original spatial endpoint is one half. The marker is derived from the exact selected geometric length divided by the whole width; it is not a second independently answered number-line task. If the requested partition is not equal, suppress the fraction notation/line comparison and explain the equality requirement. Never round unequal thirds to quarter ticks.

For baskets, display `one quarter of 12 buns = 3 buns` or `three quarters of 12 buns = 9 buns` only as an optional explanation after the relevant equal grouping is shown. A numerical bun count is distinct from the fraction of the basket. Numerator counts complete equal groups selected; denominator counts the four equal groups in this particular whole. Do not describe three selected buns as three quarters of twelve. No automatic comparison of unlike physical wholes, unit conversions or measured recipe quantities is included.

## 3. Keep and revisit

“Keep this picnic draft” works even for incomplete or unequal work and names it a draft. Freeze the exact case/request/model ID, original whole, cuts or bun arrangement, selected pieces/groups, separate check results, support and familiarity at that moment. Later manipulation cannot mutate that kept snapshot. A new keep explicitly replaces only that case's latest kept draft; other cases remain intact. A short shelf shows six case slots and opens each original request. No food inventory grant is generated.

Offer optional conversation: “What stayed the same when we changed the cuts?” and “What is our whole in this picture?” Do not store or grade the spoken answer, require typed explanations, infer understanding from word choice or insist on a minimum story length. The counter may link back to the existing equal-group bench without replacing its selected case or moving a finished arrangement into an incompatible task. No bidirectional state-copy feature is needed for this slice.

## Practice, support and exposure

All checks carry `practice:true` and `visual:true`; there is no independent-success label. Separate **manipulation**, **check feedback**, **different worked example**, **same-whole equivalence demonstration**, **notation explanation**, **audio**, and **together** flags. Flags are sticky within the working case, but each check freezes the flags present when it was made; later help does not retroactively rewrite an earlier check. Revisiting a case or rechecking after feedback marks familiarity without inventing a new answer on render. Labels/counters already visible in the model count as supplied visual material even without a help tap.

Expose the ordered whole-number fact `4 × 3` only when the complete four-groups-of-three model is actually displayed, or an explicit authored `4 × 3` explanation is actually shown. Empty, partial or unequal group mats do not display that relationship and must not trigger its exposure. The separate eight-bun/two-group example exposes `2 × 4` only when its correct example model or explicit explanation is actually visible. Use the existing app shared fact-exposure hook so a later original multiplication check-in, garden fact or Bramble/café fact cannot call those displayed relationships unseen. Register exposure when that model/explanation is actually rendered, not merely when its chooser card is listed or a support control exists. Do not record a multiplication answer or import this counter's success into any other game's score/suggestions. Fraction work here is scoped practice, not evidence of mastery or a fresh broad fraction check-in. Existing broader topic familiarity remains its existing conservative mechanism; do not invent a general learner-profile service. If a specific exact fraction task is later shared across loops, that future integration must carry its prior exposure explicitly before claiming fresh evidence.

## State and narrow action API

One optional `sharingCounter` version-1 root. Missing means fresh; validators, renderers and notes are read-only. Unknown versions, unknown keys, invalid IDs/geometry or malformed roots remain byte-for-byte intact and unavailable. Explicit recovery keeps the cases that still validate and retains the full old payload under `recovered`, consistent with existing optional destinations. Generic import/export must retain such unavailable roots, escape notes and support explorer isolation, saving off and reset. No names, dates, uploaded files, network learner data or new storage service.

Suggested shape, with equivalent simpler field names acceptable:

- `version`, `currentCase` (null or one of six IDs), `page` (`choose|make|compare|keep`), `cases` keyed only by those six IDs, optional `recovered`.
- Each case holds an immutable clone of its versioned authored request/whole, working model, sticky support, `seen`/`familiar` context, optional example step, at most 24 total check snapshots, and null or its latest kept snapshot.
- A loaf model has sorted unique integer cut coordinates 1–11 and selected piece indices valid for the current cuts. Piece boundaries include 0 and 12 implicitly. Sum of piece widths is always 12; equal means exact integer equality, never visual approximation. Cuts can be arbitrary valid guides; fraction labels are restricted to valid requested equal partitions.
- A basket model has four integer group sizes 0–12 whose sum is at most 12 and unique selected group indices 0–3. Tray count is derived as 12 minus the group sum. Selecting any group requires all four group sizes to equal 3 and the tray to be empty; unselecting always remains possible. Validation rejects any selected groups unless the complete grouping is exactly `[3,3,3,3]`; checking only the selected groups is insufficient. Any bun redistribution clears all packed selections before its move, with the explanation specified above. The model contains 12 buns total irrespective of packed marking. Stable token allocation can be derived from group order and counts; no extra mutable token registry is needed.
- Snapshots include exact original request/model version, cloned geometry/grouping and selection, separately derived count/equality/portion fit, support/familiarity and `practice:true`. Validate finite integer bounds, enum/ID membership, known keys, history limits and exact authored source semantics without coercion. Saved results are validated on counts and outcome values, never re-graded against current rules or wording. A source version no longer supported remains unavailable/recoverable rather than scoring against replacement content.

Suggested exported actions: `sharingState`, `ensureSharing`, `chooseSharingCase`, `setSharingPage`, `toggleSharingCut`, `setSharingPartition`, `toggleSharingPiece`, `moveSharingBun`, `shareSharingRound`, `toggleSharingGroup`, `requestSharingSupport`, `stepSharingExample`, `checkSharing`, `keepSharing`, `recoverSharing`. A pure view helper returns model constraints, explanatory counts and exact current fact pairs. Actions reject invalid arguments without normalizing or changing the root; they mutate only this optional destination. The app owns explicit shared exposure calls. No abstract exercise engine or persistent event system is required.
