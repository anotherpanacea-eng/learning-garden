# 242 — The Town Record

## Purpose and scope

A bounded first runtime for Keeper's Desk Case10, *What should Willowbank write down?* ([authored dossier](../content/118-town-record-case.md)), at `#learn/science/records`. The librarian has an empty record book: choose a repeatable measurement, try observation schedules, design the sheet and leave instructions for a future reader. The principal action is choosing when to look, collecting only those supplied observations, then comparing schedules. This closes a data-design bridge in course B2/B5; it is not a complete statistics course, ActII ending or real monitoring system.

All records are conspicuously **invented town data**, never a real observation, flood prediction or safety recommendation. The model uses height in centimetres from a fixed marked zero line on a fictional post. Zero means level with that fixed line, not no water. Height is not flow (volume/time), and the zero does not move with an idea of 'ordinary water'. No child is asked to go near water, go out at night or collect real observations. The fictional schedule stays separate from any real weather notebook.

Four pages stay open in any order. Supported plan choices alone can produce a useful kept plan; no earlier task must be checked. Other games, old desk work and story pages stay open and unchanged. No age, grade, timer, score, punishment, automatic proficiency inference or remote learner data.

This first slice supplies method/zero/uncertainty contrasts, repeatable simulated sampling, missing versus zero, a designed sheet and a revisable plan. The dossier's annual-check arithmetic, condition-triggered monitoring schedule and future story adapters are deferred explicitly; none is a prerequisite or falsely marked complete. It does not reveal Hollis's page2 or advance ActII.
