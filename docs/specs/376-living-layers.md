Six shared frozen teaching frames `{text,art}` in this order; art none except the last:

1. **“A cell is a basic unit of life. Some organisms have one cell. Our selected person and bean plant examples have many.”**
2. **“A tissue is a group of cells and associated material working together. Here tissue means a biological structure; paper tissues use a different meaning.”**
3. **“An organ contains different tissues. The highlighted tissue is one example, so this path does not show everything in the organ.”**
4. **“An organ system includes organs working together. Real systems can share organs; one path is not a full map of all their connections.”**
5. **“These images show different organizational levels. A cell does not turn into an organ or a whole organism when we look at its larger context.”**
6. **“One baker's yeast cell is a whole organism. It does not have tissues or organs, so our five-level path does not describe every living thing.”** Art `yeast`: one original schematic single-cell icon, not a budding pair, not a microscope/species-identification image.
