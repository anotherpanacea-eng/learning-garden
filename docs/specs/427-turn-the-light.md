## Six open stage cards and geometric contract

All cards use one ideal flat reflecting line, a fixed hit point H, a front-facing unit normal n and one incident ray aimed at H. Angles are measured at H from the normal, never from the surface. The source-side sign describes the vector from H **toward the lamp**, not the opposite propagation direction of the incoming arrow. Diagram coordinates use x right/y down, so positive angular changes are clockwise. Internal drawing positions are not physical lengths.

| ID · title | Base outward-normal heading φ | Incoming angle θ | Lamp side s | Reference outgoing angle α relative to normal |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `curtain` · Curtain mark | −90° | 30° | −1 | +30° |
| `wide` · A wider angle | −90° | 60° | −1 | +60° |

Let t be n rotated90° clockwise. The lamp vector is `cos θ·n + s·sin θ·t`. Incoming propagation is its negative. The correct reflected direction has angle `−s·θ` relative to n, equivalently `dOut = dIn − 2(dIn·n)n`. The learner's proposal uses its own α; it is not silently replaced by that reference. At θ=0 the true outgoing ray retraces the incoming line in the opposite direction. That card uses one combined lamp/return marker instead of overlapping two separately labelled objects.
