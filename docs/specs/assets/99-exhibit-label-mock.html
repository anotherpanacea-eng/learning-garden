Supplied example and help
  1. A photograph can show this board's shape and written marks. That is a visual observation.
  2. The Met explains that a board like this could be recoated and used again. That is a museum explanation about use.
  3. A photograph cannot tell us that every Egyptian child used one. The larger claim would need more evidence.
