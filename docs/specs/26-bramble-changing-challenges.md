## Play loop

Bramble offers a fresh supplies puzzle without requiring another delivery. At a resolved gate offer both **Another challenge** and **Continue my delivery**. After continuing or taking the quiet route, another challenge is still available; starting one is explicit and pauses the same parcel/route again. New challenges remain available after the parcel has arrived, as optional play alongside a completed delivery. They cannot add another delivery completion. No currency, hearts, timer, forced repetition or locked destinations.

Offer an openly selectable **Mixed parcels / Multiplication / Addition / Subtraction** mode and, for multiplication, **All tables / 1 through 12**. A table means first factor fixed to that number, with second factor 1–12. Defaults are mixed and all tables. Controls affect the next question only; a pending question keeps its exact numbers, input, attempts, helper and scene. Clearly say when a choice will apply. A fresh question is generated only on Meet Bramble/Practice first/Another challenge, never during rendering, reload or navigation.

Generated multiplication includes all 144 ordered pairs with factors 1–12, including 12×12. For minimal tasks such as 1×1 or 1+1, use another small positive example rather than claiming it is smaller. Addition uses positive addends 1–20. Subtraction uses a total 2–20 and a positive reserved count below that total, leaving at least one. Three original gate-supply situations rotate their wording while keeping pictured counts literal. Generators construct exact answers, pictures, equations and a different worked example of the same operation; no live AI or network request is involved.

## Selection and teaching

Reuse the existing multiplication garden's ordered-fact and coprime-walk approach as a design reference, without writing to its observations. Keep Bramble's evidence separate. Only new Bramble rounds enter this new selector; earlier authored encounters stay preserved and are not relabelled as adaptive evidence.

Mixed mode rotates multiplication, addition, multiplication, subtraction. Within each operation mix three purposes over successive rounds: (1) revisit an earlier example needing another look, or choose a smaller related fact when it was just seen; (2) a comfortable fact or a simple starting pattern; (3) a new example from the full chosen pool. Use a monotonically increasing round counter to rotate purposes so bounded-history trimming cannot freeze selection. Maintain a separate new-example cursor per operation; multiplication walks the ordered 144-fact pool with stride 37 (also coprime to a single table's 12 facts). Each new-example turn advances fairly through its eligible pool, preferring to avoid the last three exact operation/operand keys. Avoid immediate repeated numbers where the pool allows it. This fairness ensures new facts remain available even after many misses; no fact is permanently hidden by an inferred level. In a fixed multiplication mode/table, repeated play must eventually visit every eligible pair under both clean and support-heavy histories.

Use the latest bounded rounds of that operation, taking the latest assessed finished round per exact operation/operand key so later clean work supersedes an older miss. History order is archive/finish append order, independent of allocation ID, because waiting deliveries can finish out of order. Bypasses remain visible as exposures but do not supply a correctness judgment or override earlier assessed evidence. A round with a miss, opened practice, narration, solve-together, counting/together report, or solve-together without a numeric solution is an invitation to revisit. A correct first numeric attempt before assistance can inform a comfortable-example choice; it never proves recall or mastery. A bypass supplies no correctness judgment and never becomes a miss. When evidence is sparse, use simple starting patterns (1s, 2s, 5s, 10s where present) alongside the fair new-example turns. Selection reasons in grown-up notes use neutral terms such as new example, revisit, related example, familiar example, starting mix. Child-facing copy never labels a weak spot or a level.

Every question has exact pictures and optional narration. Multiplication at large sizes uses a compact 12-column maximum ribbon array, with row/bundle labels and a full text equivalent; 12×12 should fit a tablet/phone without 12 huge cards or horizontal overflow. A different worked example, smaller where possible, remains stepped and available before answering or after a miss. The actual question can show a controllable decomposition (for example 12×7 = 10×7 + 2×7) on request; this explicitly marks support and exposure to that answer, and does not replace the saved separate example. A miss preserves input/attempt then opens practice; retry uses the same pending problem. Solve-together resolves with support without a fabricated answer attempt.

After a correct answer offer an optional method report: **I knew it / I worked it out / I counted / We did it together**. Keep null when unreported. Record the report separately from support; together marks support, and a reported recall after support remains supported. Always allow continuation or another challenge without a method choice. Repeated numeric success, picture use, retry, narration and reported method are observations, not a level or independent-recall certification.
