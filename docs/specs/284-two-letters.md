No other benches, Set B–G, Spanish replies or Spanish teaching, fluent-reader gate, literature excerpts, story adapter, voice recording, typed answer, adaptation, mastery, score, timer, reward, lock or curriculum-coverage closure. An optional original sentence may be spoken to a grown-up or written offscreen: it is never captured, marked or stored. Local English Hear reads already-visible supplied text only.

The letters are fictional. Both mention the seed arrival and a wish for storm readings. They are **not identical messages**: Nuria is asked to find out whether her council will send readings; the council receives a direct request and a courier entrance question. “Read both” identifies that shared material and explicitly names those differences. It must not claim that only wording changed. “Request” means ask for; “require” means need or make necessary, not an impolite form of request. No ridicule, “stiff,” “Nuria might laugh,” or claims that all strangers need formal speech.
