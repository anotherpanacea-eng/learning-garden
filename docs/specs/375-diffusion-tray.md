## The model and exact finite copy

Original two-space stochastic illustration. Both spaces have the same fixed volume, are treated as internally mixed and have no bulk flow. Marker counts represent amounts of each dissolved substance; the solvent is omitted. This is a coarse-grained model of exchange, NOT a collision-resolving molecular simulation, osmosis, active transport, water phase change, a lung, a living cell or a physical membrane test. It predicts no seconds, temperature effect, measured rate or real material permeability. We do not teach that particles aim for emptier places, that concentration is a total amount independent of volume, or that motion stops at exact equality. With small populations, observed counts fluctuate; an individual step need not reduce the imbalance. Expectations over many realizations differ from one observed step.

Four source cases in this order. Each has16 circle markers and8 diamond markers; diamonds start4left/4right and cannot cross. Circles can cross except in the closed case. These numbers are invented teaching parameters, not measurements.

| Case ID / title | Circles left/right | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| left / More circles on the left | 12 / 4 | The town collection asks: does every marker travel from the more crowded side? Follow a marker and compare both crossing counts. |
| right / More circles on the right | 4 / 12 | Try the opposite starting pattern. Does a different starting side change which overall direction is expected? |
| even / Equal starting counts | 8 / 8 | The two sides start with equal circle counts. Are the markers finished moving? Advance several snapshots and look again. |
| closed / No crossings allowed | 12 / 4 | This invented boundary lets neither marker kind cross. Moving within a space and crossing a boundary are different things. |

Kinds: `circle` label **Circle markers**, symbol `circle`; `diamond` label **Diamond markers**, symbol `diamond`. Source-owned canCross flags and symbols drive all historical rendering, not today's case IDs or colors. Diagram legend and boundary-key text derive from each frozen kind label/flag; never infer a real molecule's permeability from its drawn shape/size/color. Boundary is a neutral divider labelled “Model boundary”, not a hole-size sieve or cell wall. No claim that every circle is smaller than every diamond. No new substances are created.

Shared frozen notice:

> Two equal-volume model spaces. These are symbols, not molecules drawn to scale or microscope views. One step is not a second. This model has no temperature setting. The crossing chance is invented, not a measured diffusion rate. Wiggles and straight crossing paths are diagram cues, not realistic molecular trajectories. Trials are pseudo-random and can differ. No directed pump or ATP use is represented; thermal motion still involves energy. Solvent particles are not drawn.

Shared frozen rule notice:

> At each step, a crossing-enabled marker has about a one-in-four chance to change sides, whatever side it is on and however crowded either side is. A marker that cannot cross stays in its original space. All markers represent moving matter, even when none crosses. A kept snapshot saves the starting setup, this and the preceding snapshot, and cumulative crossings, not every earlier step. Both model volumes are held fixed. Water movement and changes in volume are left out.

Six exact teaching strings, same ordered list in each source:

1. “Random movement has no goal. Our markers stand for dissolved particles; they do not choose where to go.”
2. “For one substance, concentration means how much is in a given volume. Our two spaces have equal volume, so compare that substance's counts.”
3. “When markers can cross and one side is more crowded, more crossings are expected from that side over many steps. One short step can go the other way.”
4. “Equal starting counts do not freeze the particles. When markers can cross, the counts in this small model can fluctuate even when neither side is favored.”
5. “A real cell membrane lets some substances cross more easily than others. Our invented boundary rule is not a real membrane test.”
6. “Oxygen and carbon dioxide can cross cell membranes by simple diffusion. We are not modeling a lung, blood flow, water movement or a glucose transporter.”

UI snapshot caption: **“Snapshot at step {steps}. Real particle motion continues between our snapshots.”** The Crossings turn includes this direct arithmetic instruction: **“Subtract opposite-direction crossing counts to find the net change.”** Counts/readouts are per kind, labelled by frozen kind label: starting left/right, current left/right, last-step left→right/right→left and cumulative left→right/right→left. Net cumulative change is the signed difference left→right minus right→left, with explicit direction or zero, NOT the sum of crossings. A last step with zero net can have crossings both ways; show both. A zero-step snapshot explicitly says it has not been advanced. Nothing judges whether a run is successful, converged, proof of biology or a correct response.

## Small deterministic transition, pseudo-random trials

`source.seed` nonzero uint32 initializes work.rng. One manual **Advance one step** visits markers in frozen source.particles order; take one xorshift32 draw for EACH marker, including blocked kinds (x ^= x<<13; x ^= x>>>17; x ^= x<<5; convert unsigned). A crossing-enabled marker flips side if the unsigned draw <2^30 (one quarter of the32bit range); otherwise retains side. Blocked markers retain side regardless. There is no comparison with the gradient to direct an individual marker, no condition stopping at equal counts, and no population mutation. Save previous=current before the step, update directional cumulative counters, then increment steps once. The v1 model fixes this finite threshold rule, not an adjustable saved probability. Because the nonzero uint32 stream excludes zero, its fraction is slightly below one quarter; the rule notice deliberately says about. This is a disclosed toy exchange process, not a physically calibrated rate law. RNG quality or observed game behavior is not evidence of learning or real material diffusion.

Create seeds in UI with crypto.getRandomValues (replace zero with1), not a network call; core accepts explicit seeds for repeatable tests. A catalogue setup's first local trial is generated once and remains stable across renders. **New trial from this setup** deliberately creates a new local draft from the selected source's initial particles/rules/text with a new seed; it does NOT continue or recompute a kept run, and does not discard the previous draft. It may happen to produce the same short outcome. No secret adjustment to force an example or reward.

Only manual steps; optional brief per-action CSS motion from previous to current snapshot. No play loop, requestAnimationFrame chain, timeout, audio track or background work. Every marker gets an illustrative position change even if it stays in one space; crossers can show their previous position/path, with shape and selected-marker outline redundant with color. Reduced-motion shows the final snapshot and explicit movement counts without animation. Help, chooser changes and opening saved work must not replay the last motion. No auto speech or autoplay.
