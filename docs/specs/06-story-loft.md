# 06 — Story Loft

**Status: Planned; not implemented.**

## Goal and play loop

Read for meaning, compare viewpoints, and make something for an audience. Choose an original garden story; optionally predict from its opening, reveal scenes at a chosen pace, arrange events, revisit evidence, and compare interpretations. Finish with optional retelling, composition, or poetry. No task, interpretation, or creative project is required to visit another story.

## Content minimum

Author six short stories, each told in two 80–140-word character perspectives: twelve passages altogether. Shared events must agree; noticing and interpretation can differ. Each story includes three illustrated scene cards, one ordering task grounded in explicit chronology, and one evidence task linking a claim to passage details. Accept every authored interpretation supported by the text; distinguish factual contradiction from a plausible inference. Unsupported choices invite checking a specific passage, not an emotion/personality verdict. Independent editorial review checks both perspectives and all accepted evidence combinations.

Every story offers three unscored options: retell to someone using scenes; compose a different ending from tap-selected clauses; or arrange two poem lines from reviewed word chunks. Optional paper writing, drawing, or dictation allows unrestricted ideas outside the app. Structured composition is labelled as such and never reported as free-writing proficiency. Spoken explanation is discussed with a parent, not automatically assessed. Provide short parallel examples and tap-to-highlight evidence.

## State and evidence

A versioned `loft` extension stores story/perspective, scene order, evidence choices, revision history, support, and creative chunk IDs. Preserve the latest 24 visit summaries. Keep reported independent/together/not-observed reading separate from answer correctness and help. Predictions remain unscored; acknowledging a project does not prove retelling or writing occurred. Preserve legacy storage keys and records, pause/reload, saving-off, export/reset, and conservative recovery.

## Accessibility, privacy, and non-goals

All scene movement has labelled move-up/down touch/keyboard buttons; text alternatives, visible focus, reduced motion, and manual passage reveal are required. Equal guides and independent avatars remain. Untimed parent-guided play has no grades, locks, identity fields, free-text profiles, uploads, shared learner data, analytics, server/AI backend, generative stories, or new authoring platform.

## Acceptance and dependencies

Refresh [IES comprehension guidance](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/14) for text structure/discussion and [IES writing guidance](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/17) for purposeful revision; recheck all story details and any [Marble mappings](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy) before implementation. Test twelve passages, ordering/evidence alternatives, support retention, creative-evidence limits, skip/resume, storage, and small screens. Independently review spec/content/implementation and root QA; use the existing Site and draft PR, with exact-candidate Claude clearance before merge.
