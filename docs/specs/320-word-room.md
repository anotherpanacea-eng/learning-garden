Existing `#words`, `#words/endings`, Paris and Story Loft retain their meanings and saved work. Spec250 retains its proposed `#words/sounds` route and `sounds` root for later letter-team/audio work. ## 2. Teaching encounter and spiral

Follow spec49 §5, with the scope exceptions in the [source audit](../source-audits/320-word-room.md):

1. **Teach:** show a prop/action, its short meaning and whole printed label. Invite the adult to say the word and demonstrate its three sounds while pointing left to right. The constituent letters stay available; knowing their names is not presumed to supply their sounds.
2. **Contrast:** a small side-by-side view compares a retained sibling with the waiting word, without editing either draft. It shows three printed positions in each; for the initial mat/sat pair the first sound changes. Offer only readable siblings whose final two letters match and first letter differs, in retained key order, with one native **Compare {word} and {other}** button for each. Thus mat/sat remains explicitly selectable rather than being displaced by the first sibling. The presentation-only view owns `{kind:'contrast',otherId}`; **Back to my label** closes it. Ask the adult to model both words clearly and discuss the first sound. Do not pretend to assess that oral response. If there is no such sibling, omit the comparison group. The initial twelve-word inventory does not supply a vowel-only or final-sound-only contrast.
3. **Supported practice:** tap letter tiles into three numbered places, with an optional visible word model. Allow reuse of a letter, including both p positions in pop. A match means the pictured label has been assembled, never that it was read independently.
4. **New use:** put a label beside its prop, then meet it in one adult-supported miniature. Non-target words remain explicitly adult-provided.
5. **Return:** reopen any label, compare a sibling, or read another miniature. Suggest mat then sat on a fresh visit; no scores or inferred levels change the suggestion. A later separate slice may introduce other sound positions and letter teams.

Every action here is supported exploration. Label encounters, pointing, visible models, adult pronunciation and shared reading do not prove independent decoding, spelling from dictation, fluency, comprehension or transfer. No independent-attempt field, mastery level, speed, score, lock, penalty or math exposure is written.

## 3. Interaction and exact material

Use the [finite copy](../content/320-word-room-text.md), its projected twelve meaning sentences and brief55's three exact shared texts. Original SVG props include a cat and distinguish a mat from a blanket; a toy sporting bat from the animal; a badge pin; a small lidded metal tin; one fin on a toy fish; a storage bin; a small puppet hop; a mop; a spinning top; and a bursting bubble. Sat gets a before/after picture. All art has a concise meaning alternative. Do not require physical hopping or handling a pin.

The board has three short pages, **Meet the prop / Make a label / Read together**, with explicit Back/Next controls and a persistent compact view of the chosen prop/label. Changing pages preserves tile positions and the chosen target. All twelve cards and all three texts are also directly selectable; no completion gate. A separate optional **Point through the word** display advances one of three positions manually, then the whole word. Its dots are sound positions in these examples, not syllables or timing measurements. It never animates or plays automatically.

Each word has its own draft. Choosing another prop preserves the waiting draft rather than clearing it. Initial/default draft values are slots `[null,null,null]`, place0, modelShown true and point null. No word is selected until a card is chosen; a text can be the first choice. The saved page is choose/meet/build/read; temporary example and check-result views are presentation state only.

On load, a missing selected draft is not silently created: show its card and a **Start this label** action that creates the default draft only on that explicit action. An invalid draft stays paused. For meet/build with no readable selected card/draft, show the chooser without writing a repair; it remains the same effective view after reload. Explicit Back from reading in that situation saves page choose. An unreadable/missing linked text also leads to page choose, retaining the previous textId and every draft, with a connected explanation; refusing that page change leaves the prior material in place. Show the suggested mat/sat entry line only when both retained words and their drafts (if present) are readable; never substitute today's card into an earlier source.

Choosing a card whose draft exists but is invalid selects that card and page meet, shows it paused and creates nothing. Loading page read with null/missing/unreadable textId shows the text chooser as its effective view without a save. **Choose another prop / Choose a little scene** explicitly save page choose, retaining selectedId/textId and every draft; the chooser keeps both kinds of choice open. The original word root remains absent when using these controls before an explicit first card/text choice.

| Explicit action | Selected word / text / page | Draft effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First card choice | Copy the collection, select that word, textId null, meet. | Create only that word's default draft. |
| First text choice | Copy the collection, selectedId null, select that text, read. | No word draft is created. Hide label/page-next controls that require a word; every card remains selectable. |
| Another card or gallery revisit | Select it, meet; retain the previous textId. | Restore that word's draft or create its default; other drafts stay exact. |
| Meet → Make / Back to Meet | Same target/text, build/meet. | Preserve all draft fields. |
| Read together from a card | Same target, select its linked readable text, read. | Preserve every word draft. If the linked text is unreadable/missing, show the open text chooser and an explanation; do not invent or replace the saved text. |
| Direct text choice | Same target (possibly null), selected text, read. | Preserve every draft. |
| Back from reading | Same target/text, build if a readable target/draft exists; otherwise save page choose. | No draft changes or automatic repair. |

An optional **Show a different example** demonstrates another retained word without changing the target or any draft. Prefer fin, or mat when the target is fin. If the preferred example is absent or unreadable, choose the first other readable retained word in retained source key order; if none exists, explain that this earlier collection has no separate example and leave practice open. Four manual frames show the example's meaning/whole word, then placement of its first, middle and last letters while an adult supplies the sounds. **Previous example part / Next example part / Back to my label** operate only on the local example view. Returning restores the exact page, selected place, model, pointing and tiles. No speech, auto-advance or help/reading score is produced.

Tile input: select one of the three places, then a native button from the retained `source.alphabet`. The initial authored collection uses `a b c f h i m n o p s t`; reopening never substitutes that current bank for an earlier alphabet. A tile replaces that place without consuming stock. **Clear this place** and **Clear label** work immediately. Selection, buttons and filled places have explicit accessible names. The word model can be shown/hidden without changing the draft. No drag, hover, long press or physical keyboard is required.

**Try this label** handles four cases without erasing the target or tiles:

- Incomplete: name the first empty place, preflight/commit its place index, then focus it. On refusal, preserve the old place and tiles and focus the explanation.
- Matches the chosen source word: offer **Put the label with this picture**. The gallery records an encountered label only on this explicit action.
- Matches another readable word in the frozen collection: name/show that prop and offer **Keep this other label** or **Back to my prop**. Keeping it does not replace the waiting target or tiles. This is a discovery, not a wrong reading result.
- Other combination: display the exact escaped letters and the neutral no-open-picture message. Offer **Change a letter** and **Back to my prop**; do not pronounce, grade or claim dictionary invalidity for the combination.

Match order is deterministic: chosen readable target first, then the first other readable word in retained source key order with the same label. Duplicate labels do not merge ids or count twice for another target. If there is no readable match but an opaque word entry has an own three-letter label equal to the assembled letters, use the unavailable-card notice instead of claiming it is outside the collection. Otherwise use the neutral other-combination copy; it makes no dictionary or moral judgment and provides no pronunciation. The general Story Loft invitation remains open, rather than prompting a new prop story for every arbitrary three-letter combination.

Tile edits, clearing, target selection, page changes, example/contrast opening and collection replacement clear the pending Try result and its Put/Keep actions. A result owns the exact notebook/owner/route/view, retained source object, target id and three-slot value it checked. Put/Keep rechecks that tuple before acting. Editing sat to bat after Try cannot keep either through an obsolete action, even if the old control is still connected. A stale action makes no write and shows no success. Keeping another label adds only its source id to the gallery; it does not allocate or overwrite that word's draft or replace the waiting target.

Shared texts preserve the exact brief55 strings and token classifications. Mark targets with both weight/underline and a text key; adult-supplied words remain ordinary readable text with an adjacent adult-read explanation. Do not hide or guess them from the current letter inventory. Each text has its projected open meaning question from the finite copy, with no answer choices or score. Its prop scene supports meaning, not an independent-reading claim. Story Loft is an open invitation to invent another scene; no imported text or learner work is written there automatically.

Win condition: every word id in the retained source collection is readable and appears in the gallery. The initial collection has twelve; do not hardcode twelve into saved-collection completion. An unreadable entry pauses the collection milestone, with an explanation; do not silently exclude it or count gallery length alone. All other readable cards and texts remain available. When complete, say the prop box has its labels, offer the retained readable miniatures and **Make another scene**, and keep every card open. This is a finite story contribution with unlimited revisits, not a reading certificate, act ending or commissioned film. Do not mutate the town's story services or Keeper's Pages.

## 4. Saved material and preservation

Use a new optional `wordRoom` root. Rendering an unopened chooser makes no write. The first explicit card/text choice copies the authored collection into a versioned source and preflights the actual Notes report before installing it. State version1 contains:

- `source`: `{id, revision, alphabet, words, texts}`; words/texts are objects keyed by source-local stable ids, not arrays. Alphabet is a unique array of lowercase single ASCII letters. A word owns `{id,label,letters,textId,meaning,alt,art,guidance}`. A text owns `{id,title,text,tokens,prompt}`. The id/revision pair is read only to offer an explicit current-collection replacement; it does not certify a linguistic review.
- `selectedId`: source-local word id or null; `page`: choose/meet/build/read; `textId`: source-local text id or null. Nullable ids support first entering through a text. No automatic selection is saved on render.
- `drafts`: object keyed by word id, with `{slots,place,modelShown,point}` as specified above. Drafts are created only on explicit word choice. An unreadable draft pauses that card, not its siblings.
- `gallery`: word ids explicitly placed or kept. New actions append a readable id only when its readable set does not already contain it. On load, preserve duplicates, non-codes and unknown values exactly; ignore them when deriving the unique readable set. No single bad gallery entry makes the root unreadable. The global transfer limits bound the array; do not add a current-content count requirement. No correct/incorrect counters or oral observations. Examples and pending Try results are local presentation state and are never installed by load/import.

Version1 validation contract:

- Root: non-array object, version1, object source/drafts, legal page, nullable coded ids, array gallery, whose entries are read individually as described below. Source id is a code, revision a positive safe integer; alphabet has1–26 distinct lowercase ASCII letters; words has1–128 own entries and texts0–32. Codes match `[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,79}`. Other JSON-safe fields remain untouched. The existing transfer layer remains responsible for global JSON depth/node/unsafe-key/byte limits. Failures in these structural root fields make the root unreadable and retained, not fresh.
- Word entry: id matches its own key; label is three lowercase ASCII letters and equals its own three single-letter `letters`; those letters belong to its retained alphabet. Art is a code; textId is a code or null; meaning and alt are each1–500 and guidance1–1500 characters. No sound-array field is required or read. Extra prior sound metadata, if present, is left opaque and cannot make the card unreadable. Known art uses the retained alt; unknown art uses the generic picture-unavailable alternative and shows the saved meaning as text, rather than claiming to draw the unavailable subject. Invalid word values stay raw and pause only that word.
- Text entry: id matches its key; title1–160, text1–4000 and prompt1–500 characters; tokens array1–512. Each token has exact text, integer start/end in the retained text's UTF-16 code units with `0 <= start < end <= text.length`, and role target/adult-supplied. Require sorted nonoverlapping spans and exact text slices. Roles are retained source classifications, not a fresh linguistic judgment: the reading view underlines them without needing a word-card lookup. Do not impose a word-membership check that this view does not consume, or infer/rewrite roles from today's letters or catalogue. Invalid spans/roles pause only that text, preserving its raw value. Spaces and punctuation outside tokens remain unchanged.
- Optional text art/alt fields select the reviewed, versioned scene drawing. The finite copy names the initial three. Without a known code and a nonempty alternative of at most500 characters, fall back to that source's readable word-card pictures; do not pause or replace the text, infer its picture from today's wording, or claim to draw an unknown scene. These fields are not a linguistic validation gate.
- Draft entry: three null-or-retained-alphabet letters, integer place0–2, boolean modelShown, point null or integer0–3. This is material, not an asserted correct result. A bad draft or unreadable selected word shows that card's paused notice and the open chooser without resetting it.
- References: selectedId, textId, draft keys, gallery ids and word→text links may reference absent/unreadable entries. Retain them; do not delete, rebind or add them on load. A missing reference is locally unavailable, cannot be used for a new Put/Keep, and prevents collection completion when it corresponds to an unreadable required word. Unknown extra gallery ids do not satisfy another word or increase a completion count. Duplicated entry ids under different keys fail the affected entries' id/key checks; no current catalogue comparison is involved.

Keep outcomes/status wording as runtime codes, not persisted UI sentences. The displayed draft, target, collection and gallery travel through the existing Notes import/export and separate explorer storage. Reopening uses its saved source, not today's label/meaning/key. A source-copy edit cannot regrade or relabel earlier material. Validate shape, stable codes, bounded values and self-consistency only. Do not require exactly today's twelve words, three texts or alphabet inventory. Current content is not a load-time answer key.

Read word/text entries independently: an unreadable or unknown entry stays raw and pauses only that card/text; other readable entries and all other destinations stay open. Unknown root versions stay raw with an explanation and Notes download link. Any offered replacement is explicit, cancelable, preflighted, and retains the raw earlier value using the existing recovery convention; no automatic repair or migration on render/import. Do not mutate the old `words`, parcel labels, passages, exposures or story roots.

**One explicit replacement path is mandatory.** In the For grown-ups panel and the unreadable-root view, offer **Start a fresh prop box**. If a readable saved source has the same authored id and a lower revision, relabel that same offer **Open the newer prop box**, rather than adding a second offer; for different ids or a higher/unknown revision do not call this version newer. The confirmation explains that the current box's drafts, source and gallery will be kept in the Notes download while the active box starts fresh. **Cancel** changes nothing; **Open a fresh prop box** confirms.
