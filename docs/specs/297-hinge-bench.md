## Controls, state and exact mathematics

Per-case material `{caseId,opening,goldLength,blueLength,tilt}`. Opening integer multiple of 5 in [0,360]; lengths integers 1..3; tilt integer multiple of 15 in [0,345]. Native **Turn more +5° / Turn back −5°**, separate gold/blue Shorter/Longer, **Turn more +90° / Turn back −90°** for quarter-turn steps, **Turn whole picture** (+15° modulo 360), **Reset this hinge**, **Show quarter-turn card**, **Compare turns**, **Show an example**. Endpoint length/opening controls disabled when unavailable; no wrap between opening 0 and 360. A quarter-step is available only if the whole 90° change remains in bounds; refuse rather than silently clamp it. Four +90° taps can construct a full turn without requiring 72 small taps. Tilt wrap is orientation only and never changes the selected sweep. Reset restores that case’s authored working state and hides comparison/card. Material edits hide comparison; the card may stay visible. Each case’s material/card/comparison is retained within the visit when switching cases; example pages never alter it.

Angle measure is exactly `opening`, not an inverse-cosine reconstruction or shortest-angle normalization. Categories: zero at 0; acute strictly0<θ<90; right exactly 90; obtuse90<θ<180; straight exactly 180; reflex180<θ<360; full turn exactly 360. Reference versus working relation uses direct degree equality/order. Equal endpoint direction alone never establishes equal selected turn;0 and 360 differ. Length/tilt changes preserve opening and category. Show the one-degree definition and full/quarter-turn benchmarks beside the numerical measures, not as an assumed prior lesson. Names and interpreted relation appear on explicit Compare; measured numerical settings remain available throughout to all users. No claim of independent naming, visual estimation or recall.

Use a common pin and fixed-radius sweep for the two rays of each hinge. With upward-positive mathematical coordinates, direction at α maps to `(cx+r*cos α, cy-r*sin α)`. Gold direction is tilt; blue is tilt+opening. Ray extensions follow the same directions; changing visible length does not shift the vertex or rotate a ray. Use exact cardinal handling or tolerances when rendering, without reclassifying values by rounded pixel positions. Zero has no arc and an explicit **No turn ·0°** label. Full has a full-circle path composed of two 180° arcs, an arrow and **One full turn ·360°**. Intermediate sweep follows the marked counterclockwise path; reflex uses the large arc, not its complement. Neither 0 nor360 may disappear into the same empty SVG path. Keep a common fixed arc radius independent of arm length; marks show amount of turn, not linear arc length or area. Arm lengths never determine the arc radius. For coincident rays at0° or360°, draw the gold arm wider under a narrower blue centre stroke, with distinct line style and text saying that gold and blue point along the same ray. Never shift either ray sideways to make it visible. Both arms must remain visually identifiable even at equal visible lengths. Test common rays both with equal lengths and with one longer than the other.

**Compare** keeps the reference visible and overlays a dashed reference at the working vertex, with its gold starting direction aligned to the working gold direction. Rotate reference as a rigid figure; preserve its lengths and opening. Use the same local sweep radius and distinguish line styles/labels without color alone; equal arcs may share positions, but both ray styles remain inspectable. Show exact reference/working degree values, names and greater/same/less turn, plus the relevant case explanation. No fit score or completion state. The quarter-turn card is a 90° reference aligned to the working gold arm, rotates with the whole working picture and is available before any type name. Only an actual selected 90° angle gets a right-angle mark; card and selected sweep are separately labelled.
