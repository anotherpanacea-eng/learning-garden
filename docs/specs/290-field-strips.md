## Problem and boundary

Build an openly selectable, touch-first material board at `#studio/field-strips`. A child shades a fractional extent of one invented field, then takes a fraction of that extent. The original whole remains visible. The new operation is constructing a continuous nested area and interpreting its measure against the original whole. It is not the first fraction multiplication in the app: probability paths already multiply fractions. Nor is it a replacement for Sharing Counter, Fraction Trail, Lantern Line, Ribbon Recut/Matches, Join the Portions, Whole Batches, Mixing Table or Equal Blocks.

Study draft PR158/proposal198's Field Strips, but do not merge the parent pack. Six finite cases, three optional drawn helpers, local draft/first/latest comparisons, Notes/transfer and discovery are in scope. Keep open exploration; no score, timer, gate, loss, mastery label or automatic placement. Original wording can be explained to a grown-up; no text field, microphone, speech assessment or private-data transfer is added. Historical anchors, mixed/improper multipliers, general fraction division, recipes, percent, a free partition editor and Act III adapters remain separate commissions.

## Teaching encounter

| Stage | Visible operation and claim |
|---|---|
| Teach | The frame is one original whole. Supplied equal vertical cuts make the first strips; supplied equal horizontal cuts make rows. The child chooses how many first strips, then how many rows of that first amount to take. Equal cuts are provided, not independently constructed. |
| Guided | Half of three quarters has a visible three-step explanation. Add three first strips, take one of two equal rows of that amount, then count three selected cells among eight in the original whole. The answer is supplied. |
| Contrast | The green first amount and hatched taken amount use different outlines/patterns. `1/2 of 3/4` is not `1/2 of the original field`, nor the whole first amount. Equal raw products alone do not show that the requested first and second portions were constructed. |
| Supported practice | Two thirds of two thirds; two thirds of three quarters. Optional clue, worked helpers and reported working together stay separate from comparisons. Six twelfths is accepted/displayed as six twelfths, with one half as an equivalent name, not a second test. |
| Changed use | Three quarters of one half returns the same final three eighths via a different first amount. Taking all of a first amount retains it. Taking part of an empty first amount remains empty: multiplication by a proper fraction does not strictly shrink zero. |
| Return | Leave/revisit the editable material, compare a revised plan with its retained checked plan, or open the existing Recut/Sharing tools. No new cross-game learner inference or story completion follows. |

The oral invitation about predicting size is optional and unobserved. It cannot be recorded as an answered prediction. This area representation alone does not establish general symbolic fluency or transfer to other quantities.

## Exact authored cases

Each original whole is a unit rectangle of equal area, with `columns × rows` equal cells. The table gives the requested first columns and taken rows. Original initial plans are `{columnsTaken:0,rowsTaken:0}`; a retained source can validly supply another initial plan, which must be used and labelled literally rather than reset to zero.

| id / title | columns / firstTaken | rows / secondTaken | Final cells of original whole | Teaching |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `half-three-quarters` / Half of three quarters | 4 / 3 | 2 / 1 | 3/8 | Guided original example |
| `two-thirds-twice` / A part of two thirds | 3 / 2 | 3 / 2 | 4/9 | Practice; denominator changes |
| `half-a-field` / Another way to a half | 4 / 3 | 3 / 2 | 6/12 = 1/2 | Equal amount, different name |
| `three-quarters-half` / The same amount, another way | 2 / 1 | 4 / 3 | 3/8 | Different first referent, same product |
| `keep-it-all` / Take all of this part | 3 / 2 | 3 / 3 | 6/9 = 2/3 | Multiplier one preserves first amount |
| `empty-first` / An empty first amount | 4 / 0 | 2 / 1 | 0/8 = 0 | Zero exception |

All six remain selectable. The fourth/zero/one observations must derive from actual source quantities and not catalogue IDs if imported quantities differ. A supplied initial plan fits the full request only when both columnsTaken===firstTaken and rowsTaken===secondTaken. Product match alone does not suffice: empty-first’s original (0,0) is not a fitting initial plan. A fitting supplied plan is labelled as supplied, never as learner success. Notes intentionally retains the source initial beside snapshots rather than adding a supplied-versus-edited achievement flag. Other visits/cases and helper opening remain accessible during partial unreadability according to the rules below.

## Mathematics and material

For a case with `b=columns`, `d=rows`, and actual plan `x=columnsTaken`, `y=rowsTaken`, the first amount is `x/b` of the original field. The second fraction is `y/d` **of that first amount**. The hatched amount is `x*y/(b*d)` of the original field. Counts and denominators remain integers. Compute reduced display with an integer gcd; `0/q` reduces to `0`, and `q/q` to `1`. Preserve the unsimplified cell fraction alongside its equivalent name. No learner-produced label is graded.

Draw one fixed outer frame labelled **Original whole field**. Vertical selected columns grow left-to-right. Within that region, taken rows grow top-to-bottom and receive a dark hatch; the remaining first amount has a lighter vertical pattern. Unselected original cells stay unfilled. The horizontal cut lines extend across the original whole so every cell has the same area and the denominator counts every cell, not only the green region. The first-region boundary remains visible after the second layer; never recolor the whole first region as though all of it were taken. Zero still has a full empty frame and readable counts; never divide by the number of selected first cells.

Use actual `x/b` and `y/d` in current readouts, not requested values. The prompt retains the fixed request. First/reference/taken/current/checked labels are not interchangeable. `y/d` is a specified fraction even if `x=0`; do not report a measured ratio obtained by dividing zero by zero. A final amount can match while one or both requested construction steps differ; report each separately.

The board may animate only the changed area briefly, with reduced motion removing animation. No shape, color or audio is the sole carrier of meaning. Supplied grids are ideal mathematical models, not measurements of land or evidence about plants. No metres, harvest yield, growth or food-inventory reward is inferred.
