Use a fixed (n+1)-by-(n+1) coordinate footprint. Old tile (r,c), 0≤r,c<n, never changes identity/location/style when a patch is removed or added. The **bottom row patch** occupies (n,c), c<n; the **right column patch** occupies (r,n), r<n; the **corner** alone occupies (n,n). Thus the two n-tile patches explicitly **exclude** the corner. They never overlap or double-count it. This is an L-shaped addition along two sides, not a four-sided border. Missing positions are dashed outlines with no filled-tile hatch, and the missing corner has its own outline/label. Inactive patches do not count as tiles.

Response booleans row/column/corner give eight freely reachable states, including corner alone. `addedNow=n*row+n*column+corner`; `totalNow=n*n+addedNow`; `complete=row&&column&&corner`. The finished goal always requires `2*n+1` new tiles and has `(n+1)*(n+1)` tiles, regardless of current incomplete arrangement. These are integer counts, not pixel-tolerance results. No automatic fill, correction or movement of old tiles.
