History consists solely of action codes `open|whole|piece`, replayed from initial packed floor(N/d) bundles and N%d loose pieces, sent count0. Initial bundlej contains IDsjd..(j+1)d−1; loose IDs follow all bundle IDs. Each ID belongs to exactly one rack bundle, loose rack list or sent list.

**Open one whole** pops the last rack bundle and prepends its d IDs to the loose list. N,d and sent stay fixed. **Send one whole** pops the last rack bundle and appends its d IDs to sent; only if order still needs at leastd. **Send one piece** shifts the first loose ID into sent; only while sent<T and loose exists. These operations never cut, resize, discard or invent a piece. No auto-opening or auto-sending. All three disabled once sent=T, including zero-order initial state. A no-op/invalid action returns null without mutation. **Undo last move** removes one history action and reconstructs the exact preceding material/ID layout; enabled iff history nonempty, even after order filled. No arbitrary limit: forward replay histories are acyclic because an opening reduces packed and a send reduces rack count; history length≤N+floor(N/d). Reject malformed work/history/unknown codes or an impossible replay, no coercion/clamping. No public loader or persisted history.
