## One sitting

Bramble lends a small wooden box: six numbered shutters (default) or nine. All begin open. Press Roll: use two independent uniform six-sided dice while the **sum of the open numbers is greater than six**, otherwise one die. Display actual faces as pips with numerical text. Tap any open shutters; the visible running sum and target stay together. Selected shutters can be untapped and cleared. Confirm closing only a nonempty subset summing exactly to the roll. No wrong-answer penalty: an unmatched draft remains editable and nothing closes. After closing, roll again manually. All shut means a completed box; if no nonempty open subset can make the roll, the round ends with the remaining shutters visible. Neither outcome measures ability; chance can stop a thoughtful choice. No accumulated scores, timers, automatic rolls or required sound/drag.

Repeat play retains box-size choice and archives the waiting round unchanged, with no forced finish. Leave/reload/explorer switching resumes the same roll and selected draft, never rerolling. A small six-shutter box changes the scope of the addition choices, not a child's level. Available legal combinations are not shown automatically. Optional **Show ways to make this roll** explicitly records supplied help in that round and lists its legal combinations in stable ascending order. Optional **Bramble explains** shows four pictured thoughts, one Next at a time: the target; several ways to make seven in the full box; closed numbers cannot be reused; dice/chance and the one-die rule. It records supplied explanation separately from independent work. The explanation never changes the waiting round; no saved practice result or mastery claim. Numerical examples are original and independently checked.
