# 83 · Preserve a French Paris visit's own pages

**Status: specification and internal implementation reviews complete; draft release candidate.** This is a preservation repair, not a new French lesson, proficiency measure or release authorization.

## Problem and scope

English Paris now captures a whole visit's authored source. French Paris still reads current catalog nodes and helpers while loading old visit IDs, validates exploration routes against today's story pair, and cleans observations using today's passage and key. A wording edit, changed helper or retired story can therefore relabel a response or remove a waiting visit. Apply the reviewed [English preservation contract](73-paris-saved-visit-source.md) to both French entry families: the three original errands and all nine exploration stories at three lengths. Keep their French text, bilingual support, optional tools, open destinations and existing practice-suggestion purpose.

Do not author new French prose, infer age from text length, translate an English assessment label into a French level, or change the available lessons. The repair does not promote supplied answers, rereading, listening or adult-reported reading into independent evidence.

## Required behavior

**New start.** Before replacing the current visit, capture all three route nodes, passage sentences, question titles/prompts/choices/answer values, ordering items and display permutation, explanation/meaning/word-help text, reading-focus labels, all smaller examples and their models/keys, entrance title/chapter and ending/project copy. The copy belongs to that visit. Keep the original entrance ID and exploration metadata/reason. Original errands have no exploration metadata; do not manufacture it. Each new copy must fit within the existing 24 KiB UTF-8 JSON cap and pass structural validation. If it cannot, show a focused notice on the relevant French landing/chooser and leave the waiting work unchanged. All 30 authored routes must fit before release.

**Resume and notes.** Resolve the current node, next page, passage, helper, ending, titles, speech text and observations from the saved source. A catalog change to wording, key, item order, helper ID, route membership or availability cannot change that copy or its already stored outcome. Check a source's shape, IDs, finite counts and internal linkage; never require equality with today's catalog. Preserve unknown harmless fields during ordinary load/export. Validation must cover French language tags and every field read by the UI/helper. A French source cannot be accepted as an English one or vice versa. Prefer a small extension of the existing source adapter and shared validation where the behavior is the same; retain old English exports unchanged. Exact field/helper names are implementation choices, not a reason for a second archive or generalized migration system.

**Legacy and unreadable work.** A pre-repair French visit without its own source is legacy even when today's IDs and counts happen to match. Never attach today's passage or labels to its old choice positions. Retain its drafts, node/finished position, observations and support data exactly as received. Missing, malformed and future-version sources recover at the affected visit only. Keep the rest of the garden usable. Use a short recovery turn explaining that earlier work remains saved, with 44px controls to **Start a fresh French visit**, **Back to French Paris**, and **Grown-up notes**. Offer the same-story fresh-start button only when the saved entrance/story/destination/length still has a current matching route. If it was retired or re-paired, offer **Choose another French story** linking to the French chooser, where the learner explicitly selects the new story. Do not silently substitute a different story. A fresh start adds a visit and never overwrites the old one. Notes may show safe stored labels or position-only responses with an unavailable-wording explanation, but cannot borrow current helper/title/answer text. The ordinary progress download retains raw unreadable data; no new wrapping archive or schema root is required.

**Suggestions and familiarity.** Only a structurally readable captured French visit can supply a new suggestion's checked-answer comparison. Use its own frozen key, not today's key. Keep the existing support/retry/familiar-story exclusions and reported reading distinctions. Legacy, malformed or source-less responses cannot manufacture promotion, a penalty, or a current answer. Encountering a retained earlier story remains familiarity even if its authored text or node IDs change. Keep its stored seen IDs where they can be tied to the retained visit/source, or use the retained visit’s displayed/recorded metadata to preserve that encounter independently of current catalog membership. A legacy story returning with new node IDs or a different route remains familiar; no source-less response becomes independent new-story evidence. Rendering notes/recovery cannot create an encounter or alter records. Do not redefine the suggestion algorithm or relabel historical flags on load.

**Storage and compatibility.** Use existing explorer storage, export/import, saving-off and reset. Never evict visits to fit the new copy. Keep visible quota-failure warnings, in-memory work and download access. Confirm the actual import loader retains captured and legacy French visits and observations. English Paris's existing source behavior, other story entrances and their helpers must continue unchanged. No atlas mapping, graph pin or attainment count changes.
