## Exact free geometry and interpretation

Use a flat Euclidean paper model. Both rails and the transversal represent straight infinite lines; the drawn lengths are only portions. Parallel means same direction in the same plane and no intersection even when extended. Nonparallel lines can meet offscreen: absence of a visible meeting is not evidence of parallelism. No physical straightedge/protractor qualification or inference about a real structure.

Let θ be the chosen angle, δ the second-line turn, r the picture rotation, D the selected drawing spacing, v=(cosθ,sinθ) in mathematical coordinates. Before global rotation, O=−Dv/2 and P=+Dv/2. First rail passes through O with orientation0; second through P with orientationδ. Transversal follows v. Rotate all points and directions by r, then invert y for screen coordinates. The angle at P between its positive rail ray and positive transversal ray is θ−δ, always5..175 for these controls. No parallel/transversal degeneracy is possible. Opposite pairs at each individual crossing remain equal even when the rails lean.

At O, sectors in mathematical counterclockwise order begin at0,θ,180,180+θ and are a,b,c,d with measuresθ,180−θ,θ,180−θ. At P they begin atδ,θ,180+δ,180+θ and are e,f,g,h with measuresθ−δ,180−θ+δ,θ−δ,180−θ+δ. These names rotate with the picture; they do not mean top-of-screen positions. O is the first crossing and P the second along the positive transversal direction, regardless of their screen heights.

The selected pairs are a/e corresponding, a/g alternate interior, a/h co-interior. Because O is below P in the unrotated mathematical frame, a lies inside the rails; e is outside, g/h inside. Corresponding means the same relative position at each crossing, not an identical page direction. Alternate interior means between the rails, opposite sides of the transversal. Co-interior means between the rails on the same side. In parallel mode, a=e, a=g, a+h=180°. For the mathematical model only, with lean the first two signed differences equalδ and the last sum is180+δ. Do not display a signed difference as an interior angle; the exact learner templates below use inequality instead. Do not generalize this to “no angles are equal when lines lean”: opposite pairs at each crossing still match. Do not use F/Z mnemonics.

Anchors (θ,δ; a,e,g,h): (60,0;60,60,60,120); (60,+10;60,50,50,130); (60,−10;60,70,70,110); (90,0;90,90,90,90); (15,+10;15,5,5,175); (165,−10;165,175,175,5). All values are exact chosen model angles, not screen measurements. Preserve degree symbols and Unicode minus; no negative interior angles.
