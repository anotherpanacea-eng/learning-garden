## Story order and review rule

The six acts are **recovery → community making → wider exchange → evidence and disagreement → learner-originated projects → Opening of the Ways**. Act II’s market, garden, gathering and show make the town a place the learner helps shape; later acts return to those tools for reciprocal exchange, qualified claims and a chosen contribution. This is a story and teaching design, **not six age grades, access locks or a claim that later acts are taught**. A learner may enter any available destination with support and revisit an earlier representation. A celebration or film can close a story beat while intermediate instruction remains unfinished.

Select a small concept, model, contrast, practice, new use and return for each increment. Source topics and prerequisite edges guide authoring, not child placement. Preserve separate records for support, familiarity and independent attempts; parent talk and physical work stay honest about what was actually observed. Review each substantive spec independently, then its authored content, implementation and real browser behavior before release. Keep source, rights, privacy and save-history boundaries in the linked specs and [project instructions](../AGENTS.md).
